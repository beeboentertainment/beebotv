Get Beebo on your phone

Beebo works on just about any screen — an iPhone, an Android phone, a tablet, or a laptop. You don’t hunt through an app store: you open Beebo in your web browser and, with one tap, add it to your Home Screen so it gets a real Beebo icon.

The quick version: open your family’s Beebo address in a web browser, sign in, then tap Install app or Add to Home Screen. That’s it — Beebo now lives on your phone like any other app. Everything below is just the same thing, spelled out for each kind of device.

First, the address you’ll open

🏠 On the home Wi-Fi

If your phone is on the same Wi-Fi as the computer running Beebo, use the short local address shown on the owner’s Get Started screen — a few numbers like 192.168.1.50:47811. Easiest of all: the same screen shows a QR code you can point your phone’s camera at to open it instantly.

🌍 Away from home

Out and about — on mobile data, at a friend’s place, anywhere but home — open your family’s permanent web address instead: yourname.beebo.tv (the owner sees the exact one on their Get Started screen). It works from anywhere in the world, and it never changes.

What’s the difference? “Home Wi-Fi” is the fast local shortcut for when you’re in the house. “Away from home” is your .beebo.tv web address for everywhere else. Good news: you only install Beebo once. Install it using whichever address you have handy, and the app remembers how to reach your library both at home and away.

Install on iPhone or iPad

iPhones and iPads install Beebo straight from Safari — no App Store needed.

  1. Open Beebo in Safari

    Type your family’s address (like yourname.beebo.tv) into Safari, or scan the QR code from the owner’s screen. Use Safari specifically — the Add to Home Screen button lives there.

  2. Sign in

    Enter the username and login code (or password) the owner gave you. Your library appears.

  3. Tap the Share button

    It’s the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom of the screen (or top on an iPad).

  4. Tap “Add to Home Screen”

    Scroll the share menu until you see it, tap it, then tap Add. A Beebo icon lands on your Home Screen — open it any time like a normal app.

Install on Android

On Android you’ve got two easy options. The browser install works everywhere; the Android app adds a few extras.

Option A · easiest

Install from the browser

  1. Open your Beebo address in Chrome and sign in.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right).
  3. Tap Install app (sometimes “Add to Home screen”).
  4. Confirm — a Beebo icon appears in your app drawer.
Option B · extra features

Install the Android app

  1. Open the download page and tap Download for Android.
  2. Your phone may ask permission to install apps — allow it.
  3. Tap the downloaded .apk file to install.
  4. Open Beebo, enter your server address (or scan the QR), and sign in.

Which Android option should I pick? The browser install is perfect for most people. Choose the downloaded Android app if you want the extras built for the road — Watch Together (every passenger screen in sync), picture-in-picture, and saving movies for offline viewing.

Install on a laptop or desktop

💻 One click in Chrome or Edge

Open your Beebo address in Chrome or Edge and look for a small install icon in the address bar (a little screen with a down-arrow), or open the ⋮ menu and choose Install Beebo Entertainment. It opens in its own window, just like a desktop app.

🎉 You’re done. However you installed it, Beebo now opens straight to your library. Sign in once and it remembers you. Off Wi-Fi, it uses your .beebo.tv address automatically — so movie night follows you out the door.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

I don’t see an “Install” or “Add to Home Screen” option

Make sure you’ve fully opened the Beebo page (not just a search result), and that you’re using Safari on iPhone/iPad or Chrome/Edge on Android and computers. Some other browsers hide this feature. Refresh the page and check the browser’s share or ⋮ menu again.

Is there a Beebo app in the App Store or Google Play?

Beebo installs from your browser (this is called a “PWA”), so you don’t need the App Store. On Android there’s also an optional downloadable app for extras like Watch Together and offline downloads. Beebo is on its way to the Google Play Store too — see “Become a tester” on the home page.

Do I need to install it on every phone?

Yes — install Beebo once on each phone or tablet that will watch. Every person signs in with their own account, so everyone gets their own Continue Watching.

It worked at home but not when I left the house

At home you may have used the local 192.168… address, which only works on the home Wi-Fi. Away from home, open your yourname.beebo.tv address instead. See Watch away from home for the full details.

Android says “app from an unknown source” or blocks the install

That’s normal for apps you download outside the Play Store. When your phone asks, allow installs from your browser, then tap the downloaded file again. If you’d rather skip that, just use the browser install (Option A) instead.

Can I remove it later?

Absolutely — press and hold the Beebo icon and choose Remove/Uninstall, exactly like any other app. Your account and library are untouched; you can reinstall any time.
