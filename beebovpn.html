BeeboVPN for Windows

A private VPN run by Beebo Entertainment for invited friends and family. It’s invite only: you’ll need an invite link from the person who sent you here.

⬇ Download BeeboVPN 0.1.0 (Windows, 71 MB)

Test release, September 17, 2026. Windows 10 or 11.

Set it up

  1. Run the installer

    Windows may say it “protected your PC” because this test version isn’t signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. BeeboVPN also installs WireGuard, the well-known VPN engine it runs on, if you don’t already have it.

  2. Enter your invite

    Open BeeboVPN, choose Enter your invite code and paste the whole invite link you were sent. Each invite works once, for one device, and expires after 7 days.

  3. Press Connect

    That’s it. Press Disconnect any time to go back to your normal internet.

Good to know

  • While connected, websites see the VPN server’s address in Beauharnois, Canada (OVHcloud), not your home.
  • Your device makes its own secret key. It never leaves your computer.
  • BeeboVPN keeps no logs of what you visit. The owner can see each device’s name, when it last connected and how much data it used, and can remove a device at any time.
  • The optional kill switch stops your internet if the VPN drops, so nothing slips out unprotected.
  • OVHcloud’s usage rules apply to everything sent through the VPN.
