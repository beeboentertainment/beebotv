TEST RECORD · OCT 13, 2026, 4:14 AM EASTERN

Progress, real-device testing, and what is still unknown.

Current downloads: Windows 0.1.96 (published Oct 19, 2026, 3:06 PM Eastern) and Android 1.51-tester31 (build 81, published Oct 19, 2026, 2:27 PM Eastern).

Device checks: NOT yet tested on these exact new builds. Checks made after publishing will be recorded in the release evidence. Earlier tester31 and 0.1.96 device results remain historical; no feature percentage changes. Beebo Auto 1.18 is unchanged. Read the release notes and limits.

This is the full, dated picture behind our status pages. It separates checked code from a person using a real device. It does not mean every path works, and it does not turn an untested path into a pass.

How to read the percentages. “Code checklist” is the share of the listed feature rows where both Built and Automated tests are checked. “Human-device” is the share where the separate Real device check is recorded. These are checklist measures, not a claim of bug-free software. Areas overlap when a feature belongs to both a platform and a named slice. If we have no comparable checklist rows, the page says not measured yet.

REAL PEOPLE, REAL DEVICES

What was hand tested.

Oct 12 · Two homes and a phone on 5G

Passed: direct and Relay playback, network switching, two direct viewers, library sharing from invite through stopping access, phone backup, browse, favourites, playlists, radio, podcasts, Campsite host/guest setup, landscape and restart.

Not tested: two people on Relay, a friend's Chromecast, Beebo Auto in a car, a real TV pairing code, TalkBack and the newest fixes on real devices.

Oct 12 · Windows desktop walk

Passed: about 35 screens were opened and looked right on the unpublished 0.1.88 test build, including media areas, admin screens and all eight Settings pages.

Found: settings search missed the intro/skip section, and the window did not stay maximized when brought forward. Not tested: sign-out, signed-in browser player, subtitles, track switching, PiP, keyboard-only use, high contrast and 150% scaling.

Oct 12 · Signed-in browser

Passed: sign-in and reload, 17 pages, clip and film playback, seeking, pause/resume and switching quality.

Found: old browser pages chose the wrong automatic quality, lacked a reconnect action after idle, had a nearly blank Suggestions page and showed empty-image icons. Not tested: subtitles, audio tracks, Cast, AirPlay, PiP, fullscreen, touch layout, keyboard-only use and Firefox, Edge or Safari.

Oct 12 · Campsite

Passed: S9+ host and S24 browser guest join, guest library, music controls, 41 game rooms entered and started or correctly refused, a complete scripted-guest Drop Four game and guest data pages, on the same-Wi-Fi route.

Found: abandoned rooms did not clear, the host sheet button was under the navigation bar, and a stopped host needs a watched unattended run. Not tested: a phone hotspot, a guest on mobile data, a real two-phone board-game touch run, synced audio, karaoke, recordings, guest uploads and a screen-off host.

Oct 13 · Campsite Sea Battle, two people

Passed: Nick hosted while a friend on a Samsung S20 created Sea Battle; Nick joined, they played a full game to the end, the friend won, and it worked well. They used DIRECT-BeeboTV Wi-Fi (BeeboDirect), the Wi-Fi Beebo creates for Campsite without internet: the first real-person result on that route.

Scope: this is human testing for Sea Battle only. The other 40 Campsite games still have scripted-guest coverage only. Nick's phone, browsers, game length, sound and graphics observations were not recorded. A phone hotspot and a guest on mobile data are still not tested.

Earlier two-phone checks

Passed: S24 and S9+ Watch Together joins and sync, and Campsite host/guest checks on the documented routes. The October 11–12 phone walks reported no crashes for the paths they opened.

Not tested: Watch Together rejoin after app closure, every connection route, every game and every device size.

Nick's real-car Car Party check

Passed: a guest could follow along and send reactions in a real parked car.

Failed or missing: a passenger could not watch the playing movie, and a guest could not continue with a blank name. Both fixes are still in development, not in the source snapshot used for this page. This was not a driving-safety validation.
