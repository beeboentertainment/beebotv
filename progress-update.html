What we did. What worked. What still needs work.

Updated Oct 24, 2026, 3:40 AM Eastern.

The bugs we found.

  • Audiobooks: seeking to another chapter while paused can be lost after restarting. Earlier played checkpoints and bookmarks passed. The paused seek still needs a repair and a new installed-build check.
  • Trip Journal: a minimal 720p export still times out on the Galaxy S9+ without producing a video. A recap screen opening is not export success.
  • Watch Together: Start and Join can be clipped at ordinary display density. Earlier sync, chat, reactions and reconnect checks used a temporary density workaround. That does not close the layout bug.
  • Sidecar captions: the subtitle file can be selected, but the expected cue is absent from the picture on the tested build.
  • Warm pairing: opening a new pairing link while the app is already open can leave the old Home visible.
  • Online Next: the player can skip an earlier episode already saved on the phone.
  • Memory Match: the match announcement says “heartss”. The reviewed wording repair is included in this draft. It still needs a real rendered acceptance check; no public fix or issue closure is claimed yet. Functional completion passed despite the wording bug.

These open issues keep their rows capped. Other older issues, including Campsite mobile-data and an unmatched stop route, stay in the feature record until a matching check closes them.

Chromecast has one confirmed media-loading bug. Earlier connection, volume and stop checks passed. The later S9+ native check used the normal supported route and allowed 93 seconds of preparation. The sender said Casting, but four receiver polls still showed no media session. The isolated test certificate alone does not explain it. The internal cause is not localized. This is one open bug across several failed attempts, and the score is capped at 90%. TV captions, seek and working second-sender playback remain blocked.

The S24 joined a test Campsite over Wi-Fi with mobile data also on. That is a guest-admission pass. It is not a pass for mobile-data-only reachability. The local guest address was unreachable on cellular alone; that test-route limit does not prove a new product bug.
