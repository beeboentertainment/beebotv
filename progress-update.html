Next-release changes and what still needs testing · Oct 22, 2026, 6:47 AM Eastern

In development · next-release changes

Updated Oct 22, 2026, 6:47 AM Eastern. Windows 0.1.99 and Android 1.51-tester34 (build 84), published Oct 21, 2026, 3:46 PM Eastern; Beebo Auto 1.20 (build 21), published Oct 21, 2026, 6:52 AM Eastern. Device runs after publishing are recorded in the release evidence; most of the changes below have still not been tried by a person.

New in Windows 0.1.99 and Android 1.51-tester34 (published Oct 21, 2026, 3:46 PM Eastern; not yet tried on real devices): audiobooks keep your place when the player closes and books that showed "[object Object]" are read again, hidden files such as Mac "._" copies are skipped in every library scan, sidebar Movies and TV Shows counts update when a folder is added or removed, Live TV handles a failed parental check the same way everywhere, the converter can use libx264 when ffmpeg has no OpenH264, Next and Previous work on downloaded episodes (also offline), the screen-off switch moved into Quality & audio, genre names follow the app language, and more screens are translated. Beebo Auto is unchanged at 1.20. Full release notes.

Earlier the same day (Oct 21, 2026, 6:52 AM Eastern), Windows 0.1.98, Android 1.51-tester33 and Beebo Auto 1.20 added (In development where marked; not yet tried on real devices): Picture frame and Family achievements (both off until turned on), 12-hour guest passes with early renewal, Live TV confirmation of home-network playlist addresses, Movie Night and walkie-talkie fixes, and Beebo Auto sign-in storage fixes. Full release notes.

The changes below are in those downloads unless a card says otherwise. The Oct 22 device runs added no passed check; they found failures that are listed on the release evidence page. Recorded feature percentages and device limits remain separate.

Parental limits in Live TV and Movie Night · In development

In the Oct 21, 2026 release: a Live TV link stops when its profile is restricted, and a Movie Night room removes blocked titles when limits tighten. If a parental check itself fails, viewing continues; this is the owner’s choice.

What is left to reach 100%: Check restricted and normal profiles on a real PC after release, including changes during viewing.

Guest-safe Campsite video downloads · In development

In the Oct 21, 2026 release: guests get an H.264/AAC copy when the original audio will not play in a browser, and host approval no longer cuts a running download.

What is left to reach 100%: Play and save on real guest phones, hear the sound, seek, and approve another request during a running download.

Walkie-talkie from anywhere · In development

Off and in the Oct 21, 2026 release: Invite from anywhere always asks the host to approve each guest, Android has a host panel without a microphone, and each guest has message limits. Code is complete; it has not been tested on real phones. The server part is on; the Oct 21, 2026 release is the first app release that uses it.

What is left to reach 100%: Use a host and guests on unrelated networks; check approvals, message limits, disconnect and rejoin.

Android accessibility and layouts · In development

Another accessibility and layout pass is in Android 1.51-tester32 (Oct 21, 2026, 3:43 AM Eastern). Earlier phone tests cover the earlier screens and builds only.

What is left to reach 100%: Try TalkBack, 200% text, high contrast, landscape and large playback controls on real phones after release.

Beebo Auto hardening · In development

In the Oct 21, 2026 release: typing is blocked while projecting to the car, saved sign-in is kept in encrypted storage, and Car Party controls stay usable. Beebo Auto 1.20 has not been tested in a real car; an earlier build had one first check in one car on Oct 6, 2026.

What is left to reach 100%: Run the first real-car and head-unit tests, parked, including sound, controls, sign-in, maps and reconnect.

Windows first-run connection test · In development

A first-run connection test card is in Windows 0.1.97 (Get Started shows a background connection test). It is not a promise that every router or away-from-home route will connect.

What is left to reach 100%: Check a real PC and different routers, including failed and unavailable connections.

Podcast import · In development

Podcast import from an OPML list file or a public feed link is in the downloads (Windows 0.1.88 and later; fixes in 0.1.97). Existing podcast playback keeps its earlier status.

What is left to reach 100%: Try real files, links and names, duplicates, failed imports and playback after release.

Remote Desktop keyboard helper · In development

Off by default. The keyboard helper change is in Windows 0.1.97; Remote Desktop has never been run on a real PC.

What is left to reach 100%: Use a real PC and phone to check keyboard, mouse, viewing, approval, disconnect and revoke.

Online browser games · In development

The online browser-games page connection is merged, off and unreleased. The separate browser games already on this website keep their own status.

What is left to reach 100%: Play with real people on different networks; check reconnect and host controls.

BeeboVPN · In development

The owner connected one phone with the standard WireGuard app and it worked. A BeeboVPN Android app has not been started. BeeboVPN is not offered for public sale.

What is left to reach 100%: Verify supported devices and connection behaviour before calling the service Available.

Ideas, not promised: starting a film on your home TV from your phone while you are away (a design only; nothing is built), a fuller wishlist. TV volume from the phone while casting, media requests and kitchen announcements are now in the downloads (In development where marked). Casting to a TV near you while you are away from home is a different, existing Beta feature (Windows 0.1.85 and later).

Roku, Samsung, LG, Xbox and Apple TV apps still need real hardware. Beebo Auto 1.20 has not been tested in a real car (an earlier build had one first check in one car on Oct 6, 2026), walkie-talkie from anywhere still needs real phones, and Remote Desktop still needs a real PC.

What was hand tested.

Oct 22 · Four phones on Android tester34 (muted)

Passed: the five tabs on a Pixel 3, a Galaxy S24 Ultra, a Galaxy S25 and a Galaxy S9+ with no crash logged; playback with Original and 720p; Watch Together between a Galaxy S25 and a Galaxy S9+ (play, seek and pause followed within 1 to 3 seconds); Campsite join and a finished Tic-Tac-Toe game; cast controls to a real Chromecast TV; music, audiobook resume after a force-stop, Radio and podcast search; genre names in French, German and Spanish; photos for a normal member; a child profile that saw only G and PG films.

Failed: no way to choose embedded subtitles, no sidecar subtitles and no version picker on the Galaxy S25; Next on a saved episode online skipped a saved episode when one in between was missing; text cut off at 150% text on all four phones; the Campsite guest page said nothing after the host stopped; pictures did not load for a child profile; a child profile’s Movie Night page showed a raw error. App repairs are in unpublished test builds; server repairs are reported deployed. RETEST-1 has mixed results, with child delete-screen and sidecar failures and online Next/offline wording partial. Folder poster/NFO handling has no recorded repair. Not tested: any sound heard by a person, real screen-off sound, picture and sound on the TV, TalkBack, mobile data, Italian and Portuguese.

Oct 21 and 22 · Windows 0.1.98 and a browser through the away address

Passed: a hands-on run on Windows 0.1.98 (nine areas, no failures, including 10 of 11 picture-frame checks); a test library run (29 passed): the five folder types, embedded and sidecar subtitles, three audio tracks, chapters and a version picker; a Windows test PC updated itself to 0.1.99 on Oct 22 and ran normally.

Not tested: a second PC, sound by ear, the web library signed in, a restricted profile on the PC itself, the picture frame on a tablet or TV.

Oct 12 · Two homes and a phone on 5G

Passed: direct and Relay playback, network switching, two direct viewers, library sharing from invite through stopping access, phone backup, browse, favourites, playlists, radio, podcasts, Campsite host/guest setup, landscape and restart.

Not tested: two people on Relay, a friend's Chromecast, Beebo Auto in a car, a real TV pairing code, TalkBack and the newest fixes on real devices.
