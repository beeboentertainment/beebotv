Stream anywhere

Your library lives on your home PC — but you can reach it from your phone, a laptop, or the car, wherever you happen to be.

How it works

📱 On your phone or in a browser

Open your library from any device with a web browser, or the Beebo Entertainment app — same collection, same posters, wherever you are.

🔐 Your own access code

Everyone signs in with a personal access code you control, so only the people you invite can watch. Manage who has access any time.

🏠 Streamed from your PC

Video streams directly from your home computer — nothing is uploaded to a company’s cloud. It’s available whenever your PC is on.

Good to know: because it streams from your own PC, your library is reachable whenever that computer is on and connected.
