Stream anywhere

Your library lives on your home PC — but you can reach it from your phone, a laptop, or the car, wherever you happen to be.

How it works

📱 On your phone or in a browser

Open your library from any device with a web browser, or the Beebo Entertainment app — same collection, same posters, wherever you are.

🔐 Your own sign-in for everyone

Everyone signs in with their own username and password, so only the people you add can watch. Manage who has access any time.

🏠 Streamed from your PC

Your source video lives on your computer. A live stream can use a direct connection or a relay, depending on the available route. That computer must be on to serve its files; a completed offline download can be played with the computer off.

📥 Or take it offline

Download a title to your phone before you leave and watch it later with no signal and no data used — perfect for flights and road trips.

Good to know: because it streams from your own PC, your library is reachable whenever that computer is on and connected.

FOLLOW THE NUMBERS

Carry on where you left off

  1. Open Continue in the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap the play triangle beside a title to resume.
  3. Use Continue or All history at the top to change the list.

The guide describes the controls; the artwork is an original Beebo illustration, not a current device capture. Layouts and labels can vary by build.
