Know what costs money.

Beebo’s software and owner-operated connections are separate from the managed services we run. Prices below are our planned launch terms; paid activation and final limits are being verified.

At home

$ CA$0 Beebo software fee

Use your own computer and local network. Your hardware, power, storage and internet remain your own costs.

Your own remote connection

$ No Beebo relay charge

Use your own open ports, compatible automatic port mapping, VPN or relay. The owner chooses capacity based on their equipment and connection. Any outside provider’s charges are separate.

Account discovery and sign-in across these routes are still undergoing end-to-end validation; this is not a promise that every configuration is ready today.

Beebo-managed relay

Planned: CA$3/month for 2 simultaneous away-from-home streams, or CA$5/month for 4, per household.

These are concurrent streams using our managed service, not the number of people you can create profiles for. Final quality, household and billing limits will be shown before paid activation. No guaranteed speed or 4K conversion is implied.

BeeboVPN

Planned: CA$2/month per household.

The proposed allowance is 4 simultaneous VPN connections. Supported locations, limits and paid activation need final verification. This subscription is separate from media relay.

Current testing: the owner has reported successful Relay and VPN use. This is not a published guarantee for every account, location or device. Managed-service billing is being prepared. These cards are informational and do not start a subscription.

Why choose the paid option?

A managed relay can help when a direct connection is unavailable and you do not want to operate your own relay. A VPN is a separate network service. Paying does not add movies, shows, music rights, extra computer power or support for every source format.

Before you pay

Check the actual checkout for Canadian-dollar price, billing period, taxes, trial terms, included connections and cancellation details. Multi-month discounts and additional services are not final offers yet. Existing account terms remain the terms you accepted when purchasing.
