How it works

  1. Install Beebo on the computer that holds your movies and open it.
  2. If direct, automatic UPnP, and your own relay don't work for you and you need Beebo Relay, click Start free trial and create your login — your email and a password. You get 30 days free, no card needed.
  3. When you're ready to keep Beebo Relay running, Subscribe above to either plan and keep using the same login. Switch tiers or cancel anytime.

Already subscribed? Add more people
