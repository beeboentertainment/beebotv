Feature contributor reward

Beebo plans to offer a lifetime 50% subscription discount when a member’s original feature idea is selected, implemented, and released.

The reward will have published program rules before launch: Beebo must be able to verify the original contributor, the idea must be selected and shipped by Beebo, and the discount will apply to eligible Beebo subscriptions. Submitting an idea alone does not guarantee implementation or a reward.

Built for a respectful community

Before registration opens, Beebo Space will publish community rules, reporting tools, moderation standards, and a privacy notice. The launch will begin small so feedback stays useful and people can be supported properly.
