A GAME MADE FROM THE PEOPLE IN THE ROOM

Portrait Party

Take a photo or choose one photo you want to use. Approve a playful portrait version of yourself, join a private game board, and work out who is behind each card.

It uses a Beebo-made game name and original board design. Portrait Party is still being designed, so this page explains the intended experience rather than promising a released feature.

Private by default

Guests choose exactly one photo or use the in-app camera. The game should not request broad access to a phone’s photo library.

Each person approves

The guest sees the finished portrait before it joins the room. They can retake, change style, or leave without sharing a card.

Made for a group

Use a TV, computer, tablet or phone as the board. Guests play from their own phones after joining the room.

How a round is intended to work

01

Join

Scan the room QR code and choose a nickname.

02

Make a card

Take or pick one photo, choose a portrait style, then approve it.

03

Ask and narrow

Use original Beebo question prompts and card actions to narrow the board.

04

Reveal

Make a final pick. The room can start another round or clear every guest card.

Photo handling we are designing for

Local first: use Android’s system Photo Picker for a chosen image, or a camera photo taken for this game. Process the portrait on the device where practical. The original photo should never be placed on the game board; only the guest-approved game portrait is shared with the private room. The host can clear the room, and each guest can remove their own card. A future cloud-style option, if ever offered, must be separate, clearly explained and opt-in.

More than one game

Team trivia portraits

Use approved portraits for team badges and scoreboards, without exposing a person’s original photo.

Campfire story cast

Let the group choose their approved portrait as a character card for a shared story.

Trip recap covers

Offer an approved group-portrait cover for a private trip recap or event album.

Questions

Is this available now?

No. Portrait Party is planned. Beebo will only mark it available after it is in a downloadable build and tested with real phones.

Will Beebo upload everyone’s photo?

The intended default is no: select or take one photo, create a local portrait and share only the approved game card within the current private room. Any future service that sends photos away from the device must ask clearly before it does so.

Can children use it?

Adults should control the room and approve participation. The game should have no public profiles, public sharing or open chat. Beebo will review the final design against the applicable Google Play family and privacy requirements before release.

Why not call it “Guess Who?”

Portrait Party will use its own name, artwork, prompts and board design. It is a Beebo game inspired by the broad idea of a social guessing game, not a copy of another product’s branding or presentation.
