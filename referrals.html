COMMUNITY REFERRALS

Share Beebo. Keep track.

Regular users and creators can have referral codes. Sign in with an existing Beebo account to get yours. Your code records account referrals; it is not a coupon or a promise of a reward.

Nick can privately review which accounts recorded your code. You see only your totals, not their identities. Tester applications and Beebo accounts are counted separately.

Rewards are a future possibility.

Nick is considering creator discounts and a subscription benefit for people who maintain a qualifying referral group. Eligibility, qualifying subscriptions, cancellation rules and timing have not been set. There is no active five-member reward or 50% discount today. Honest feedback is welcome; referrals never require a positive review.

Already applied as a tester? Check your application and tester code. A tester application password is separate from your Beebo account password.
