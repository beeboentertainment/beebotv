Beebo Championships.

Free game tournaments designed to reward the people who play together. Each eligible winner will receive one month of Beebo subscription access as account credit.

No entry is open yet. Beebo is completing score verification, account-credit delivery, fair-play controls and official rules before the first tournament starts. No purchase will be required to enter.

Game-by-game winners

Each tournament will have its own published scoring method, deadline, prize value, winner count and tie-breaker.

Practice first

Visitors will be able to practise supported games before entering. Tournament results will use verified scores rather than self-reported results.

Private by default

Leaderboards will show chosen player aliases. Beebo will keep account and prize-contact information private.

Score challenges

Party events
