PLANNED

Beebo Championships.

Picture game nights with clear rules and friendly competition. Championships are a planned idea, not an open event.

No event is open yet. Score verification, fair-play controls and event rules need more work before any tournament can start.

Clear rules for each game

Each event would need a published scoring method, deadline and tie-breaker before people could join.

Practice first

Players could practise supported games before an event. Competitive results would need verified scores rather than self-reported results.

Privacy by design

A future leaderboard would need chosen player aliases and clear privacy controls. No leaderboard is open today.

Head-to-head games

Ideas for future brackets include Drop Four, Sea Battle, Chess, Checkers, Reversi, Mancala, Nine Men’s Morris, Tic-Tac-Toe and Dots and Boxes.

Score challenges

Possible score events include Five Dice, Hidden Mines, Number Grid, Five Letters, Patience, Car Bingo and Nature Bingo.

Party events

Movie Trivia, Sketch & Guess, Campfire Werewolf, Fake Out, Two Truths and a Lie, Who’s the Spy? and more are ideas for group events.
