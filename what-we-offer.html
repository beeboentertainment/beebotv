Who we are

Beebo Entertainment makes Beebo: a free, local-first media server with apps. It runs on your own Windows computer, holds the movies, shows, music, photos, audiobooks, podcasts and radio you already own, and streams them to your phone, tablet, TV, car or any web browser. Video goes straight from your computer to the viewer whenever it can. Beebo's own servers carry only small text, such as sign-in and connection set-up.

Beebo is made by a family man, not a data company. The code is closed source (all rights reserved). Beebo includes no movies, shows, music or channels; you bring your own files, and Live TV means your own tuner or your own playlist. We use the four labels Available, Beta, In development and Planned, and we never write a flat "100% working". Optional extras such as Beebo Relay (Beta) and BeeboVPN (In development) are listed with their label; this page does not list prices.

The testing percentages on this page come from our testing evidence page (release-evidence.html), and its overall figure is 70%: each of its 70 features is scored on 5 checks (Built 24, Automated tests 25, Real device 25, Real-world check 25, Ready to call Available 1). Each feature has five checks. Built is worth 24 points. Passing automated tests adds 25. Running on a real phone or PC adds 25. The real-world check adds 25. Our test process uses AI testers on real devices for that check. At 99%, all four checks have passed and no known error is open. That means tested and confirmed working within the recorded checks. The feature is considered fully coded and working. The last 1% is a human look, not more building. Nick or a tester looks with their own eyes and says Ready. Only Nick records the Ready check. An open known error caps the score at 90%. Existing record limits can keep it lower. At 99%, the label is "Tested and working, waiting for a human look". At 100%, it is "Ready, a human confirmed". How to read this. These percentages are share-of-list counts, not a promise or an audit. Every feature we list counts once, whether it is big or small, so a long list of small Beta items can move the number as much as one big feature. Features that were dropped or retired, and ideas, are not counted. Available means it is in a current download and a person has run its main use on a real device or PC. Beta means it is in a download or switchable today but is new, tested only by automatic tests, or had real problems. In development means it is being built, switched off, not in a download, or never tried on the real equipment it needs. Planned means intended, not built. Common questions

What is Beebo Entertainment?

Beebo is a free, local-first media server with apps. It runs on your own Windows computer, holds the movies, shows, music, photos, audiobooks, podcasts and radio that you already own, and streams them to your phone, tablet, TV, car or any web browser. Beebo includes no movies or shows; you bring your own files.

Is Beebo free?

The Windows server and the apps are free to use at home. Optional extras such as Beebo Relay (Beta) and BeeboVPN (In development) are listed with their status, and their terms are not final. Prices are listed on one page only, the pricing page, and Relay is not available to buy yet.

Is Beebo open source?

No. Beebo's own code is closed source and all rights are reserved. It does use third-party open-source components, and the apps include a notices screen that lists them.

Does my video go through Beebo's servers?

Whenever possible, video goes straight from your computer to the viewer. Beebo's servers carry only small text such as sign-in and connection set-up. If a direct connection cannot be made, the optional Beebo Relay (Beta) can carry the video; it is end-to-end encrypted and the relay cannot see it.

Which devices work today?

The server runs on Windows 10 and 11 and is Available. You watch with the Android test app, in any web browser, or by casting to a Chromecast. Linux and Docker or NAS are Beta. Fire TV, Roku, LG, Samsung, Xbox, macOS and the native iPhone, iPad and Apple TV apps are In development and have not been tested on real hardware.

Is Beebo on Google Play?

No. The Android app is a test download from this website that you install yourself. The Google Play version is in development and has not been submitted.

What is not finished?

Many things. The full list on this page says exactly which features are Available, Beta, In development or Planned, what was tested on real devices and when, and what nobody has tested yet. Examples: TV apps on real TVs, a real tuner, a real Mac, a real Linux desktop, and most of Beebo Auto beyond one real car check.

How is the completion percentage worked out?

It is a share-of-list count, not a promise and not an audit. For example, the share of listed features whose label is Available. The rules and the data file are on this page, and the numbers are calculated from that file by a script.

What does Beebo keep about me?

Your files stay on your own computer. Beebo keeps the amount of data used per account and basic connection records, including IP addresses, for 7 days for abuse checks. Nothing is sold.

Do you test on real devices?

Yes, on our own devices: a Galaxy S24 Ultra, a Galaxy S9+, a Chromecast TV, Windows PCs and one real car on one drive. These are our own checks, not a broad beta. Every line says what was tried and what was not.
