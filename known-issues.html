October 24 real-world checks

Added Oct 24, 2026, 9:15 PM Eastern. Real-device checks run by AI helpers on Oct 24, 2026 (afternoon and evening). Most phone checks used an unpublished phone test build (tester40 to tester42), not a published download. No percentage, checkmark or status label changed.

  • Away from home: a Galaxy S24 on 5G mobile data only reached the home server directly (about 1:30 PM Eastern, no relay, a TV episode played with the clock keeping pace) and through Beebo Relay (about 3:40 PM; a film played, but the clock ran slower than real time). At a real second household (about 7:50 to 8:05 PM) the S24 on 5G connected to a different Windows PC through Beebo Relay in about 2 seconds and played the original file of a film with picture and sound. Failed: choosing a converted 720p quality on that old 4-core PC failed twice. Not tested: Wi-Fi to mobile-data hand-over, sessions over a few minutes, a direct route to that PC.
  • Chromecast: on the real Living Room TV (test builds), the cast controls that had been dimmed and untappable worked by finger after a fix, and a streamed film cast with picture and sound on test build 42. Failed: casting a downloaded copy did not play on the TV. Not proven: the phone pass-through route, seeking by dragging, captions on the TV, a second sender.
  • Browser: in the browser player on the S24 over 5G through Beebo Relay, a separate subtitle file listed as “English (file)” and drew its three cues at the right times. Chrome on a PC passed playback, pause, arrow keys and quality changes. Failed (fixes written, not merged): Space and F need the video to have focus; a show page had no poster and low-contrast buttons; Suggestions loaded blank; Media requests showed a household member owner-only controls.
  • Also failed: Watch Together did not connect for two phones signed in only to the home server (test build 41 now says so honestly; it is not a working room), and Trip Journal “Save as a video” stayed at 0% on a Galaxy S9+.

October 26: failures still open

Evidence recorded through Oct 27, 2026, 8:30 AM Eastern (report receipt times and operator clocks; updated Oct 27, 2026, 8:45 AM Eastern). Passing another route or merging a source fix does not clear these failures in the tested build. A row with a failure is held at 90% or lower and marked to be fixed (several rows were updated on the evening of Oct 26: some failures are closed below and new ones are added); once fixed it goes back to 99%, and Nick and Claude then confirm it together before it is called 100%.

  • Captions appeared on the phone but were missing on both TVs when casting from the Android app. On the morning of Oct 27 captions on the phone were confirmed by two testers (an AI tester and Nick); on the Samsung TV via Chromecast they were still missing with a new test subtitle file, although the film now loaded and played with sound.
  • A cast stuck after the short test film ended; play and seek did not recover it normally.
  • On Nick’s own home server, watched away from home in a Samsung TV browser, 480p resume did not start. Original played, Skip intro worked and the next episode auto-played. The route and exact observation time are unknown.
  • The Pixel typed-address certificate-repair box was missing or partial; the in-place pasted-link sign-in attempt was inconclusive. The fix is merged but not verified: the retest was blocked on both test phones on the newer builds (they were signed in; a fresh install or a second user is needed). The warm-link pairing repair is also merged but not verified.
  • The Pixel saved resume point disagreed after picture-in-picture and a backward seek. On S9+ round 6, the Home Resume tile restarted at zero and overwrote the saved point. On the newer test builds the resume point itself passes. The Home “Continue watching” list failed after Start over on two builds (tester52 and tester53) but passed in 9 of 9 runs on the newest build (tester54, Pixel 3 and Galaxy S9+, including cold starts). It is still sometimes empty after a short resumed session (Pixel 3 of 3 runs, Galaxy S9+ twice), even after a cold start; both test phones used one test account at the same time, so the other phone’s write may be the cause (not proven). The row is held at 90%. Early morning Oct 27 (tester55, Galaxy S9+, test server refreshed): Home Resume after resumed sessions passed 5 of 5 and Start over passed 2 of 3; the one failure came right after the phone was signed in afresh (Home empty for 30 seconds although the server had the position), so the issue stays open. In the web player, Skip intros passed (one jump, no re-skip after a hand seek).
  • Closed on the evening of Oct 26: a long subtitle cue at 200% that clipped in landscape did not clip on a Pixel 3 and a Galaxy S9+ on two newer test builds. The Pixel’s first 200% session once drew no cue text in four looks; it was not seen again and is unexplained.
  • Wi-Fi to 5G switching during browser playback failed in three of four runs. Steady five-minute 5G playback passed; that does not clear the switching failure.
  • New on Oct 27 (second household): a phone on the same home Wi-Fi as the PC usually connects directly (5 of 5 launches in a baseline) but sometimes falls back to Beebo Relay: 5 of the PC’s last 30 logged connections went through Relay, and one launch said “Through Beebo Relay” and blamed the router. It spends the owner’s Relay data when it happens. A fix is under review and is not merged.
  • The Kitchen announcements Enable box did not switch on after three attempts; nothing was sent. At a second household later the sender worked and logged two messages, but a Galaxy S24 showed no banner, notification or sound, so the phone receiver is a new failure.
  • Closed on the evening of Oct 26: the More connection card falsely warning that a working computer needed attention was not seen in 14 fresh starts on two test phones (a brief “could not be confirmed” notice was seen once on the earlier build and not on the newer one). Open question on the newest build: after a language or font change the card said “could not be confirmed” for about 30 to 45 seconds, twice, on a Galaxy S9+ (tester54); it may have been a brief real network drop. Closed on the morning of Oct 27: it did not reproduce on tester55 (4 of 4 changes on a Galaxy S9+ and repeated changes on a Pixel 3), so it is closed as not reproduced (cause never found).
  • Closed on the evening of Oct 26: the paused mini player can now be closed (an X appears while paused, the strip stays gone) on an audiobook, music and a podcast on test phones, and the Radio first-open failure did not reproduce in 7 of 7 runs on a Galaxy S9+. The QA server’s get-app page still showed stale Android and Auto versions.
  • Casting to the Samsung TV: the TV volume slider and the Unmute button did nothing audible (the number on the phone changed but the TV’s real volume did not; Unmute flipped back to Mute). Captions were again missing on the TV with a proper 3-minute film although they showed on the phone. A 20-second film would not cast properly. Marked to be fixed; the Chromecast row is held at 90%. A Samsung TV volume change and a captions fix were merged into the main code on Oct 27 but have not been seen on a TV. Later on Oct 27 (second household): a streamed film cast with picture and sound; a film downloaded on the phone failed to cast (“The media link expired”); and starting a different film after a cast had reached its end did not load it (the Chromecast row now has six open issues).
  • In Chrome on a PC, choosing 480p turned the picture black (“Playback interrupted”) while Original worked; the player remembered 480p and repeated the failure after signing in again. A second-household check found 720p fails the same way, reloading the page signs the person out, and the server started converted 480p, 720p and 1080p in under a second when asked directly, so the web player or its path stops the session. Marked to be fixed.
  • Camera backup and “Add a video from this phone” blank the location data in the copy on the PC, so it is not the original file (Nick wants originals kept, with an option to remove location). On the PC, adding a Photos folder failed when a saved default folder was missing. Marked to be fixed.
  • On the PC, the Bell showed a blank entry for the phone’s “Downloads running low” alert (the phone’s own alert was correct). Email alerts were not tested: no email sender is set up, so nothing was sent.
  • The new-device notice calls a Samsung TV’s own browser “Chrome on Linux” (a label, not a security problem).
  • Closed on the morning of Oct 27: Surprise me replacing a film’s saved place (43% became 0% on Oct 26). On tester55 (Pixel 3) a film at 33% kept its place after two Surprise me runs and the server position did not change. Limits: one phone, a made-up test film, the second film and the empty and error states not reached. The row is back at 99% (tested and working, waiting for a human look).
  • Campsite games (two real phones): after a ladder landed on square 100 in Snakes and Ladders the game did not end until the host’s next roll; and after the host leaves a finished room the guest sees “Player wins!” instead of the winner’s name and the scores vanish. Four games (Drop Four, Snakes and Ladders, Mancala, Dots and Boxes) were otherwise played to a natural end with both screens equal. The row is held at 90%; a fix was merged on Oct 27 and has not yet been checked on installed phones.
  • Closed late on Oct 26: the landscape player’s top control row at large font sizes now folds what does not fit into a More popup, and nothing was clipped or lost on a Galaxy S9+ (1.3 and 2.0) and a Pixel 3 (1.0, 1.3 and 2.0) on tester53 and tester54. The player is landscape-only, so the portrait fit cannot be tested; a label that wraps and a popup that can overlap the Markers label are cosmetic. The Accessibility row is back at 99%.
  • Audiobooks: the speed button showing 1.15x after a finished book was restarted from a bookmark (second household) did not reproduce on tester55 (Pixel 3, Oct 27); Reset to 1x works and a bookmark can be deleted with Undo. The earlier paused chapter-position loss is closed. Still open: once, tapping a bookmark’s trash signed the Pixel out, and another test member then showed the first member’s queued place (not isolated; it may be an expired sign-in after the test server was refreshed). The row is held at 90%.
  • Beebo Auto 1.20: Nick’s first look in his own car on Oct 27 (about 8:28 to 8:29 AM Eastern) showed the app, four tabs, Continue Watching and TV Shows with real items, and an episode started and played with sound. Not checked: Next, Previous and Pause working, steering-wheel buttons, search, the Movies and Music tabs and any drive. Open: three small defects on Auto’s phone settings screen (a raw code object, a doubled “Passenger games” heading, content under the bottom bar); a fix exists on a branch, is not merged and has not been seen on a device. The row is capped at 90%.
  • Story Studio on the installed Windows app: the preview is a blank white box and Play does nothing (character art files are not in the installed package). Work on the richer story features is on hold.
  • Movie Night and the Picture frame announce a wrong address (a PC’s virtual-adapter address instead of its Wi-Fi address): a phone scanning the frame’s QR code got a blank page, although the frame itself worked with the Wi-Fi address. The link fix was merged on Oct 27 and is not yet checked on an installed build.
  • Jellyfin-compatible mode: the protocol worked, but the official Jellyfin desktop client connects and cannot play films, and its failed playback queued a conversion of a real film that nobody asked for.
  • Parental controls: tightening a profile’s limit while it is watching does not stop the running video; only the next start is refused.
  • Keyboard use on the installed Windows app: controls on film cards have no visible focus ring, so a keyboard user can reach a delete confirmation unseen. A screen reader (Narrator) was not run.   • Kitchen announcements: the PC sender worked but the Galaxy S24 app received nothing.
  • Space Saver: “Back up now” for a new photo failed twice on the same Wi-Fi (cause not proven). A scan retest on Oct 27 passed (2 files backed up, the PC copies matched, nothing deleted); the new-photo case was not retested.
