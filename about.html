A hello from Beebo

Made by a family man,
not a data company.

A personal collection should feel personal. That’s the idea behind Beebo.

Beebo is a free media server and a set of players for the things you already own. Your Windows PC gives your collection a home. Your phone or browser gives you another place to enjoy it.

Nick is building Beebo around the everyday things that matter: an evening watching something together, music from your own collection, or a trip with a few things ready offline.

It’s still growing. Some parts are Available, some are Beta, and others are still being built. The unfinished parts deserve just as much clarity as the finished ones. Each feature’s tested score is on our testing evidence page.

What matters here

Keep it personal.
Keep it honest.

  • A library that starts with you.

    Your own media, on your own computer. Beebo includes no movies or shows.

  • Clear about what is ready.

    Visible status labels, dated testing records and open issues. An automated test is different from a real trip or a real device.

  • Room to choose.

    The server and apps are free. Optional paid extras are not for sale yet.

  • A website with a little breathing room.

    No web analytics, trackers, remote fonts or computer narration in the new pages. Beebo is for adults 18+.

A plain privacy promise

video is end-to-end encrypted and the relay cannot see it; connection records, including IP addresses, are kept 7 days for abuse handling; nothing is sold.
