THE LATEST FROM BEEBO

What's new

Recent changes, what is included in the public downloads, and work still being checked.

Updated Nov 6, 2026, 6:30 AM Eastern.

Saved public release records: Windows 0.1.113, published Nov 3, 2026, 1:41 PM Eastern; Android 1.51-tester68 (build 118), published Nov 2, 2026, 9:22 AM Eastern. These are saved feed identities, not a fresh live-feed or installed-file check.

Available below means the stated change is included in that public release record. It is not a new device pass or percentage. In development means the change is still being checked and is not in those public downloads. Source and automated checks do not establish an installed build or complete device coverage.

The test phones have tester70 (build 120), while the public Android record remains tester68. The latest phone journeys each have 9 PASS and 1 INFO and touched parts of the same 2 of 31 active phone features (6.45% smoke coverage only). Full qualifications remain 0/53 established; full coverage and current error counts are UNKNOWN.

Sound was muted. Casting, away-from-home, seeking, PiP, complete phone features and Windows behavior were not tested by those journeys. The current Windows installed-file/feed match remains unverified. Pending work and later source merges are not claimed to be in the recorded test APK.

Versioned release notes · Current progress and limits · Dated evidence

Phone app

Clearer casting to your TV

While your TV gets ready, the phone now shows "Preparing your TV". If it does not work, you can try again. If you pick something else meanwhile, the old attempt is dropped so it cannot start by surprise.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Smarter choice of what to send to the TV

Beebo now tries your original file on the TV first. It only makes a converted copy if the TV cannot play the original. A stream worked out for an older choice is thrown away when you load a newer one.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Stereo requests stay with the cast attempt

The stereo request is tied to the current cast attempt to avoid repeating an older request.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Keeping a saved position when a ready TV reloads

The test source includes a change to retain the saved position when a ready TV reloads. The latest phone smoke checks did not test receiver reloads.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Seeking by touch is easier

If the player controls are hidden, your first touch on the seek bar now brings them back instead of jumping by accident.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

The Back button and Stop behave better

Back keeps the film playing while you browse. The test source adds protection for that handoff and for Stop after Back. Broader reconnection and recovery gaps remain open.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Photos keep loading after a bad moment

If the photo grid hits an error or you retry, scrolling now carries on loading the rest instead of stalling.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Saved playback positions in tester68

Android tester68 includes fixes for saved playback positions: a stalled TV's zero reading no longer replaces the kept position, and an audiobook keeps its last remembered place when a song or a film takes over.

Available · In the saved PUBLIC tester68 download (build 118)

Wording updated Nov 6, 2026, 7:32 PM Eastern.

Windows app

Background conversions wait while you watch

Routine background conversions are held while playback is active. The effect on playback performance has not been measured by the latest checks.

In development · Source changes being checked; not in the public download yet

No newer installed Windows build is established by these notes.

Fix for "This title could not be found"

Windows 0.1.113 lists a known problem: "Prepare for streaming" can fail when a film and its converted copy share a name. A fix is in testing. The movie counts in the sidebar were also made to agree with the movie list.

In development · Source changes being checked; not in the public download yet

No newer installed Windows build is established by these notes.

Photo folders on a drive that is unplugged

You can now edit your list of photo folders even when one of them lives on a drive that is not connected right now. New folders are still checked properly.

In development · Source changes being checked; not in the public download yet

No newer installed Windows build is established by these notes.

Removing a picture frame link stays removed

If you remove a picture frame and the screen then fails to refresh, the removed frame no longer reappears in the list.

In development · Source changes being checked; not in the public download yet

No newer installed Windows build is established by these notes.

Email settings keep what you typed

The email login and notification-address drafts are kept when Settings finishes loading. This is a change being checked, not a claim that every Settings draft is preserved.

In development · Source changes being checked; not in the public download yet

No newer installed Windows build is established by these notes.

Connection cards, mini player, retries and notices

Windows 0.1.113 includes fixes for saved connection choices and the mini player's selected title and saved position. Live TV guide and podcast loading errors have Retry controls. Prepare for streaming locks its quality choice while Start is pending. The browser player explains when a daily watching limit stops a film, and email settings have visible labels.

Available · In Windows 0.1.113

More translated text

Windows 0.1.113 has translated explanations for common hardware-conversion failures, plus wording fixes in French, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese.

Available · In Windows 0.1.113

Unknown raw error details may still appear untranslated.

Campsite and games

Stop really stops for a Stream Once host

When the host of a shared stream taps Stop on their own phone, they now leave the shared stream properly.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Wireless speaker status in your language

Common status messages on the phone's wireless-speaker screen use translated labels instead of raw status text.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Movie Night menu works better with a keyboard

Keyboard focus stays where you expect it, holding Enter no longer picks the first choice by accident, and a failed save is announced.

In development · Source changes being checked; not in the public download yet

No newer installed Windows build is established by these notes.

Accounts and safety

Casting through Beebo's relay is tied to you

Cast relay requests are checked against the signed-in account and the selected home server. Changes also retire older requests when their owner changes, and casting diagnostics use fixed categories rather than web addresses. Broader account and reconnection cases still need their own checks.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Family dashboard follows who is signed in

The family dashboard on the phone now follows the person who is signed in, and retries stay with that person if someone else switches in.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

Behind the scenes

More translated web pages

The viewing privacy form messages and the appearance (theme) page in the browser are now translated.

In development · Source changes being checked; not in the public download yet

No newer installed Windows build is established by these notes.

Smaller phone reliability fixes

Changes being checked cover Library Health loading, quick playlist taps, cancellation of wake requests, saved announcement settings after a refresh failure, and emoji-safe Trip recap text. These are scoped changes; broader wake, cache, recovery and account-change gaps are not declared fixed.

In development · In testing, not in the public download yet

What the labels mean

Available identifies inclusion in the saved public release. Beta identifies a feature that can be tried while it is still being shaped. In development identifies work still being checked; Planned identifies work not started. These labels do not add readiness credit. Only Nick records final acceptance.

Individual fixes, recovery, wake and account-change scenarios still need matching tests. No whole-feature or universal recovery claim is made here.
