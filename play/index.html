How it works

One link. Two players. Your own screens.

  1. Choose “Invite a friend” and pick a nickname.
  2. Send the invite link (or show the QR code) to one friend. It works once, for 15 minutes.
  3. Your friend opens it, picks a nickname, and you take turns from your own devices. Keep this page open: reloading leaves the table.
