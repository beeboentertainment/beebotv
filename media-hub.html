Your own media hub

Your movies and shows, organized into a library that looks and feels like the streaming apps you already know — except everything in it is yours.

What it does

📚 Builds your library automatically

Point it at your Movies and TV folders and it catalogs everything into a clean, browsable grid — no manual tagging.

🖼️ Posters & details

Each title gets its poster, cast, description and year pulled in automatically, so your collection looks the part.

🔎 Search & browse

Find anything fast by title, actor or release date — and see at a glance what’s new or what’s missing from a series.

🕹️ Emulator launcher

Point it at your emulators and your own legally-owned games and launch them from the same place — movies, shows and games, all under one roof.

It’s all yours. BeeboTV ships with no media of its own — it simply organizes and plays the collection you already have on your home computer.
