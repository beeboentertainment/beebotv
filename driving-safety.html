Driving safety

The car apps are built so screens stay dark while you drive. Here’s how that works — and what you agree to when you use them.

A note on Beebo Mirror: the video-on-the-car-screen behavior described below belongs to Beebo Mirror — a separate, partner project, still in development and not yet available. This page describes how it's designed to work once it launches. Beebo Auto (audio, already available today) doesn't put video on the car screen at all.

Never watch video or operate a screen while driving. By using the Beebo Entertainment car apps you agree to use them safely and lawfully, and you take full responsibility for doing so. The driver’s attention belongs on the road.

What the app does to help

⏳ Video only in the start-up window

Beebo Mirror shows video on the car screen only after the car has stayed in Park since you turned it on, and only once a built-in 2-minute countdown has fully elapsed. Until then the screen shows a waiting card, not video.

🔒 It locks for the rest of the trip if the car moves

If the car moves or shifts out of Park at any point, mirroring stops and stays off for the rest of the trip — the screen shows “Mirroring paused for safety.” To use it again you must turn the car off and back on, keep it in Park, and wait the two minutes again. There is no way to mirror video while moving.

📱 Controls on your phone

Start and stop live on the phone, not the car screen. On the rare head-unit that can’t report its gear or speed, a clearly-labelled “My car is parked” confirmation on the phone lets a genuinely parked driver watch — and mirroring switches off automatically the moment any movement is detected.

Your responsibility

These features are safety aids, not guarantees. Sensors, cars, and software can be wrong, delayed, or unavailable, and the app cannot know your situation. You must not rely on the app to make it safe or legal to use a screen while driving.

You are responsible for knowing and obeying all laws that apply where you drive, including laws about distracted driving and screens visible to a driver. You assume all risk from using the apps in or around a vehicle. Full terms are in the Terms of Service.
