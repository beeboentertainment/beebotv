YOUR BEEBO SERVER

Open your library.

Enter the address for the computer that runs Beebo. You will sign in on your own server — this website never receives your password.

For example, familyname.beebo.tv when away, or http://192.168.1.50:47811 on home Wi-Fi.

Where do I find this?

On the computer running Beebo, open Get Started and look for the connection address or QR code. Your permanent address usually ends in .beebo.tv.
