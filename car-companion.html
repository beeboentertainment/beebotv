Car companion apps

Turn the back seat into a movie theater and a game room. Beebo Entertainment brings your own library along for the drive — so a long road trip with the kids flies by.

Made for road trips with kids. Everyone watches the same film in sync, the sound comes through the car, and when they get restless there are games to play. Or pre-load your phone with your videos before you leave and watch with no signal and no data used — no data-hungry streaming service, and no “are we there yet?” meltdown.

What it does in the car

🎬 Real video on the car screen Beebo Mirror

Android Auto normally only lets apps play sound in the car. Beebo Mirror puts the actual picture up on the car display, so the kids can watch their movies and shows — not just listen to them.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Everyone watches together, in sync Watch Together

Have more than one screen in the car? They all play the same movie in step. A built-in audio-delay slider nudges the sound so Bluetooth speakers and headphones stay perfectly lip-synced. You can shrink a screen, move it, or switch one off entirely.

🎮 Passenger games Roadside Rumble

When the movie’s over and they’re getting antsy, the kids can play games together — or, if a child is riding solo, against Cruisin’ Carl, a friendly built-in opponent, so there’s always someone to play with.

🔊 Sound through the car speakers Beebo Auto

Your library’s audio plays right through the car’s speakers over Android Auto — the same simple, hands-free experience as any car media app.

🔗 Bring your own stuff

Paste a web link to pull up your own online files, or stream a movie straight from your home PC over the internet. Their favorites, wherever you are.

The three apps

Beebo Auto

The audio companion — plays your library through the car speakers, with passenger games and your own saved links.

Beebo Mirror

Puts your phone’s whole screen on the car display, so films play as real video. Installed from Google Play.

Beebo Entertainment (movie app)

Your library and player, with Watch Together, Picture-in-Picture, and Stream-from-PC built in.

A note for parents: the driver’s controls stay simple and hands-free — the movies, screens, and games all live in the back seat where the kids are.
