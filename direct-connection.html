How it works

Straight from home to you

Video goes over WebRTC, straight between your home computer and your phone or browser. It’s encrypted from end to end (WebRTC DTLS).

Beebo’s service (beebo.tv) only helps your phone find the computer and signs people in. With a direct connection, your video never passes through Beebo’s servers or any relay.

What the connection test checks

The first time you set up Beebo, the Test your connection wizard runs two quick tests with your phone.

  1. Test 1: Watch at home

    Put your phone on the same Wi-Fi as the computer. Open the Beebo app, tap Scan QR to connect and scan the code on the computer.

    The test ticks when a device on your home network reaches the computer. No address is stored.

  2. Test 2: Watch away from home

    Turn Wi-Fi off on your phone so it uses mobile data. Open the Beebo app, or scan the code to open yourname.beebo.tv. Sign in and start any video.

    The test shows live whether it connected Direct, through a relay, or couldn’t connect.

  3. Result

    You’ll then see your options: Beebo Relay, Cloudflare only, Open ports on my router, or Home only for now.

What the computer checks by itself

While you test, the computer also looks at your home internet on its own:

Did the router open the ports?

Beebo asks your router to open its UDP ports automatically. It tries NAT-PMP first, then UPnP. You can see the result in Settings › Your Beebo address.

Anything in the way?

  • Shared internet address (CGNAT): your provider shares one internet address between many homes. Direct connections from outside probably won’t work.
  • Two routers in a row (double NAT).

The automatic check is a hint. The phone test is the proof. If Test 2 says Direct, you’re all set.

If the router didn’t open the ports

Switch on UPnP, or forward the ports by hand

Most home routers have UPnP switched on. If the check says your router didn’t open the ports, you have two choices:

  • Switch on UPnP in your router’s settings. It’s sometimes called UPnP IGD or NAT-PMP.
  • Forward Beebo’s ports by hand. See Open ports on your router.

A note on security: UPnP lets any program on your home network ask the router to open ports. That’s a small risk. Forwarding only Beebo’s ports by hand is the tighter option.

Home only

Watch on the same Wi-Fi as the computer

There’s nothing to set up, and it is free (no relay). Choose it in the wizard result, or go to Settings › Connection › Change option › Home only for now.

With Home only, watching away from home may not work. You can change it anytime.

Test again anytime

Changed your router or internet provider? Go to Settings › Connection › Re-run test. The same screen shows your current status, and Change option lets you pick a different way to connect.
