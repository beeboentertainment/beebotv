Beebo vs Plex

Plex is a very widely used personal media server. Beebo is a newer, smaller one built around Windows PCs, Android and browsers. Here is how they differ today, including where Plex is the stronger choice.

Checked September 21, 2026. As of September 2026 and only for the versions named on this page: Beebo for Windows 0.1.58 and the Beebo Android app 1.40.

The short version. Plex is the more mature and more widely available product: more devices, more server choices and a larger feature set. Beebo is a free, Windows-first alternative where away-from-home viewing does not need a pass and your library and video stay on your own PC. Choose Plex if you need Apple TV, Roku, a NAS server or Plex’s Live TV and DVR today. Choose Beebo if a free, Windows-first setup with Android and browser viewing matches how you watch.

Cost, in numbers

  • Beebo: free to use at home. Away from home is free with a direct connection, automatic UPnP or your own relay. Only optional Beebo services, such as Beebo Relay, are paid (about CA$3/month for 1080p or CA$5/month for 4K, per household; its checkout is not open yet).
  • Plex: the free plan streams on your own network. Remote Watch Pass is US$2.99/month or US$29.99/year. Plex Pass is about US$7/month or US$70/year, or US$749.99 lifetime for new purchases since July 1, 2026, and a new 5-year plan is US$249.99. Check Plex’s plans page for current prices.

Prices as of September 2026 and in the currency each company shows. They change, so check the source before you decide.

Away from home

Plex’s support pages say that remote playback of your own media needs a Plex Pass on the server owner’s account, or a Remote Watch Pass on the account that is watching. Beebo tries a direct connection first, then automatic UPnP or your own relay, all at no charge. Only if none of those work is the optional paid Beebo Relay there as a fallback.

Ads

Plex also offers a free, ad-supported catalogue of movies and shows, separate from your own library, and says a Plex Pass does not remove ads from it. Ads are off by default. If Beebo ever offers optional ad-supported credit, it will only be switched on if you opt in, can be turned off any time, and is never required to use the app.

When Plex may suit you better

  • More devices: Plex has apps for Apple TV, Roku, Fire TV, Samsung, LG, consoles and more. Beebo’s Fire TV, Roku, Samsung, LG, Xbox and Apple apps are in development and not published.
  • More server choices: Plex runs on Windows, Mac, Linux, FreeBSD, Docker and many NAS brands. Beebo’s server is Windows today, with Linux and Docker in Beta.
  • Live TV and DVR, available now with a Plex Pass and a tuner. Beebo’s is Beta and needs your own HDHomeRun tuner.
  • Plexamp and other music extras with a Plex Pass, and a long-established set of paid features.
  • An optional free, ad-supported catalogue of movies and shows, if you want it alongside your own library. Beebo includes no catalogue.
  • Years of development and a large community, where Beebo is new and small.

When Beebo may suit you better

  • The app is free to use at home, and features Plex lists under Plex Pass (hardware video conversion, offline downloads on Android) are included in Beebo. Some others, such as skip intro and themes, are Beta.
  • Away-from-home viewing is free with a direct connection, automatic UPnP or your own relay.
  • Your library and video stay on your own PC. Beebo’s servers handle sign-in and introductions.
  • Campsite Mode (Android, available now) and Trip Journal (Beta) for camping and road trips.
  • Family controls enforced by your PC: age ratings, blocked titles, a daily limit, bedtime hours and an owner PIN.

Who should choose what

Choose Beebo if

  • You run Windows 10 or 11 at home.
  • You watch on Android, in a browser, or on Android TV and Google TV.
  • You want away-from-home options built in.
  • You are happy with a newer app that is still growing.

Choose Plex if

  • You need the widest choice of devices today.
  • Your server will be a NAS, Mac or Linux box.
  • You value its maturity, community and long list of features.

Trying Beebo alongside Plex

Beebo does not move or change your files, so you can point it at the same folders Plex uses and try it beside Plex. The current download does not import Plex watch history, so expect to start fresh today. An importer for watched marks, resume points, ratings and playlists is in Windows 0.1.58 (Beta), with a review step and an undo. Posters and descriptions in Beebo come from TMDB with your own free key.

Questions

Do I have to pay to watch Beebo away from home?

No. Away from home is free with a direct connection, automatic UPnP or your own relay. The optional Beebo Relay is a paid fallback for households where none of those work.

Should I choose Beebo or Plex?

It depends on what you need. Plex is more mature and runs on far more devices today. Beebo may suit you if you run Windows, watch on Android or in a browser, and want a free app that keeps your library on your own PC.

Can I use Beebo and Plex at the same time?

Yes. Beebo does not move or change your files, so both can read the same folders. Windows 0.1.58 includes an importer (Beta) for Plex watch history, with a review step and an undo.
