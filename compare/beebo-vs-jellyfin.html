Beebo vs Jellyfin

Jellyfin is a free, open-source media system run by volunteers. Beebo is a newer app for Windows households. Here is how they differ today, including where Jellyfin is the stronger choice.

Checked September 21, 2026. As of September 2026 and only for the versions named on this page: Beebo for Windows 0.1.57 and the Beebo Android app 1.38.

The short version. Jellyfin is free, open source and available on many more devices and server platforms, with a plugin system and free Live TV and hardware conversion. Beebo is not open source yet, but it builds in away-from-home access, family controls and camping and road-trip features, and it runs on a Windows PC you already own. Choose Jellyfin if open source, plugins or a NAS server matter most. Choose Beebo if you want a Windows-first app with built-in away-from-home options and you accept that it is newer and smaller.

Cost, in numbers

  • Beebo: free to use at home. Away from home is free with a direct connection, automatic UPnP or your own relay. Only optional Beebo services, such as Beebo Relay, are paid (about CA$3/month for 1080p or CA$5/month for 4K, per household; its checkout is not open yet).
  • Jellyfin: free. Jellyfin says the server and official clients are free to download “now and always”, with no premium licences or features.

Away from home

Jellyfin has no built-in relay or NAT traversal. Its documentation says you must expose the server yourself and describes a reverse proxy (its preferred method), a VPN or a VPS relay, and advises against forwarding ports straight to the internet. Beebo builds the choice in: a free yourname.beebo.tv address, a connection test, and direct connection, automatic UPnP or your own relay, all free, with the optional paid Beebo Relay as a fallback. If you enjoy building your own access, Jellyfin gives you full control.

Ads

Jellyfin says it is not in the media business. Ads are off by default. If Beebo ever offers optional ad-supported credit, it will only be switched on if you opt in, can be turned off any time, and is never required to use the app.

When Jellyfin may suit you better

  • Open source: Jellyfin is free software under the GNU GPL. Beebo is not open source, and an open-source direction is only Planned.
  • Free everything: hardware video conversion, Live TV with DVR and its other features have no paid tier. Beebo’s Live TV is Beta and needs your own HDHomeRun tuner.
  • More devices: Android TV, Fire TV, Roku, LG, Samsung, Xbox and, since July 2026, Apple TV through Swiftfin. Beebo’s Roku, Samsung and LG apps are in development, and Apple is Planned.
  • More server choices: Windows, macOS, Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, Podman, Synology and TrueNAS SCALE. Beebo’s server is Windows today, with Linux and Docker in Beta.
  • A plugin system and a large volunteer community. Beebo has no plug-ins, only a small read-only API in Beta.
  • Books and comics support, expanded in version 12.0.
  • No company account: Jellyfin says it has no tracking, phone-home or central servers collecting your data. Beebo uses a small service for sign-in and introductions.

When Beebo may suit you better

  • Away-from-home access is built in and guided, without setting up a reverse proxy or VPN.
  • Family controls enforced by your PC: age ratings, blocked titles, a daily limit, bedtime hours and an owner PIN.
  • Campsite Mode (Android, available now) and Trip Journal (Beta) for camping and road trips, plus Beebo Auto for the car.
  • An Android app with downloads for offline viewing, available now.
  • Your library and video stay on your own PC, and Beebo’s servers only handle sign-in and introductions.

Who should choose what

Choose Beebo if

  • You run Windows 10 or 11 at home.
  • You watch on Android, in a browser, or on Android TV and Google TV.
  • You want away-from-home options built in.
  • You are happy with a newer app that is still growing.

Choose Jellyfin if

  • You need the widest choice of devices today.
  • Your server will be a NAS, Mac or Linux box.
  • You value its maturity, community and long list of features.

Trying Beebo alongside Jellyfin

Beebo does not move or change your files, so you can point it at the same folders Jellyfin uses and try it beside Jellyfin. Beebo does not import Jellyfin watch history. Beebo also has an optional Jellyfin-compatible mode, off by default and in Beta, that lets some Jellyfin-compatible apps connect to Beebo. It has not been tested against real Jellyfin apps yet. Beebo is not Jellyfin and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project.

Questions

Is Beebo open source like Jellyfin?

Not today. An open-source direction is Planned, with no date. Jellyfin is free software under the GNU GPL.

Can I use Jellyfin apps with Beebo?

Beebo has an optional Jellyfin-compatible mode, in Beta for the next release and off by default. It has not been tested against real Jellyfin apps yet, and Beebo is not Jellyfin or affiliated with that project.

Is Beebo easier to use away from home than Jellyfin?

Beebo builds in a free address, a connection test and several free options. Jellyfin has no built-in relay and its documentation describes setting up a reverse proxy, VPN or VPS yourself. Which is easier depends on how comfortable you are with networking.
