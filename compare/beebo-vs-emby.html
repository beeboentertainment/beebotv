Beebo vs Emby

Emby is an established personal media server with a free server and a paid Premiere tier. Beebo is a newer, smaller app for Windows households. Here is how they differ today, including where Emby is the stronger choice.

Checked September 21, 2026. As of September 2026 and only for the versions named on this page: Beebo for Windows 0.1.57 and the Beebo Android app 1.38.

The short version. Emby runs on far more devices and server platforms, and its Premiere tier adds mature features such as DVR and CarPlay and Android Auto. Beebo is free, and several features Emby lists under Premiere are included in Beebo at no charge, though some of them are still Beta. Choose Emby if you need broad device support, a mature DVR, or you like a one-time lifetime purchase. Choose Beebo if you want a free, Windows-first app and are comfortable with a newer product.

Cost, in numbers

  • Beebo: free to use at home. Away from home is free with a direct connection, automatic UPnP or your own relay. Only optional Beebo services, such as Beebo Relay, are paid (about CA$3/month for 1080p or CA$5/month for 4K, per household; its checkout is not open yet).
  • Emby: the server is free. Emby Premiere is $4.99/month, $54/year or $119 lifetime for a household, with a 30-device limit, as listed on emby.media.

Prices as of September 2026 and in the currency each company shows. They change, so check the source before you decide.

Away from home

Emby Connect, its sign-in service for remote access, is listed as a free feature, and Emby’s guides cover setting up access, including port forwarding. Beebo builds in a free yourname.beebo.tv address, a connection test, and direct connection, automatic UPnP or your own relay, all free, with the optional paid Beebo Relay as a fallback.

Ads

Emby describes itself as software for your own library and not a streaming service. Ads are off by default. If Beebo ever offers optional ad-supported credit, it will only be switched on if you opt in, can be turned off any time, and is never required to use the app.

When Emby may suit you better

  • More devices: Android TV, Fire TV, Apple TV, Roku, LG, Samsung, Xbox, PlayStation and Chromecast. Beebo’s Fire TV, Roku, Samsung, LG, Xbox and Apple apps are in development and not published.
  • More server choices: Windows, Linux, macOS, FreeBSD, Docker and many NAS brands. Beebo’s server is Windows today, with Linux and Docker in Beta.
  • DVR with Premiere and free guide data for the US, Canada and the UK. Beebo’s Live TV is Beta and needs your own HDHomeRun tuner.
  • CarPlay and Android Auto support with Premiere, and voice control through Alexa and Google Home.
  • A one-time lifetime price of $119, if you prefer to pay once. Beebo’s app is free at home, so there is nothing to buy once, though optional Beebo services are paid.
  • Years of development, an active community and community plugins and themes.

When Beebo may suit you better

  • The app is free to use at home. Several features Emby lists under Premiere are included in Beebo at no charge: hardware video conversion, offline downloads on Android and backup and restore are available now, while intro skipping, Live TV and themes are Beta.
  • Away-from-home viewing is free with a direct connection, automatic UPnP or your own relay.
  • Campsite Mode (Android, available now) and Trip Journal (Beta) for camping and road trips, plus Beebo Auto for the car.
  • Your library and video stay on your own PC. Beebo’s servers handle sign-in and introductions.
  • Family controls enforced by your PC: age ratings, blocked titles, a daily limit, bedtime hours and an owner PIN.

Who should choose what

Choose Beebo if

  • You run Windows 10 or 11 at home.
  • You watch on Android, in a browser, or on Android TV and Google TV.
  • You want away-from-home options built in.
  • You are happy with a newer app that is still growing.

Choose Emby if

  • You need the widest choice of devices today.
  • Your server will be a NAS, Mac or Linux box.
  • You value its maturity, community and long list of features.

Trying Beebo alongside Emby

Beebo does not move or change your files, so you can point it at the same folders Emby uses and try it beside Emby. The current download does not import Emby watch history. An importer is in Beta for the next release. Emby says some of its apps need Premiere, or an app purchase, for full playback, so check that before you compare like for like.

Questions

Is Beebo cheaper than Emby Premiere?

The Beebo app is free to use at home, and Emby’s server is free too. Emby Premiere adds features for a fee. Several of those features are included in Beebo at no charge, but some are still Beta and not in the current download, and Emby is ahead on devices and DVR.

Is Beebo open source?

Not today. An open-source direction is Planned, with no date. Emby has also been closed source since 2018, so neither is open source at the moment.

Does Beebo have anything like Emby Connect?

Beebo gives every household a free address like yourname.beebo.tv and a connection test for away-from-home viewing. Beebo’s servers handle sign-in and introductions, and your library and video stay on your PC.
