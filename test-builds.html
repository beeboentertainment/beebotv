How to read this (true, not flattering)

  • "Passed of those that ran" = passed ÷ (passed + failed). It says how many of the checks we actually ran came out right. It is not how finished the app is.
  • "Not tested yet" is the number of checks that need real hardware, a real network, sound, Cast, a car, or a person, so we did not run them. They are not passes. About 30 of 73 checks fall here on tester71.
  • Many passes are screen-only: the screen opened and showed the right words, but the action behind it was not exercised.
  • Most failures are not confirmed app bugs. A first look (not yet confirmed) suggests many are mistakes in our own test script (it looked for the wrong words or the wrong screen) or sample content missing on a test phone (a sample album and a sample audiobook). One is a real, known gap: joining a Car party without signing in shows no result and no error. Fixing the script and the sample content comes next, then we rerun.
  • tester71 vs tester70: the phone scores are about the same; the two-phone check passed one more check (6 of 9 instead of 5 of 9). Differences this small do not show the app got better or worse.
  • Earlier runs today are not counted: some tester70 runs on the S9+ failed because of our own test-script mistakes (wrong screen size), not the app. We show only the latest clean run per phone.

Every individual check for tester71 (what it proves, what it does not test, and the receipt) is on the full list of 73 checks.

Nothing on this page is a promise that a feature works for everyone. Only the owner marks a feature 100%, and no feature is at 100% on this page.
