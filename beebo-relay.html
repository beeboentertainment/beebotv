Beebo Relay Included with your subscription

Beebo Relay passes your video along when a direct connection isn’t possible. Beebo always tries direct first. Beebo Relay is only used when direct doesn’t work.

How it works

Your computer → Your modem → Beebo Relay → Your phone or browser

Your video is encrypted from end to end (WebRTC DTLS). The relay passes it along but can’t see it.

What it costs

Included with your household away-from-home subscription. No additional Beebo Relay usage charge.

Beebo Relay is included in the CA$3/month household plan (up to 6 people) — there’s no separate wallet, top-up or per-GB usage bill for it. This is not a promise of unlimited bandwidth or a specific video quality.

Fair use

Each account has a monthly Beebo Relay data limit (200 GB a month by default) to keep the service fair for everyone on it.

Turn it on

  1. Choose Beebo Relay

    In the connection test result, press Turn on Beebo Relay. Or go to Settings › Connection › Change option › Turn on Beebo Relay. Turning it on accepts the Beebo Relay terms (version 2026-09-17).

    You can also go to Settings › Your Beebo address, and under Away from home: Relay choose Beebo Relay only.

  2. Check you’re ready

    You need an active Beebo subscription, and the computer must be signed in to Beebo. Each account has a monthly limit (200 GB a month by default).

  3. Test away from home

    Turn Wi-Fi off on your phone so it uses mobile data, then play something.

Turn it off

Go to Settings › Connection › Change option and pick another option. This turns Beebo Relay off for your account.

Your privacy

Your video is encrypted from end to end (WebRTC DTLS). The relay passes it along but can’t see it. We don’t harvest or sell your data.

What we keep

  • How many GB your account used this month, to enforce your monthly limit.
  • Basic connection records, including IP addresses, for up to 7 days, to stop abuse.
  • A count of connections per country each month, with no addresses and not linked to your account, to help decide where to add servers.

A note from Nick

“Beebo is made by a family man, not a data company. I have no interest in what you watch or how you use Beebo. Beebo Relay exists only to get your video past your home internet when a direct connection isn’t possible. Where it runs

Beebo Relay runs on an OVHcloud server in Beauharnois (BHS), Canada. More locations are planned.

My Cloudflare first, then Beebo Relay

Use your free Cloudflare allowance, then Beebo Relay

This uses your own Cloudflare relay (set it up with this guide) while it’s inside Cloudflare’s free allowance: 1,000 GB a month, per Cloudflare account.

  • Once your Cloudflare relay has carried 950 GB that month, new connections that need a relay use Beebo Relay (included with your subscription, no extra usage charge) until the month resets. We stop at 950 GB as a safety margin, because Cloudflare also counts packet overhead.
  • Films already playing finish where they are.
  • You choose the day the month starts in Settings (counted in UTC).

Cloudflare prices were checked on 2026-09-15: $0.05 per GB after the free 1,000 GB (source). Cloudflare may change them.

Cloudflare only

Your video goes through your own Cloudflare account, not Beebo’s servers. Cloudflare bills you directly past its free allowance. See Use your own Cloudflare relay.
