Local-first & private

Your movies stay your movies. Everything lives on your own machine — no cloud middleman, no monthly fee to watch films you already own.

Why it matters

💾 Your source library stays on your PC

Your collection sits on your own PC. Beebo Entertainment catalogs and streams it from there — it’s never copied to anyone else’s servers.

🚫 No lock-in

Plain files, played in a normal player. Your original files stay in your chosen folders. You keep them independently of the Beebo software.

🧾 Your media and software are separate

Your media remains yours. Beebo’s software subscription is $0.99 USD per month after a free 30-day trial; movies and shows are not included.

🕵️ Nothing tracking you

Beebo Auto runs no ads and includes no trackers. See the privacy policy.

FOLLOW THE NUMBERS

Save a title, then find your downloads

  1. Tap the down-arrow on a title to start its download.
  2. Open Downloads along the bottom. Wait until downloading finishes before leaving Wi-Fi.
