Local-first & private

Your movies stay your movies. Your source library stays on your computer. Local viewing has no Beebo software fee; relays, downloads and optional sharing use their own data paths.

Why it matters

💾 Your source library stays on your PC

Your source collection stays in the folders you choose. Beebo can stream it directly, forward a stream through a relay, or save a download to a device you select. Those are different routes; see the privacy details for the information each uses.

🚫 No lock-in

Plain files, played in a normal player. Your original files stay in your chosen folders. You keep them independently of the Beebo software.

🧾 Your media and software are separate

Your original media remains yours. Local playback has no Beebo software fee; picture and sound depend on the file and player. Owner-operated remote access and optional Beebo-managed services have different costs. See the free options and planned paid-service terms. Movies, shows and their distribution rights are not included.

🕵️ No tracking

Beebo Auto runs no ads and includes no trackers. See the privacy policy.

FOLLOW THE NUMBERS

Save a title, then find your downloads

  1. Tap the down-arrow on a title to start its download.
  2. Open Downloads along the bottom. Wait until downloading finishes before leaving Wi-Fi.
