Local-first & private

Your movies stay your movies. Everything lives on your own machine — no cloud middleman, no monthly fee to watch films you already own.

Why it matters

💾 Your files never leave home

Your collection sits on your own PC. Beebo Entertainment catalogs and streams it from there — it’s never copied to anyone else’s servers.

🚫 No lock-in

Plain files, played in a normal player. No proprietary format, nothing that stops working if a service shuts down.

🧾 No subscription to your own movies

You already own the films. There’s no monthly fee just to watch what’s on your own drive.

🕵️ Nothing tracking you

Beebo Auto collects no personal data, runs no ads, and includes no trackers. See the privacy policy.
