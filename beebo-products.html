One company. One set of servers. A few different jobs.

Beebo started as a personal media server. We're building it out into a small family of products that all run on the same infrastructure — so nothing sits idle, and the savings go into keeping prices low instead of into running separate servers for separate products.

Available now

Beebo Entertainment

The core product: turn your own movies, shows, music and photos into a real library, streamed from your Windows PC to your phone, tablet, browser and TV.

  • Local streaming is always free
  • Away-from-home streaming is free with a direct connection, automatic UPnP, or your own relay; Beebo Relay is an optional managed fallback (Beta; paid plans are Planned and not available to buy yet; see pricing)
  • Campsite Mode: zero-internet local party mode with ~40 games
  • Parental controls, household sharing, offline downloads

Beebo Relay

When a direct connection, automatic UPnP, or your own relay isn't possible, Beebo Relay carries your video the rest of the way through our managed relay. Encryption and connection details depend on the selected route. It's an optional fallback, not something you need just to watch away from home.

  • Direct connection, automatic UPnP, and your own relay are all tried first, always — all free
  • Beta; paid plans are Planned and not available to buy yet; see pricing
  • Runs on the same servers as everything else Beebo does

BeeboVPN

A straightforward VPN for your household, run by the same team and the same servers as everything else. It is In development and works in our own tests on our first server, with a kill switch, a connectivity watchdog and a per-program tunnel bypass list. We're keeping it invite-only while we finish billing, testing and polish before it's for sale.

  • Running on our first server (Beauharnois, Canada) for invited tests only; not yet available for purchase
  • Built on the same shared-infrastructure model as Beebo Relay
  • More server locations, chosen by you, as the user base grows enough to add them

Why we can keep this affordable

Most companies run a separate set of servers for every product — one for media relay, another for VPN, another for game hosting — and a lot of that capacity sits unused most of the time. Beebo runs all of its products on the same servers instead, with separate quotas so one product can never slow another down. That efficiency is what lets most households pay nothing for away-from-home access in the first place, and lets us price Beebo Relay by streaming quality instead of by the gigabyte when you do need it — it's the model behind everything else we build.

As a region gets enough households actually paying for away-from-home access, we plan to add server capacity closer to them, so connections stay fast without relying on one central location. This isn't automatic or instant — every new region goes through a real cost-and-reliability check and a human decision before it happens, the same way any responsible infrastructure change should. But the intent is straightforward: grow where people actually are, funded by what they're already paying, not by raising prices to cover empty capacity elsewhere.
