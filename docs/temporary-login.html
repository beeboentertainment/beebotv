ACCOUNT HELP

Given a username and temporary password?

Prepared for the next tester update.

This setup flow has passed local tests. The account-server update and optional Windows 0.1.61-rdtest.6 installer still require publication. The current downloads do not include it yet. Real-device testing remains pending.

You can receive your login before supplying an email. On your first sign-in, you must enter your own email and replace the temporary password before using that account. Existing accounts keep their current sign-in.

For a library profile

  1. The person running the library opens Users → Create a library profile, chooses your username and privately gives you the temporary password.
  2. Open that library’s normal sign-in page in a browser. Use your own username and temporary password.
  3. Enter your email and choose a new private password of at least 12 characters. Save to open the library.
  4. Return to the phone or TV app and sign in using your username and new password. Older apps may require this browser step first.

Your email is saved as unverified contact information. It does not enable email sign-in or password recovery for a library profile. This profile does not create a Beebo subscription or grant owner permissions.

For a Beebo service account

  1. The Beebo owner issues a username and temporary password in the private Owner Console, under Customers & access → Username & temporary password.
  2. Use the account-setup link supplied privately with those details.
  3. Enter your own email and a new private password. Setup cannot claim an email already associated with an existing account or purchase; contact the owner if you already have one.
  4. Once setup is complete, sign in with your email or issued username and your new password. Older clients may accept email only.

The temporary service-account password expires after seven days. Ask the owner to replace an expired invitation. It stops working after setup. Service subscriptions and tester approval are separate; issuing a login does not start a subscription or bypass the tester limit.

Keep your login private

Do not put passwords in public tester reports, screenshots or chat. Entering an email is different from proving that you own its mailbox. The setup flow does not label an address verified just because it was entered.

If you cannot finish setup, tell the owner which app/build you use and the error text. Leave your password out of the report.
