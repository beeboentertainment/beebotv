ACCOUNT HELP

Set up your temporary Beebo login

Beta: service accounts. In development: library profiles.

Status as of October 5, 2026. The Beebo service-account setup below is part of Beebo’s account server (updated October 4). It has passed our automatic tests, but no tester has used it on a real device yet. The library-profile setup needs a Windows update that is not in a download yet: Windows 0.1.79 does not include it. The older 0.1.61-rdtest.6 test installer mentioned here before is not offered.

You can get a login before you give anyone your email. The first time you sign in, you must enter your own email and replace the temporary password. Only then can you use the account. Existing accounts keep their current sign-in.

For a library profile

  1. The person running the library opens Users → Create a library profile, chooses your username and privately gives you the temporary password.
  2. Open that library’s normal sign-in page in a web browser. Sign in with your username and the temporary password.
  3. Enter your email and choose a new private password of at least 12 characters. Save, and the library opens.
  4. Go back to the phone or TV app and sign in with your username and new password. Older apps may need this browser step first.

Your email is saved as unverified contact information. It does not enable email sign-in or password recovery for a library profile. This profile does not create a Beebo subscription or grant owner permissions.

For a Beebo service account

  1. The Beebo owner issues a username and temporary password in the private Owner Console, under Customers & access → Username & temporary password.
  2. Open the account-setup link the owner sent you privately with those details.
  3. Enter your own email and a new private password. Setup will not accept an email that already belongs to an account or purchase. If you already have one, contact the owner.
  4. Once setup is complete, sign in with your email or the username you were given, and your new password. Older apps may accept only your email.

The temporary service-account password expires after seven days. If it expires, ask the owner for a new one. The temporary password also stops working once setup is done. Service subscriptions and tester approval are separate; issuing a login does not start a subscription or bypass the tester limit.

Keep your login private

Do not put passwords in public tester reports, screenshots or chat. Entering an email is different from proving that you own its mailbox. The setup flow does not label an address verified just because it was entered.

If you cannot finish setup, tell the owner which app and version you use, and the exact error message. Leave your password out of the report.
