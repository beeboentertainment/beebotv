Privacy in plain words

Your library and video don’t go to Beebo. Beebo’s servers handle sign-in and introductions, and a few optional features talk to other services, which this page lists. It is a plain-language summary and not a legal document: the privacy policy has the formal terms.

The short version: your library and video don’t go to Beebo. Beebo’s servers handle sign-in and introductions. The details are in the privacy policy.

What stays on your PC

  • Your movies, shows and music, and their file names.
  • Who has watched what: watch history, viewing profiles and parental settings.
  • Photo backups from your phone.
  • Saved secrets such as your TMDB key, OpenSubtitles password and email app password. They are encrypted using the PC’s own keystore.
  • If you use a relay, it passes along locked packets. Beebo Relay cannot read your video.

What Beebo’s service handles

Beebo runs a small service (beebo.tv) that does the introductions. It handles your Beebo account email and sign-in, your yourname.beebo.tv name, and setting up the connection between your phone and your PC. That includes connection details such as internet (IP) addresses, and, for away-from-home sign-in, your household members’ usernames with scrambled passes rather than plain passwords. It also handles subscriptions and payments for the paid options. Video and your library go straight between your devices, or through a relay if you chose one; they do not go through this service.

The Beebo Android app and desktop app contain no advertising or third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking components that we know of. The Android app does include Google’s own casting and Play components, which are closed-source, so we cannot describe everything they do.

Check it yourself

  1. Pick a place to watch the traffic

    Use your router’s DNS log, a Pi-hole, or on the Windows PC a network monitor such as Wireshark or the netstat -b command. On Android, a tool such as PCAPdroid works.

  2. Use Beebo normally for a while

    Sign in, play something and open a few pages. For a clean test, leave the TMDB key, OpenSubtitles account and email settings empty.

  3. Compare the names you see

    With those left empty, we expect a Beebo PC to talk mainly to login.beebo.tv, your yourname.beebo.tv address, relay1.beebo.tv and beeboentertainment.com (for update checks), plus the place that hosts the installer download. Names from the table above appear as you use those features. If you see something else, tell us at privacy@beeboentertainment.com.
