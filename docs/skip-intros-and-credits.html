Automatic intro and credits skipping

Beebo can find where a show’s opening titles start and end, and where the end credits begin, entirely on your own PC. The web player then shows a Skip intro button while the intro is playing.

Beta Status: Detection and the web player’s Skip intro button are in Beebo for Windows (Beta, since 0.1.58). It has been tested on generated audio and sample video and only lightly on real libraries. The Android app does not read the new markers yet.

How detection works

  • Intros. An opening title sequence is the same sound in every episode of a season. Beebo compares the first ten minutes or so of the episodes with each other and keeps the stretch that at least two other episodes confirm. It does not need to “recognise” the music. The intro must be between 15 seconds and 3 minutes long. A scene that plays before the opening titles (a “cold open”) is fine.
  • Credits. Only the end of each file is examined (roughly the last 12 minutes, or the last fifth of a short item). Credits are marked where the picture goes dark and stays mostly dark, and the episodes of a season must agree about how far from the end that is. Films are held to a stricter bar.
  • Local only. It uses Beebo’s own built-in video tool (ffmpeg), on your PC. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is looked up online.
  • Polite. It works on one file at a time (at most two), at low priority. It waits while anyone is watching or while a video is being converted. Results are remembered, so a restart does not redo them. If Beebo is not sure, it shows nothing.
  • You stay in charge. Anything marked by hand always wins over what Beebo finds. If you clear a show’s markers, they stay cleared.

Step by step

  1. Check it is on

    On the Beebo PC, open Settings, then Playback, and find Skip intros and credits. The box Detect intros and credits automatically is ticked by default. Untick it any time to stop detection.

    What you’ll see: A tick in the box. A progress tile also appears on the Dashboard while Beebo scans.

    Screenshot coming. The Skip intros and credits box in Settings.

  2. Let it scan

    Leave Beebo running. Scanning happens in the background while nobody is watching, so a big library can take a while to finish.

  3. Use Skip intro in the web player

    Play an episode in a browser. While the detected intro is playing, a Skip intro button appears.

    What you’ll see: The button only appears while the detected intro is playing. It does not automatically jump to the next episode when it finds credits by itself.

  4. Fix or redo a show

    In a browser, open your Beebo server’s Admin page and go to the Markers tab. Next to a show, click Re-scan intro/credits to look again, or Clear auto markers to remove what Beebo found.

What it cannot do

  • It works well on shows with several episodes of a season in your library, all sharing the same opening. A single episode, or a show whose intro changes every week, may get nothing.
  • If Beebo is not sure about a mark, it hides it instead of guessing.
  • It has been tested on generated audio and sample video, and only lightly on real libraries, so treat it as a beta.
  • The Android app and TV apps do not show the new markers yet.

If something goes wrong

There is no Skip intro button

The show may not be scanned yet, Beebo may not have been sure enough about the intro, or you are using an app that does not read markers yet. Only the web player shows the button so far.

The button skips to the wrong place

On the Admin page, open the Markers tab and use Clear auto markers or Re-scan intro/credits for that show, and tell us about it at testers@beeboentertainment.com.

Does scanning slow down my PC?

It runs at low priority, one file at a time, and pauses while anyone is watching. You can switch it off in Settings.
