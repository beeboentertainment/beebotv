Trip Journal: a recap of your trip, as a slideshow or video

Tap “We’re heading out” when you leave and “We’re home” when you return. Beebo collects that stretch’s games, stories, badges and packing list into one recap you can present as a slideshow or save as an MP4 video. It all stays on your phone.

Beta Status: The Trip Journal is in the Android test download (Android only; checked on 1.51-tester11). It is new, and it has not been tested on many different phones. Saving as a video is not offered on a TV. A private share link, served from your own PC, needs Android 1.39 or later and Windows 0.1.58 or later on the PC.

Step by step

  1. Start a trip

    On the Campsite Mode screen, find the Trip journal card and tap We’re heading out. Give the trip a name (it starts as “Our trip”). If you like, list who is coming, with commas between the names. Tap Start trip.

    What you’ll see: The card changes to “On a trip: …” and says that games, stories and scavenger hunt finds from now are kept for the recap.

    Screenshot coming. The Trip journal card on the Campsite Mode screen.

  2. Enjoy the trip

    Play, tell campfire stories and pack as usual. You can add more names from the card with Add a name. Names are whatever people typed on their phones; nothing checks who they are.

    If you want, turn on Save hunt locations on the card. With it off, a scavenger hunt keeps only what was found and who found it. With it on, each found waypoint’s coordinates are kept on this phone too, and turning it off erases them.

  3. End the trip

    When you are back, tap We’re home.

    What you’ll see: The trip is saved as a recap on this phone.

  4. Open the recap and pick photos

    Open the Play tab, then Camp & outdoors, and tap Trip Recap (under Trips & memories). Choose the trip, then tap Choose photos and videos to add the ones you want in the show. Photos and videos dated outside the trip are left out unless you switch on Include ones dated outside the trip.

    What you’ll see: A count such as “12 photos or videos in the show”.

  5. Present it

    Tap Present to run the recap as a slideshow. Swipe to move between slides.

    What you’ll see: Full-screen slides with a counter like “3 / 14”.

  6. Save it as a video

    Tap Save as a video, then:

    1. Under Picture size, choose 1080p or 720p (smaller).
    2. Under Sound, choose whether to tick Add campfire crackle. It is off by default, so the video is silent.
    3. Under Names on the video, guests appear as “a friend” unless you tick their names.
    4. Tap Make video.

    Beebo makes the video in the background. This takes about as long as the video itself, so keep the phone charging; it may get warm. You can leave the screen.

    When it finishes, tap Share video.

    What you’ll see: “Making the video… 40%”, then the length and size of the finished file.

    Screenshot coming. The save-as-video screen with quality and crackle options.

Privacy

  • The video is made on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. Photos are redrawn from their pixels and the finished file is scrubbed of any GPS location, so the video carries no location.
  • A video can be sent to anyone, so guests appear as “guest” unless you choose to show their names.
  • The crackle is made on your phone. Music from your library is never used, and the sound in your own clips is left out.
  • Deleting a trip removes its recap from your phone. Your photos, videos and game history are not touched.

If something goes wrong

Making the video fails or gets stuck

Choose 720p, which is smaller and works on more phones, keep the phone charging and free of low-storage warnings, and try again. The Trip Journal is a beta and has not been tested on many phones. Tell us your phone model at testers@beeboentertainment.com.

Some photos or clips are missing

The saved video skips items that are unreadable, too short, or videos that are too long. The finished screen says how many were left out. Photos dated outside the trip are also excluded unless you include them.

A photo “can’t be opened any more”

Choose it again from the trip screen. The phone can no longer open that file.

I do not see Trip Journal at all

It is in Android 1.39 and newer. Update the Android app. Check What’s new.
