Answers about using Beebo

Find out how to watch your own library, connect your devices and manage your household. Each answer points to more detail and says when a feature still needs testing.

QuestionsRelated pages

Questions

Can I use my phone to move my computer’s mouse with Beebo?

Not yet as a finished feature. A first version, called Remote Desktop, is inside Beebo for Windows (0.1.58 and later). It is switched off by default and has not been tested on a real phone yet. A simple touchpad-only version is planned. Phone as mouse and keyboard (In development).

Can guests' phones be my movie speakers?

Yes, as a Beta. Beebo has a Phone speakers feature. The big screen shows the film and each guest phone plays one channel of its sound, such as front left, front right, centre or surround. Guests join by QR code over local Wi-Fi with no app and no account. So far it has been tested on one computer and in simulation, not yet on real phones over real Wi-Fi. Phone speakers (Beta).

What is Beebo?

Beebo lets you watch movies, shows and home videos you already have on compatible phones, tablets, browsers and TVs. It builds a library from folders you choose on a Windows 10 or 11 PC. No movies or shows are included.

Does Beebo cost anything?

Beebo Entertainment is free to use at home. Away-from-home access is also free with a direct connection, automatic UPnP, or your own relay, at any resolution. Beebo Relay, Beebo's own relay servers, is an optional paid fallback for households where none of those work: planned CA$3/month for two simultaneous managed streams or CA$5/month for four; final billing, quality and household limits await verification. No payment card required to get started.

Does my PC have to stay on?

Yes. Your library is served from your PC, so it has to be on, awake and running Beebo whenever someone watches, at home or away. See Fix connection problems for keeping it awake.

Do I need a TMDB key?

You do not need one to play your files, but posters, cast photos and descriptions come from TMDB, and Beebo asks you to use your own free key. It takes about three minutes; the steps are in Get started on Windows.

Which devices can I watch on?

The Android app on phones and tablets (Available, as a test download) and any web browser, including Safari on iPhone and iPad (Available). The Android app on Android TV and Google TV, and casting to a Chromecast or Google TV, are Beta. Fire TV, Roku and LG have Beta tester builds you install yourself; Samsung, Xbox and native Apple TV apps are in development, with no download yet. None of the TV apps has been tested on a real TV yet. See Playing on TVs and Beebo on your devices.

Does Beebo work for a home theater?

Partly, and we say which part. Android TV and Google TV, and casting, are Beta. Video conversion with your PC’s graphics card and Live TV are Beta in Beebo for Windows; Linux and Docker servers are Beta but not offered for download right now. Tester builds for Fire TV, Roku and LG are Beta and not tested on real TVs. Passthrough of HDR10+, Dolby Vision, Atmos and DTS:X is in development, so Beebo does not claim it. See Home theater and big screen.

Is there an iPhone or iPad app?

Not yet. A native iPhone and iPad app (in development) is not published, has never been tested on a real iPhone or iPad, and needs Apple’s approval. iPhone and iPad watch in Safari today, and you can add Beebo to the Home Screen. Offline downloads need the Android app.

Can I run Beebo on Linux or a NAS?

Both are betas. The Linux desktop build (an AppImage and a .deb, tested on Ubuntu 22.04 in our automated build only) is a beta that is not offered for download right now. A headless Docker image for Linux servers and NAS boxes is a beta that is not offered for download right now. We have not tested any specific NAS model, and Linux has no automatic updates yet. Both were taken down on October 4, 2026 because they were many versions behind the current Windows release. Linux has not been dropped and the app is not being rewritten; newer builds will be posted when they have been built and checked, with no date yet. See Run Beebo on a NAS or Linux server and the Linux page.

How do I watch away from home?

Open your free yourname.beebo.tv address, or type your home’s name in the Android app, and sign in. Beebo tries a direct connection to your PC first. Away from home: your three options explains direct, your own relay and Beebo Relay.

Does my library get uploaded to Beebo?

No. Your library and video don’t go to Beebo. Beebo’s servers handle sign-in and introductions, meaning finding your PC and setting up the connection. Some optional features talk to other services, and each is listed in Privacy in plain words. The privacy policy has the details.

What household controls can adults set?

Beebo is not a children’s app and has no child-themed screens. Adults who run the PC can set age ratings, blocked genres and titles, a daily limit, bedtime hours and an owner PIN. The PC enforces these controls. See Family and profiles.

Can I download movies to my phone?

Yes, on the Android app. Save a title while you are on Wi-Fi. Later, open Downloads to watch it with no signal. Downloads are not available in a browser.

Does Beebo do Live TV, DVR or plug-ins?

Live TV and DVR are Beta with your own HDHomeRun tuner only, and have not been tested on a real tuner. Adding your own M3U/IPTV playlist is in Beta too (in Beebo for Windows since 0.1.72). Beebo supplies no channels or guide data. A small read-only API with signed webhooks is in Beta too, and there is no plug-in ecosystem. Other media servers are ahead on those, and on how many devices they support. How Beebo compares says where.

How do I update Beebo?

Beebo for Windows checks for a newer version when it starts. You can also check yourself: open Settings, click Help & updates, open App updates and click Check for updates. To update the Android app, download the newer file from the Get Beebo section and install it over the old one. Your settings are kept. What’s new lists releases.

How do I back up my Beebo settings?

Open Settings, click Security & backups, then open Backup & restore. A backup contains none of your movie files. See Back up and restore.

How do I delete my account?

Follow Delete your Beebo Entertainment account. Your movies and shows never leave your computer, so Beebo never had a copy.
