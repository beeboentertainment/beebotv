Themes and appearance

The Appearance page lets each person choose how Beebo looks in the browser: dark or light, cool or warm. The choice belongs to your account, follows you to every device you sign in on, and never changes anyone else’s screen.

Available now — Beta (Windows 0.1.59) Status: Themes are in Windows 0.1.59 (Beta), together with layout packs and screens in English, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Italian for the main screens. The Appearance page appears after the owner updates the Beebo PC to 0.1.58 or newer. The Windows app itself now has its own Settings › Appearance page too — see What is themed where below.

Step by step

  1. Open Beebo in a browser and sign in

    Use your own login. At home that is the address from the PC’s Get Started screen; anywhere else it is your yourname.beebo.tv address.

  2. Open Appearance

    Click Appearance in the navigation at the top of the page.

    What you’ll see: A page headed Appearance that says “Choose how Beebo looks on this account. It applies on every device you sign in to, and other people keep their own.”

    Screenshot coming. The Appearance page with the four themes and their colour swatches.

  3. Pick a theme

    Choose a theme with the round buttons. The page changes colour straight away as a preview, before you save.

    What you’ll see: A preview in the theme’s colours, with a swatch strip beside each name.

  4. Save

    Click Save appearance.

    What you’ll see: The page reloads in your theme. If a problem is found, the page lists it and keeps your old theme.

  5. Optional: adjust a few colours

    Under Custom overrides, type one setting per line, like --gold: #ff8800;. It is applied on top of your theme. Only Beebo’s known colour, gradient and corner-size settings are accepted, and a combination that would make text hard to read is refused. The Variables you can set list on the page shows every name.

    What you’ll see: A message listing any line Beebo did not accept. Nothing is saved if any line is refused.

The four themes

ThemeStyleNotes
MidnightDarkThe original Beebo look: deep navy with purple and gold. It is the default, and what signed-out pages such as the sign-in screen use.
GraphiteDarkNeutral charcoal greys with a quiet steel-blue accent.
DaylightLightNear-black text on white and pale grey, with deep violet accents. Built for contrast.
EmberDarkCharcoal-brown surfaces with copper-orange highlights.

What is themed where, and what is not themed yet

This page’s four themes (Midnight, Graphite, Daylight, Ember) are for the browser site: this Appearance page and the rest of Beebo when you open it in a web browser, on any device.

Available now — Beta (Windows 0.1.59) The Beebo Windows app now has its own colour picker too, in Settings › Appearance inside the app. It is a separate picker from this page, with its own four choices — Dark, Light, High contrast and OLED black — not the same four names as here, though Dark and Light use the same colours as Graphite and Daylight. It changes the colours you see in the app itself (library, sidebar, cards and so on), alongside layout choices such as density and card style; it does not yet change the small number of places noted below.

  • The video player page has its own look, and a few standalone pages (Suggest a Feature, and some admin and print pages) keep theirs in every theme.
  • Some status boxes use fixed colours that do not change with a theme.
  • The Android app has its own colours and no theme picker yet.

If something goes wrong

I saved colours I cannot read

At the bottom of the Appearance page, the Reset to default box always uses fixed colours, so it stays readable whatever you saved. Its button puts you back on Midnight and removes every custom override. If the page itself is hard to read, open /appearance?safe=1 on your Beebo address, which shows the page in the default colours.

The Appearance link is missing

Themes are in Windows 0.1.58 and newer. Ask whoever runs your Beebo PC whether it has been updated, or check What’s new. Looking for the Windows app’s own theme picker instead of this page? That’s Settings › Appearance inside the app.

Does my choice change my family’s screens?

No. It is stored per person, so one member’s taste never changes another’s.
