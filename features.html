What you can do

One home for your media.
A few ways to enjoy it.

01 / Settle in

Your own library.

Organise your movies and shows, find something to watch, and pick up where you left off. Beebo also brings together your music, photos, audiobooks and podcasts.

You choose the files and folders. There’s no bundled movie catalogue. Features vary by player, and there are known issues; the testing page records the detail.

AvailableWindows, Android phone & browser

02 / A little further afield

Take your library
with you.

Connect back to your home PC over the internet without manually forwarding ports. Beebo tries a direct connection between your devices.

The PC must stay on. Your home upload speed and the connection where you’re watching affect playback. Beebo Relay is a Beta fallback when a direct route isn’t available; it isn’t available to buy.

Understand the connection →
03 / Switch off a little

Campsite Mode.

Beta

Games and stories for time away from the internet. Solo and pass-and-play activities can work with no Wi-Fi at all. For a group, nearby guests join in a browser over your shared local Wi-Fi or hotspot.

Try original games such as Drop Four, with Beebo as the computer opponent. Prepare local items before going offline; activities that fetch new content still need internet.

Group joining, sound and a full trip are separate checks. A working solo game doesn’t prove every group activity.

04 / Keep the good bits

Trip Journal.

Beta

Gather photos and moments from your trip in the Android app. Make a recap and decide what you share.

Journal work stays local unless you choose to share it. Recaps, slideshows and exports still have testing gaps. Video export isn’t offered on TVs.

05 / On the road

Beebo Auto.

Available

A separate Android Auto companion, installed by sideloading. It is not on Google Play and has never been tested in a real car.

Set up while parked. Drivers must keep their attention on the road; passenger video belongs on passenger devices.
