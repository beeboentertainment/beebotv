Built, tested and available are different facts.

A ✓ pale-green check means the stated local checks passed and device testing remains. A ✓ dark-green check needs a recorded real test for that exact scope. Yellow ~ means unfinished or limited work; red × means not offered.

Most entries below identify built modules reviewed in the code and say verification pending where this review did not establish a matching test result. This does not mean they are known to be broken, and it does not claim they have passed. The appearance section names its completed local checks.

Understand the cost before choosing a route.

$ Included: all Beebo Entertainment software features for organizing and watching your own media, at home or through your own compatible connection or relay. There is no Beebo relay fee when you supply the route. You supply the computer, storage, internet connection and media you are entitled to use.

$ Separate service: Beebo-managed remote connectivity, VPN or future hosting may have paid plans or a clearly stated introductory offer. External content, tuner hardware and third-party accounts can have their own charges. A paid service does not make an unavailable feature available.

Use the feature-by-feature comparison and published plans and launch terms for exact paid conditions. Free owner-operated routes and paid Beebo-hosted capacity are different products.

Beebo checks whether a player can use the original file, whether repackaging is enough, or whether conversion is needed. A compatible original or copied video stream avoids video re-encoding.
