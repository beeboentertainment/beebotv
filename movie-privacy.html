Beebo Entertainment — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 August 2026

Beebo Entertainment (com.beeboentertainment.movie) is a personal media player: it connects to your own home media server and streams the movies and TV shows you already own to your phone. This policy explains, honestly and completely, what the app does with your information.

In short: everything the app handles stays between your phone and your own server. Nothing is sent to the developer, to Google, or to any third party. There are no ads, no analytics, and no trackers, and nothing is ever sold or shared.

What the app is

Beebo Entertainment is a client for a media server that runs on your own computer. Your video files live on that computer. The app catalogs them, shows posters and details, and plays them by streaming directly between your phone and your server.

What the app stores, and why

  • Your server address — so the app knows where your library is.
  • Your access code (login) — kept on your device so you stay signed in to your own server. It is used only to authenticate to that server.
  • Viewing progress — where you left off in a title ("continue watching"), so playback resumes. This is kept on your device and your server.
  • Downloaded videos — if you choose to download a title for offline viewing, the file is stored on your device until you delete it.

That's all. The app does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any advertising identifier.

Where your data goes

Video and the items above travel only between your phone and your own server, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. The developer of this app receives none of it and operates no servers that your data passes through.

Ads, analytics, and third parties

There are none. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs or trackers. Nothing about your use of the app is measured or reported to anyone.

Content

Beebo Entertainment ships with no media of its own. It plays only the content you provide from your own server. You are responsible for the media in your library.

Children

This is a general-purpose personal utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Data retention and deletion

On-device data (your saved login, viewing progress, and any downloads) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Anything stored on your own server is under your control on that server.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
