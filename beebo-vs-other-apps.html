We've left the other names off the table to keep it simple to scan — see "Sources" at the bottom for exactly who's who and where each fact came from.

Checked September 21, 2026. Prices, features and policies change — check the linked source before making a decision, and treat anything below as a snapshot, not a permanent fact.

Beebo Relay and BeeboVPN, honestly, as of today: both are live and genuinely working, running on our first server in Beauharnois, Canada. Beebo Relay is included with every Beebo Entertainment subscription, no extra usage charge. BeeboVPN is live too, but currently as an invite-only test on that same single server while we finish billing and polish before opening it up publicly. We plan to add server capacity in a region once enough households there actually need it — that's a real cost-and-reliability decision each time, not an automatic or instant process — and once there's more than one location, you'll be able to choose which one you connect to.

Where they're still ahead of us, honestly: all three of the others run on far more platforms today (Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, smart TVs, game consoles) — Beebo is Windows-server-plus-Android/browser right now, with a native iPhone app planned but not shipped. All three also have mature Live TV/DVR support and a large ecosystem of third-party automation add-ons (auto-acquisition tools, watch-history dashboards) that Beebo doesn't have built in yet. One of the three has no subscription of any kind and a large, active third-party client and plugin community. If any of those matter more to you than away-from-home streaming, a household VPN, or game hosting, one of them may suit you better today.

We're a small, newer company. Our bet is that most households care more about "does it just work when I'm away from home, and can it grow with my family" than about the long tail of automation plugins — and that we can keep prices honest by running Beebo Relay, BeeboVPN and Beebo Play on the same shared servers instead of three separate bills' worth of infrastructure. See our products page for the full picture of what that looks like today versus what's still coming.
