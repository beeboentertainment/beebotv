AN HONEST, ANONYMOUS COMPARISON

Choose the service that fits your home.

Beebo is newer than three established personal-media services. This page explains what each type of service is good at, where Beebo is different, and what Beebo is still building.

Beebo release status, plainly: A pale-green tick means that feature is already built in Beebo's current release candidate. Testing verifies it on real devices; it does not mean the feature still needs to be built before we call it fully released. A gold mark means the feature is still being built or is in beta. We do not label either state as fully released until the final checks pass.
Why no company names? This is designed to help you compare approaches, not to borrow another company’s reputation. Features, devices and prices change often, so always check the provider you are considering before purchasing. This page was reviewed September 22, 2026.

Where Beebo is focused

  • A friendly Windows-first home library for media you already own.
  • Household setup, phone connection, trips, campsite tools and shared games in one experience.
  • Clear labels for features that are ready, in beta or still in development.
  • Simple local setup first, with away-from-home options designed to be understandable.

Where established services are ahead

  • More years of real-world testing and larger support communities.
  • More mature apps across television, Apple, Linux, NAS and other platforms.
  • Broader plug-in ecosystems and integrations.
  • More established live-TV, DVR, music and home-theater support.

What costs money — and what does not

Beebo at home: Your personal library, playback on your own home network, household profiles, and local Campsite activities are free. Beebo self-hosted away from home: if you use your own port forwarding, UPnP, relay, or VPN, Beebo does not charge a per-stream fee or impose a Beebo household stream limit. Your computer, upload speed, electricity, and any service you choose remain your responsibility.

Beebo-managed away-from-home: In development — expected soon, pending final security and real-device testing. The planned CA$3 household option includes up to 2 simultaneous managed remote streams. The planned CA$5 option includes up to 4. These limits apply only while Beebo supplies the managed connection. Beebo VPN: In development — expected soon, pending final security and real-device testing. Planned at CA$2 per household, with up to 4 managed VPN connections.

Other services: “available” does not always mean “free.” A feature can require a subscription, one-time mobile unlock, supported hardware, a tuner, app-store purchase, or the customer’s own server. The table below calls out those differences.
