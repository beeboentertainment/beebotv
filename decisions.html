Open and honest

Decisions made

I’m Nick. Beebo is made by a family man, not a data company, and I would rather show you how I make choices than keep them to myself.

Below are my recorded answers to D1–D9. Each is marked DECIDED, with the options kept for context. A decision being made does not mean a feature is finished, shipped or released.

Nothing here is a promise. A question being on this page does not mean a feature is coming. Features named here are not released unless it says they are. Ideas such as the picture frame, Family moments, Family achievements, the home intercom, the time capsule, the home dashboard and the phone touchpad are Planned or In development, and none of them is in any download. The full list with honest labels is on what we offer.

These are my recorded decisions. The choices cannot be changed here. The buttons copy the recorded answers; nothing is sent anywhere and no cookies are used. If you want to tell us what you think, the tester chat is the place.

D3DECIDED · ALinux server (headless, Docker): Beta

How we word the Linux server status

The Linux server is listed as Beta. Its code is done and its automated tests pass. A recent test on real Linux (Ubuntu) ran it from first setup through video conversion and found one fault; a fix is written but is not in any download yet. A virtual ARM run also worked. It has not been tested on a Raspberry Pi or on a real NAS, and we will not say that it has.

DECIDED: A. Tested on real Linux (Ubuntu in WSL) and an emulated ARM run on Oct 21, 2026; one fault found and fixed (video conversion on a Linux ffmpeg without OpenH264). Not yet tested on a Raspberry Pi, a real NAS, or the Docker image.

Options for D3: How we word the Linux server status
D3: A

D5DECIDED · APicture frame: Planned, not released

Picture frame over plain HTTP

The picture frame is an idea that is not released. An old tablet or TV on the wall would show photos you picked, from your home PC. In a first version it would talk to your PC over your home Wi-Fi without encryption (plain HTTP). The link is meant to work only on your home network. The trade-off is that someone on that same Wi-Fi who had the link could see the chosen photos.

DECIDED: A. Accept plain HTTP for v1 and say so plainly in the guide.

Options for D5: Picture frame over plain HTTP
D5: A
