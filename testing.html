Reading the record

Different checks.
Different kinds of proof.

What does an automated check prove?

It checks a particular piece of code with the inputs in that test. It can find mistakes without proving that an installed app works on a phone, a television or a car.

What does an emulator check prove?

An emulator imitates a device on a computer. It can check screens and some behavior, but it cannot stand in for every real device, sound system, network or remote control.

What does a real-device check prove?

The stated result on the stated device and build. Some checks were performed by an AI tester on real hardware; others involved Nick. Muted tests do not prove sound. A short screen check does not prove a complete journey.

Why can a feature have checkmarks and open issues?

A past check may have passed while another part failed later. We retain the original record and keep the known problem visible. Only Nick records final owner acceptance, and that acceptance stays within its recorded scope.

Is Beebo Auto tested in a real car?

No. Beebo Auto is available as a separate sideload, but it has never been tested in a real car. The example above covers its phone settings on an emulator. It does not prove car controls, sound or driving behavior.
