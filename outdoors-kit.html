Camping, hiking and road-trip extras

Explore small offline activities for hikes, campfires and road trips. They are in the Android test download as a Beta, but no real-phone test of them is recorded and nobody has tried them on a trip yet. The designs do not require an account or a network connection.

Testers wanted — posted Sep 21, 2026, 4:46 PM Eastern. We need testers on real phones and on a real trail. Real feedback earns a lifetime streaming pass. Help test Beebo on Android →

Beta Status: everything on this page is in the Android test download (1.51-tester11) and Riddle Road is in the Beebo Auto test download, and it passes our automated tests. No real-phone test of it is recorded, and nobody has tried it on a trail, at a campsite or in a car.

Trail Kit (on a hike)

Hike tracker

  • A timer with trail stamps at set times and, if you allow it, step counts.
  • Water and snack reminders shown on screen only, and rotating trail-leader and photographer roles.
  • Step counting is off by default and uses the phone’s own step counter.

Sun and moon

  • Sunrise, sunset, golden hour, first and last light, and a “be back before dark” planner.
  • The Moon’s phase and the next full and new moons.
  • Calculated on the phone using a location you enter. No network call.

Nature bingo and Quiet ears

  • A card matched to the season and the kind of place, made only of things to look for or listen for.
  • A timed listening game. The microphone is not used and nothing is recorded.

Journal, lists and badges

  • A page per walk that can also go into your Trip Journal.
  • Tick-off lists: before you go, ten essentials, and care for the trail.
  • Twelve keepsake badges with no streaks or ranking.

Campfire and camp games

Story Stew

A fill-in-the-blanks story game with twelve stories (cozy, silly and adventure). Players take turns adding a word, and nobody sees the story until the last blank is filled. It works on one phone or with guests on the campsite Wi-Fi.

Campfire Cards

Riddles, jokes and say-it-fast phrases in three age bands. The answer is only sent to phones when the leader taps “Show the answer”.

Night Sky Cards

Fourteen cards with a “find it tonight” tip, plus the Moon tonight. The moon is approximate and can be off by about half a day.

Rainy-day box and Camp Jobs

Thirty-three quick activities for the tent, the car and rest stops, and a fair chore wheel with a star chart. Names stay on the phone.

In the car

Riddle Road (Beebo Auto)

A voice game for passengers: riddles, jokes and say-it-fast phrases read aloud, using the same cards as the phone app. It is for passengers only and never for the driver.

What these are not, and what is not tested

  • Trail Kit is not a map, not navigation and not a safety device, and it cannot call for help. Do not rely on it for safety.
  • Nothing here has been tried on real phones, on a trail, in a phone browser at a campsite, or in a car. Text size, glare, screen readers and hotspot use are unknown.
  • The riddles, jokes and stories are new and have not had an outside review.
  • The moon phase is good to a few hours. There are no moonrise or moonset times and no star maps.
  • These are written for families and groups of all ages. Parents or adults run them. Beebo is not a children’s product.

Questions

Can I use these today?

Yes, as a Beta. They are in the Android test download, but they are only tested by machine so far, and no real-phone test of them is recorded.

Do they need the internet or an account?

No. They are designed to work with no signal and no account, and they send nothing anywhere.

Is Trail Kit a map or a safety device?

No. It is not a map, not navigation and not a safety device, and it cannot call for help.

Where do I say what I would like added?

Tell us on the testers page. Real feedback from a real trip is the most useful thing you can give us.
