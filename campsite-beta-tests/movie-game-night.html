Test: Movie Night and Game Night

Two features on one sheet: a film quiz and vote on your TV with phones as controllers, and a host panel that keeps score across a run of Campsite games. About 50 minutes in total; do the parts you can.

Status: In development, open for testing.

Movie Night on Windows is already Beta (see the Movie Night page). Game Night, and Movie Night in the Android app, are in development and open for testing.

Nothing here has been run on a wide range of TVs and phones yet. That is what this test is for.

What you need

  • The Windows PC running Beebo (0.1.79 or newer), signed in.
  • A TV with a browser, or the PC plugged into the TV.
  • Two phones: one Android with the Beebo test app, and one with any browser (an iPhone if you can borrow one).
  • Everything on the same home Wi-Fi, not guest Wi-Fi.

Your ticks are saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to report.

0 passed, 0 failed, 41 steps

Part A: Movie Night on the TV (about 20 minutes)

  1. Step A1

    In Beebo on the PC, open a film. Press Start Movie Night. Choose Show it on this computer.

    You should see: A window opens with a big QR code and a short room code.

  2. Step A2

    Second run: on the TV browser open the /tv page of your PC. The Movie Night window shows the exact address to type.

    You should see: The TV shows a QR code and a room code.

  3. Step A3

    Phone 1: scan the QR with the camera.

    You should see: A page asks for a nickname and a colour.

  4. Step A4

    Pick a name and a colour. Join.

    You should see: Your name and colour appear on the TV.

  5. Step A5

    Phone 2: join with the room code only.

    You should see: A second name appears on the TV.

  6. Step A6

    Try a name with a web address in it.

    You should see: It is refused.

  7. Step A7

    Try the same name as phone 1.

    You should see: It is refused or changed.

  8. Step A8

    On the TV start Blurry Poster with 3 rounds.

    You should see: The poster gets clearer. Phones show 4 choices.

  9. Step A9

    Answer on both phones.

    You should see: After the question the TV shows the answer, who got it and the points.

  10. Step A10

    Play to the end.

    You should see: A final scoreboard shows both players. Ties share a rank.

  11. Step A11

    Turn teams on (2 teams). Play one question.

    You should see: A team score shows.

  12. Step A12

    Phone 1 (the first guest) taps Pause, then Resume.

    You should see: The game pauses and resumes.

  13. Step A13

    While paused, try to answer.

    You should see: Answers are refused.

  14. Step A14

    Run Pick Tonight's Movie. Tap Yes on some films, one Veto, Send.

    You should see: The TV shows a winner and says which rule decided it.

  15. Step A15

    Press Play it. Tap an emoji on a phone.

    You should see: The film starts. A small emoji appears on the film.

  16. Step A16

    From the host phone remove phone 2.

    You should see: Phone 2 is out. It cannot rejoin with the same name.

  17. Step A17

    Lock the room. Try to join with a third device.

    You should see: Join is refused.

  18. Step A18

    Lock phone 1's screen for 30 seconds, then unlock.

    You should see: The phone rejoins by itself.

  19. Step A19

    Press End the night.

    You should see: TV and phones say it is over.

  20. Step A20

    Read the TV from your sofa.

    You should see: Text is easy to read. The QR scans from where people sit.

  21. Step A21

    Try the iPhone (Safari) for steps A3 to A9.

    You should see: It works.

Part C: Game Night on two Android phones (about 20 minutes)

Phone 1 is the host. Phone 2 is a guest (browser).

  1. Step C1

    Phone 1: open Camp, then Game Night.

    You should see: The Game Night panel opens.

  2. Step C2

    Phone 1: open Invite players. Phone 2 scans and joins.

    You should see: Phone 2 is in the game list.

  3. Step C3

    Back on Game Night: wait up to 5 seconds.

    You should see: Phone 2's name shows as "not yet joined". Add it.

  4. Step C4

    Try to start a round with only yourself.

    You should see: It says you need two players.

  5. Step C5

    Pick Tic-Tac-Toe. Press Start round. Play the real game through Invite players.

    You should see: The game plays on both phones.

  6. Step C6

    When the game ends, press the winner's name.

    You should see: The scoreboard adds 1 for that person.

  7. Step C7

    Press Rematch with another game.

    You should see: A new round opens with the same people.

  8. Step C8

    Press No winner on a round.

    You should see: The round closes, nobody scores.

  9. Step C9

    Rotate phone 1 to landscape and back in the middle of the night.

    You should see: The night, the people and the scores are still there. If they vanish, tick Fail and tell us.

  10. Step C10

    Press Remove on phone 2.

    You should see: Phone 2 leaves the roster.

  11. Step C11

    Add a practice bot. Look for a winner button for it.

    You should see: The bot has no winner button.

  12. Step C12

    With the night locked, try adding a bot. Write down what happens.

    You should see: Either answer is useful. Tell us what you saw.

  13. Step C13

    Join phone 2 with a rude name.

    You should see: It is refused or changed.

  14. Step C14

    Press End the night.

    You should see: The night ends. All-time standings still show next time.

  15. Step C15

    Read all text at the largest font size.

    You should see: Nothing is cut off.

How to report a problem

Tick Fail on the step, then tell us what you saw. Use the tester checklist and report form, or email testers@beeboentertainment.com.

Please include: your phone or PC model, the Beebo version, the step number, what you expected and what happened. Do not include passwords, email addresses, Wi-Fi names, QR codes or photos of other people.
