Campsite testing

Help us test Campsite features

Two features are ready for real people on real phones and TVs. These sheets walk you through them one step at a time.

Nothing on these pages is Beta yet. Both are labelled In development, open for testing. The only exception is the Windows part of Movie Night, which is already Beta on its own page.

What testing means

You follow the steps, look at what happens, and tick Pass if it matches "You should see", or Fail if it does not. A Fail is useful, not a mistake. We would rather hear about a problem now than after launch. If something works, please tell us that too.

Your ticks stay in this browser on this device. You can leave a sheet and come back to it later.

Shared photos & videos

Show photos and short videos from one phone on every phone at your campsite. About 45 minutes. Status: In development, open for testing.

Movie Night and Game Night

A film quiz and vote on your TV, and a score-keeping panel for Campsite games. About 50 minutes. Status: In development, open for testing (Movie Night on Windows is already Beta).

Before you start

  • Use test accounts and media you are allowed to use. Do not use photos of other people.
  • Keep original files safe. Test with copies.
  • Use your home Wi-Fi, not a guest network.

How to report a problem

Tick Fail on the step, then tell us what you saw. Use the tester checklist and report form, or email testers@beeboentertainment.com.

Please include: your phone or PC model, the Beebo version, the step number, what you expected and what happened. Do not include passwords, email addresses, Wi-Fi names, QR codes or photos of other people.
