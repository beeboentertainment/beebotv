THE ROAD AHEAD

Useful first.
More capable over time.

Beebo is growing around your personal library, your household and the places you go together. This page separates what you can use today from what is still being built.

AVAILABLE NOW

Your own library

Choose the folders Beebo uses on your Windows computer and enjoy your personal media at home.

Campsite guest sessions

A guest can join with the campsite QR flow, play games and watch prepared downloads. This core flow has been tested.

Original games

Enjoy Beebo original games with guests and bots. Game presentation and group tools continue to improve.

Stories and photos

Story Mode, personal photo views and phone photo backup are part of Beebo. Availability varies by app version and device.

Private household spaces

Profiles, parental controls and private-vault work are part of the Beebo household direction. The app shows the available controls for each account.

BETA / TESTING

Watch Together

Signed-in people can share a room with play, pause, seek, chat and reactions. Cross-device reliability still needs more real-world testing.

Movie Night

TV-led party games and phone joining are built around Beebo. More real TV and phone testing is still needed.

More personal media

Audiobooks, podcasts, internet radio and owner-supplied live TV hardware are supported or being tested. Beebo does not supply channels or a media catalogue.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Library Health

A clearer place to review duplicate copies, missing episodes, unavailable files, offline sources and folders needing attention.

Download Planner

Choose personal media before a trip, check device space, and know which downloads are truly ready for offline use.

Family Timeline

Host-led sharing for selected photos and videos, with next, previous, pause and stop controls for a campsite group.

Movie Night and Game Night

Refine the existing party games with clearer guest aliases, scoreboards, rounds and host controls.

Accessibility choices

Large controls, high contrast, simpler navigation, captions and clearer connection status across more screens.

Portrait Party

A private, consent-based photo-avatar activity for a campsite session. No public gallery or profile is planned.

LATER, AFTER TESTING

Household Library

Multiple approved computers contributing to one household view, while keeping an offline computer titles visible but unavailable to play.

Optional backup

Selected-folder backup and recovery tools, designed only after privacy, restoration and storage controls are ready.

Home server tools

Guided controls for customer-owned computers. Managed game hosting is a later idea, not a current Beebo service.

Our promise: Beebo will not label an unfinished feature as available. Personal media stays the focus; Beebo does not include a catalogue of films, television, music or channels.
