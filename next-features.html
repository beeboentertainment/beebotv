AVAILABLE OR BETA NOW

Your own library

Choose the folders Beebo uses on your Windows computer and enjoy your personal media at home.

Original games (Beta)

Beebo includes original games with guest and bot options. Check each game's supported platform and testing status before a group session.

Stories and photos (Beta)

Story Mode, personal photo views and phone photo backup are part of Beebo. Availability varies by app version and device.

Library Health

A place in the phone app to review duplicate copies, missing episodes, unavailable files and folders needing attention. Checked on a real phone with a real library on October 2, 2026; some wording is still being made clearer. It is in the Android test download.

Beebo Auto (Beta, test download)

Beebo Auto 1.21 (build 22), published Oct 27, 2026, 2:59 PM Eastern, is a sideload test download for Android Auto, not on Google Play; 1.21 has not been tried in a car yet. Beebo Auto 1.20 had one first look in one car on Oct 27, 2026 (the app appeared, four tabs and a TV episode with sound; next, previous, pause, steering-wheel buttons, search and any drive were not checked). An earlier build had one first check in one car on Oct 6, 2026 (sign-in, video list, sound, fast-forward). Video on the car screen, music, car-screen titles, away from home, passenger games, Car Party and other cars are not tested. Use only when parked. Download and testing limits.

Not every Available or Beta feature is verified on the newest downloads; each one lists what was tried. The full list of every feature with its label is on Everything we are and offer.

BETA / TESTING

Campsite guest sessions

A host and guest joined over real home Wi-Fi using the QR flow on two phones. On October 4, 2026 one film was shared from a host phone to a guest phone in Chrome. More films, more guests, game play and synchronization still need real-device tests. Android 1.51-tester11 keeps Wi-Fi names in other alphabets in the join QR code (automatic tests only).

Download Planner

Pick what to take on a trip and check the phone's free space before downloading. Tried on a real phone on October 2, 2026; a full start-to-finish trip download still needs a real test.

More personal media

Audiobooks, podcasts and internet radio are included in supported builds. They were tested on a real Beebo server with generated test audiobooks, local test podcast feeds and test radio streams, and on one phone (lists, playing, resuming, speed, sleep timer, playing with the screen off). Real podcast hosts and radio stations, the desktop app's own tabs, Android TV and Android Auto are not tested. From Beebo 0.1.75 a limited profile cannot use them unless the owner allows it. Beebo does not supply a media catalogue.

Live TV and DVR

Beta with your own HDHomeRun tuner only; real-tuner testing is still needed. Beebo supplies no channels. Adding your own M3U/IPTV playlist is in Beta in the Windows app (from version 0.1.72) and has not been tested with real playlists yet.

Casting to a TV

Beta. On October 5, 2026 casting from a Galaxy S9+ and a Galaxy S24 Ultra to a Chromecast TV started first try in about 8 seconds; 21 of 22 cast and phone checks passed. Stopping a cast after switching to the next episode breaks the phone player; that and the lock-screen title and notification picture after an episode switch are being fixed (the fix is being tested). Casting to a TV near you while you are away from home is a separate Beta feature (from the website since Windows 0.1.85, and a converted film from the Android test build since 1.51-tester22). Starting a film on your home TV from your phone while you are away is only an idea: a design was written on Oct 20, 2026, and nothing is built. See Cast to your TV.

Private household spaces

Profiles and parental controls are part of the Beebo household tools. The app shows the available controls for each account. Profiles and parental limits are Beta until restricted and normal profiles are checked on a real PC. Fixes for Live TV and Movie Night limits are in Windows 0.1.97 (Oct 21, 2026, 3:43 AM Eastern).

Watch Together

Host a film on one Android phone and watch in step on another. On October 2 and 3, 2026 it worked on two real phones in the same home, one connected directly and one through Beebo Relay: playing, pausing, jumping ahead, a dropped connection, the host leaving, and two different household members. Not tested yet: more than two phones, iPhone and long sessions. If a viewer's app is closed and reopened, it does not rejoin the party yet. The Back key in the Watch Together panel and the notice when a party ends were fixed in Android 1.51-tester12, which has not been re-checked on a real phone yet. On Oct 13 and Oct 22, 2026 it worked again on two phones in one home (on Oct 22 play, seek and pause were followed within 1 to 3 seconds; the phones were muted). Use the current Android test build.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Car Party

May not work yet. On October 3, 2026 a host and a passenger phone were tried together; two problems were found and fixed (the join code hint and the app on a Wi-Fi with no internet). The fixes still need a re-test, and it has not been tried in a real car while driving. In a real parked car a guest could follow along and send reactions, but a passenger could not watch the movie and a blank name blocked a guest. Car Party privacy fixes are in Android 1.51-tester12 (not re-checked on a real phone yet).

Movie Night

TV-led party games and phone joining are built around Beebo. More real TV and phone testing is still needed.

Link a TV

Sign a TV in with a code approved on your phone. Needs the newest phone app. Not tested on a real TV yet.

Wireless speakers

The Campsite Wireless speakers screen, with speaker placement and a timing trim, is in the Android app. It has not been tested with real wireless speakers, and brand compatibility is unverified.

TV apps

In-development tester builds for Fire TV, Roku and LG TVs, not tested on a real TV yet. Samsung and Xbox apps are in development, with no install file yet; try the TV's web browser or Edge on the Xbox. See Beebo on your devices.

Family Timeline

Host-led sharing for selected photos and videos, with next, previous, pause and stop controls for a campsite group.

Movie Night and Game Night

Refine the existing party games with clearer guest aliases, scoreboards, rounds and host controls.

Accessibility choices

Large controls, high contrast, simpler navigation, captions and clearer connection status across more screens.

Household Library

Multiple approved computers contributing to one household view, while keeping an offline computer's titles visible but unavailable to play. Built, switched off on our servers until it is tested.

Parental limits in Live TV and Movie Night (Oct 21, 2026 release)

In the Oct 21, 2026 release: a Live TV link stops when its profile is restricted, and a Movie Night room removes blocked titles when limits tighten. If a parental check itself fails, viewing continues; this is the owner’s choice.

What is left to reach 100%: Check restricted and normal profiles on a real PC after release, including changes during viewing.

Guest-safe Campsite video downloads (Oct 21, 2026 release)

In the Oct 21, 2026 release: guests get an H.264/AAC copy when the original audio will not play in a browser, and host approval no longer cuts a running download.

What is left to reach 100%: Play and save on real guest phones, hear the sound, seek, and approve another request during a running download.

Walkie-talkie from anywhere (Oct 21, 2026 release)

Off and in the Oct 21, 2026 release: Invite from anywhere always asks the host to approve each guest, Android has a host panel without a microphone, and each guest has message limits. Code is complete; it has not been tested on real phones. The server part is on; the Oct 21, 2026 release is the first app release that uses it.

What is left to reach 100%: Use a host and guests on unrelated networks; check approvals, message limits, disconnect and rejoin.

Android accessibility and layouts (Oct 21, 2026 release)

Another accessibility and layout pass is in Android 1.51-tester32 (Oct 21, 2026, 3:43 AM Eastern). Earlier phone tests cover the earlier screens and builds only.

What is left to reach 100%: Try TalkBack, 200% text, high contrast, landscape and large playback controls on real phones after release.

Beebo Auto hardening (Oct 21, 2026 release)

In the Oct 21, 2026 release: typing is blocked while projecting to the car, saved sign-in is kept in encrypted storage, and Car Party controls stay usable. Beebo Auto 1.20 had one first look in one car on Oct 27, 2026 (a few screens and an episode with sound; not a full real-car test); an earlier build had one first check in one car on Oct 6, 2026.

What is left to reach 100%: Run the first real-car and head-unit tests, parked, including sound, controls, sign-in, maps and reconnect.

Windows first-run connection test (Oct 21, 2026 release)

A first-run connection test card is in Windows 0.1.97 (Get Started shows a background connection test). It is not a promise that every router or away-from-home route will connect.

What is left to reach 100%: Check a real PC and different routers, including failed and unavailable connections.

Podcast import (Oct 21, 2026 release)

Podcast import from an OPML list file or a public feed link is in the downloads (Windows 0.1.88 and later; fixes in 0.1.97). Existing podcast playback keeps its earlier status.

What is left to reach 100%: Try real files, links and names, duplicates, failed imports and playback after release.

Remote Desktop keyboard helper (Oct 21, 2026 release)

Off by default. The keyboard helper change is in Windows 0.1.97; Remote Desktop has never been run on a real PC.

What is left to reach 100%: Use a real PC and phone to check keyboard, mouse, viewing, approval, disconnect and revoke.

Online browser games (next release)

The online browser-games page connection is merged, off and unreleased. The separate browser games already on this website keep their own status.

What is left to reach 100%: Play with real people on different networks; check reconnect and host controls.

BeeboVPN

The owner connected one phone with the standard WireGuard app and it worked. A BeeboVPN Android app has not been started. BeeboVPN is not offered for public sale.

What is left to reach 100%: Verify supported devices and connection behaviour before calling the service Available.

PLANNED

Beebo Points Rewards

Opt-in rewards are switched off. There are no points to earn or redeem in the current app.

Optional backup

Selected-folder backup and recovery tools, designed only after privacy, restoration and storage controls are ready.

Home server tools

Guided controls for customer-owned computers. Managed game hosting is a later idea, not a current Beebo service.

Portrait Party

A private, consent-based photo-avatar activity for a campsite session. No public gallery or profile is planned.

IDEAS (NOT PROMISED)

Start a film on your home TV while away

Your phone, away from home, would ask your home computer to play a film on the TV at home. A design was written on Oct 20, 2026; nothing is built.

TV volume from the phone while casting

TV-volume controls were included in release-2026-10-21c. A complete receiver-volume acceptance is still needed; see the dated test record.

Media requests and a wishlist

Media requests were included in release-2026-10-21c. A dated owner-observed test sent a title request and saw its status change; fuller acceptance remains limited.

Kitchen announcements

A short voice message that plays at once on chosen household phones or speakers. Included in release-2026-10-21c; recorded tests and open problems are listed on the testing page.

RETIRED

Stream from my PC

Retired. The old button was removed from the phone app on Oct 3, 2026; showing your computer's screen on your phone is not available. Remote Desktop is a separate feature in development.

Roku, Samsung, LG, Xbox and Apple TV apps have never run on real hardware. Beebo Auto 1.20 had one first look in one car on Oct 27, 2026 (a few screens and an episode with sound; not a full real-car test) (an earlier build had one first check in one car on Oct 6, 2026). Walkie-talkie from anywhere still needs real phones; the server part is on, and the apps released on Oct 21, 2026 use it. Remote Desktop still needs a real PC. This update changes no feature percentage or passed check.

Our promise: Beebo will not label an unfinished feature as available. Personal media stays the focus; Beebo does not include a catalogue of films, television, music or channels.
