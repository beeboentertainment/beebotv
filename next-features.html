BETA / TESTING

Campsite guest sessions

A host and guest joined over real home Wi-Fi using the QR flow on two phones. On October 4, 2026 one film was shared from a host phone to a guest phone in Chrome. More films, more guests, game play and synchronization still need real-device tests. Android 1.51-tester11 keeps Wi-Fi names in other alphabets in the join QR code (automatic tests only).

Movie Night

TV-led party games and phone joining are built around Beebo. More real TV and phone testing is still needed.

Library Health

A place in the phone app to review duplicate copies, missing episodes, unavailable files and folders needing attention. Checked on a real phone with a real library on October 2, 2026; some wording is still being made clearer.

Download Planner

Pick what to take on a trip and check the phone's free space before downloading. Tried on a real phone on October 2, 2026; a full start-to-finish trip download still needs a real test.

More personal media

Audiobooks, podcasts and internet radio are included in supported builds. They were tested on a real Beebo server with generated test audiobooks, local test podcast feeds and test radio streams, and on one phone (lists, playing, resuming, speed, sleep timer, playing with the screen off). Real podcast hosts and radio stations, the desktop app's own tabs, Android TV and Android Auto are not tested. From Beebo 0.1.75 a limited profile cannot use them unless the owner allows it. Beebo does not supply a media catalogue.

Live TV and DVR

Beta with your own HDHomeRun tuner only; real-tuner testing is still needed. Beebo supplies no channels. Adding your own M3U/IPTV playlist is in Beta in the Windows app (from version 0.1.72) and has not been tested with real playlists yet.

Watch Together

Beta. Host a film on one Android phone and watch in step on another. On October 2 and 3, 2026 it worked on two real phones in the same home, one connected directly and one through Beebo Relay: playing, pausing, jumping ahead, a dropped connection, the host leaving, and two different household members. Not tested yet: more than two phones, iPhone and long sessions. If a viewer's app is closed and reopened, it does not rejoin the party yet. The Back key in the Watch Together panel and the notice when a party ends are fixed in the next test build. Use the current Android test build (1.51-tester11).

Car Party

Beta: may not work yet. On October 3, 2026 a host and a passenger phone were tried together; two problems were found and fixed (the join code hint and the app on a Wi-Fi with no internet). The fixes still need a re-test, and it has not been tried in a real car. Car Party privacy fixes are in the next test build.

Casting to a TV

Beta. On October 5, 2026 casting from a Galaxy S9+ and a Galaxy S24 Ultra to a Chromecast TV started first try in about 8 seconds; 21 of 22 cast and phone checks passed. Stopping a cast after switching to the next episode breaks the phone player; that and the lock-screen title and notification picture after an episode switch are being fixed (the fix is being tested). See Cast to your TV.

Link a TV

Beta: sign a TV in with a code approved on your phone. Needs the newest phone app. Not tested on a real TV yet.

TV apps

Beta tester builds for Fire TV, Roku and LG TVs, not tested on a real TV yet. Samsung and Xbox have no install file yet; try the TV's web browser or Edge on the Xbox. See Beebo on your devices.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Wireless-speaker placement

A Campsite placement and timing-trim screen is being built. Independent wireless-speaker brand compatibility is still planned and unverified.

Family Timeline

Host-led sharing for selected photos and videos, with next, previous, pause and stop controls for a campsite group.

Movie Night and Game Night

Refine the existing party games with clearer guest aliases, scoreboards, rounds and host controls.

Accessibility choices

Large controls, high contrast, simpler navigation, captions and clearer connection status across more screens.

Portrait Party

A private, consent-based photo-avatar activity for a campsite session. No public gallery or profile is planned.

PLANNED

Beebo Points Rewards

Opt-in rewards are switched off. There are no points to earn or redeem in the current app.

Stream from my PC

Not in the app. The old button was removed in October 2026; showing your computer's screen on your phone is not available.

Household Library

Multiple approved computers contributing to one household view, while keeping an offline computer's titles visible but unavailable to play.

Optional backup

Selected-folder backup and recovery tools, designed only after privacy, restoration and storage controls are ready.

Home server tools

Guided controls for customer-owned computers. Managed game hosting is a later idea, not a current Beebo service.

Our promise: Beebo will not label an unfinished feature as available. Personal media stays the focus; Beebo does not include a catalogue of films, television, music or channels.
