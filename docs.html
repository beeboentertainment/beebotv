What the status words mean

Start here, Quick answers and similar labels mean the feature works in the current downloads (Beebo for Windows 0.1.58 and the Android app 1.40). Available now — Beta means it is in the current download but is new, with rough edges, and its page says what is untested on real equipment. Available now — test build (Beta) means it is in the current Android download, a test build that has not yet been checked on real phones. In development means it is being built with no date, and planned means we intend to, with no date. Beebo on your devices shows the live status of every device.
