One home for activities

The Android app’s Games tab is now Other. Open it for three clear sections:

  • Games: Tic-Tac-Toe, I Spy and the party games.
  • Outdoors: Campsite Mode, Star Chart, Nearby, Scavenger Hunt, Campfire Mode, Junior Ranger Badges and Trip Recap.
  • Tools: My Photos & Videos, Space Saver, Packing List, Read It To Me and Settings.

Starting Campsite Mode keeps these activities available. Use the campsite status card to return to the joining codes while guests keep watching.

Single-phone games work offline. Games synchronized across separate phones still need the Beebo app, internet and hub sign-in. The campsite video QR opens the video library.

Find photos anywhere on your computer

  1. Open Other → Tools → My Photos & Videos.
  2. Choose My backups for Space Saver files, or Browse computer with your administrator account.
  3. Open a drive and folder, or search a photo/video filename across the drives or inside the current folder.
  4. Tap a photo or video to show it. Use Search further to continue a large search.

Computer browsing is read-only and requires Windows 0.1.25 or later. Your PC must be on and reachable. Protected or unavailable folders and folder links may be skipped; guest accounts cannot browse your drives.

Install both updates

On Windows: choose Check for updates in Beebo Settings, or download the installer below, then reopen Beebo.

On Android: install the signed Android 1.9 update over your existing website app. Open Other to find the new sections.
