What is new in Windows 0.1.58

Everything below is Available now — Beta (Windows 0.1.58) unless it says otherwise: it is in the download, but it is new and some parts have not been tried on real equipment. Each item names what has not been tested.

  • Rebuilt video conversion. It tries your PC’s NVIDIA, Intel Quick Sync or AMD encoder (VAAPI on Linux), falls back automatically if one fails, tone-maps HDR, limits how many conversions run at once with a waiting line, and has an old-PC mode. Only Intel Quick Sync and software conversion have been exercised on real hardware. Details
  • File badges and playback decisions. 4K, HDR10, HDR10+, Dolby Vision, Atmos and DTS:X badges, and a per-device choice between playing the original, copying it or converting it. Passthrough to a receiver is unverified on real equipment, so Beebo does not claim it. Sound options: surround, night mode, volume levelling and audio delay.
  • Live TV and DVR with your own HDHomeRun tuner and antenna. Never tested on a real tuner.
  • Audiobooks, podcasts and internet radio. Podcasts and radio contact the feeds and directories you choose.
  • Watch Together rooms with an invite link, chat and reactions.
  • Movie Night party games for the TV (tested only with simulated phones), Cinema Mode pre-show trailers (official YouTube embeds, so it needs the internet) and phones as movie speakers (simulated and measured on one computer only).
  • Player and library. Seek previews, chapters, subtitle styling, more than one version of a film, automatic intro and credit skipping, library views (filters, shelves, a detailed list, backdrops, grouped views, folders), and a Trailers page.
  • Switch to Beebo. An importer for watched marks, resume points, ratings and playlists from Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Kodi and Letterboxd, with a review step and an undo.
  • Sign-in and tools. Optional two-factor sign-in, per-person API keys and signed webhooks.
  • Jellyfin-compatible mode, off by default. Checked against the official Jellyfin API description and SDK, but not tried with real Jellyfin apps. App-by-app table
  • Themes, languages and accessibility. Theme and layout packs, screens in English, Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Italian for the main screens, and accessibility improvements.
  • Speech Pack, an optional add-on that downloads the whisper.cpp speech engine (MIT licence) on request and makes automatic subtitles on your own PC.
  • Trip Journal sharing: a private link served from your own PC (Android 1.39 or later with Windows 0.1.58).
  • Linux and Docker. A Linux desktop AppImage and .deb, and a Docker image. Tested on Ubuntu 22.04 in our automated build only.
  • Faster. Big libraries open several times faster and idle CPU is much lower. Away-from-home streaming was about four to five times faster in our tests on a good connection.
  • Safer. A large security review, with fixes.

Not yet: apps for Roku, Samsung, LG, Fire TV, Xbox and Apple devices are in development and have never been run on real devices. Beebo does not claim HDR10+, Dolby Vision, Atmos or DTS:X passthrough. Watching at home never needs the internet; the owner check with the router unplugged is still to be done.

What is new in Android 1.40 (test build)

Published Sep 21, 2026, 4:26 PM Eastern (build 41). Everything below is Available now — test build (Beta): it is in the current download, but this is a test build, and none of it has been checked on real phones yet. It includes everything in Android 1.39.

  • License Plate & Sign Hunt. A checklist game for passengers, with Team and Race modes.
  • Trip Clock. A clock for the trip. It gives an estimate and is not navigation.
  • Quiet Hours and Wind-Down. Quiet Hours shows a banner on every guest phone. The Bedtime Wind-Down plays a story or ambience with a fade.
  • Roadside Quiz packs. 336 original questions in age bands.
  • Campfire Songbook engine. You import your own song packs, so check that you may use the songs you add. Only two tiny placeholder chants are built in.
  • Scavenger Hunt for Everyone. A hunt that works offline for guests in an ordinary phone browser, with no app and no account.
  • Android TV and Fire TV device profiles. The app tells the server more about what your TV device can play, for better direct play. HDR and surround are asked for only when the TV reports them. The Fire TV app itself is still in development.
  • Movie Night tile. A tile in the app for Movie Night, which needs Windows 0.1.58 on your PC.

In development: new family features for the separate Beebo Auto car companion (a Trip Clock glance, voice games and Roadside Stories). They are not in any download.

Earlier releases

The sections below describe changes from earlier versions (Windows 0.1.56 and Android 1.37).

Away Play: join a home-hosted game from anywhere

Home Game Server can now be reached the same way Beebo video already works away from home: a direct connection is tried first, falling back automatically to Beebo Relay if needed. Household members and invited friends can join a Minecraft server running on your PC from anywhere — no router or firewall changes, ever.

Two away-from-home quality tiers

The household away-from-home subscription now has two tiers: 1080p or 4K. Pick the one that matches how good you want it to look when you're away — local home streaming stays always free and always full quality either way. See the pricing page for details.

More reliable away-from-home connections

Fixed connections freezing for up to a minute after a network change (switching from Wi-Fi to mobile data, or a drop and reconnect), and fast-forwarding/seeking taking far too long to catch up while away from home. A new password now works away from home immediately, and choosing "Beebo Relay only" actually turns the relay on right away.

Know how your phone is connected

The Android connection banner now follows the active connection when its route changes. It identifies Beebo Relay or Cloudflare Relay when that provider can be confirmed. While checking, it says so instead of assuming a direct connection.

A friend’s Wi-Fi or a cellular connection can still connect directly to your home over the internet. The banner reports the actual connection route, not just which network your phone has joined.

Beebo Relay’s encrypted port 443 connection has also been repaired on our server. An enabled relay and a completed away-from-home phone test are now described separately in desktop setup.

A desktop that is easier to find your way around

  • Get Started: setup panels sit side by side on wider screens and stack on smaller windows.
  • Phone Backups: a clearer label for your backed-up phone photos and videos.
  • Video Playlists: makes it clear these collections contain movies and TV episodes.
  • Upload history: remove one entry or clear the displayed history without deleting the video files.
  • Log out: a dedicated button beneath the version and update information.
  • Away access: enable it for all approved users and choose a default for new users. Pending and revoked accounts still need approval; individual restrictions are respected.

Dice that roll on the board

In Ludo, Snakes and Ladders, and Five Dice, three-dimensional dice tumble over the board or dice tray, then settle on the host’s result. Guests see the same result on their own phones.

  • In Five Dice, tap a die to keep it. Kept dice stay still while the others roll.
  • Rolls stay close to the board as you scroll, and reduced-motion settings skip the tumbling.
  • A failed connection does not display an invented result. You can retry the action.

A fresh look across your games

  • New illustrations for 38 shared games and four solo games.
  • Detailed chess pieces, wooden boards, glossy counters, ivory dominoes and a matching card deck.
  • 4-in-a-row counters drop into place. Cards and dominoes lift when selected.
  • Refreshed Solitaire, Five Letters, Sudoku and Minesweeper graphics.

Play with friends on their own phones

  1. Update Beebo on the host phone.
  2. Open Other → Games → Play with guests and start your campsite session.
  3. Guests join the same hotspot or local Wi-Fi, scan the guest QR code and enter their names.
  4. Everyone opens Games, chooses the same game, and the leader starts the round.

After updating, restart Campsite Mode and have guests reload their game page. The artwork is included in the app, so guests do not need an internet connection to fetch it.
