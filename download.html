A good first step

New to Beebo?

Follow the setup guide →

You bring the media. Beebo brings it together.

Beebo does not include movies or shows. Choose files you have permission to store and play, create your owner account, then connect your phone on the same Wi-Fi for the first setup.

Prefer a browser?

The browser player is Available. Open the Beebo address shown by your home computer and sign in. There is no separate player to download.

These notes describe the named releases. Each feature’s tested score is on the testing evidence page. Earlier device checks do not prove a newer download.

Windows 0.1.129

Sharing with another household: this PC is now ready for a coming phone app update that accepts a share with just its share code and one press. This PC does the accept itself, so the phone never handles the licence, and only the owner or an admin of this home can accept a share this way. The current phone app (1.75) does not use it yet. Browser player: screen readers now find one main area on the player page, holding the title bar and the video, so they can jump straight to it; the page looks and works the same as before. Windows only. The phone app (1.75, build 147) and Beebo Auto (1.22) are separate releases. Full list in the release history.

Testing scope: Automated checks: the merged changes were tested on the 375 Windows desktop test files they touch, and all 375 passed; the new share-code accept has its own 10 tests, which pass. The share-code change had two clear code reviews (it touches sharing between households) and the player change one. The signed installer passed its signature, size, converter, connection-helper and language checks. The full Windows desktop test suite was not run on this release, and no person has installed this version or tried its in-app update yet. Windows 0.1.129 (Windows only) has had automated tests and code reviews only; no person has tried its changes or its in-app update yet.

Windows 0.1.128

Cast from the browser player: the Cast button now starts a real Google Cast session that sends only the film to your Chromecast or Google TV, from where you were watching, instead of a copy of the whole browser tab; while casting, the page's play, pause and seek controls drive the TV, and stopping puts the page back where the TV got to. Google's Cast script is loaded only when you tap Cast; if it cannot load, the old cast prompt is used. Browser player resume: when this browser has a newer spot than the home PC, even one too close to the start or end to offer, the older "Resume from ...?" question is no longer shown and the film starts from the beginning with the intro skip working as usual; Start over at once forgets this browser's saved spot; a player page opened without a household member never reuses a Reconnect spot saved for someone else. Browser player: after a seek, an old subtitle line is no longer left on screen while the subtitle list is briefly unavailable; Phone speakers waits for a full second of bad timing before showing Out of sync, also right after the page opens. Live TV: recordings with no file can only be removed from the list, while finished recordings can still be deleted; scheduling something that is already scheduled says so even when the recording list is full; a Jellyfin app that asks for the same live channel twice at once uses one tuner, not two. Library and metadata: a show named only by a year (such as 1923 or 1883) is looked up by its name instead of as a show that started that year, also from New items and TV Shows; an empty [ ] at the end of an episode file name no longer wipes out the episode title; library search treats punctuation from any language (curly quotes, long dashes and similar) as a space; the Organizer treats a folder you picked to scan as extras only when it is really named Extras, Featurettes, Trailers or Samples, so a picked folder whose name starts with a dot is scanned normally. Audiobooks and podcasts: on the PC, when the Library, Series or Reading order view cannot load, the Audiobooks screen now says so instead of staying silent; in the browser, opening a series (or All series) while a search is still pending no longer jumps back to the library list; a podcast speed picked while your settings are still loading no longer erases the speeds saved for your other shows. Campsite Mode on the PC: your newest choice of what to share always wins, even if the Wi-Fi or hotspot reconnects while it is being applied, and a slow Share all can no longer fill a room that has since ended or been restarted. Movie Night: if the room behind an open Movie Night window has already closed, pressing Movie Night again closes that old window instead of leaving it next to the new one. Link a TV: in Account security, signing out a linked TV now also unlinks it from beebo.tv, so it cannot sign straight back in; the screen says whether the unlink was confirmed, or tells you to remove the TV on your phone under Settings > Link a TV. In the browser, only the owner or the member who linked a TV can unlink it this way; anyone else only ends their own session. Plain error messages: the Movies, TV Shows, Settings and Users screens no longer show raw computer error text (such as permission codes or [object Object]); they say what to do next, and the technical detail goes to the log. Sharing with another household: the share preview says 1 screen or 2 screens correctly. Photos, Converter and more: Ctrl + mouse wheel zooms a photo without also zooming the whole Beebo window, and the plain wheel still moves around a zoomed photo; shows moved to the front of the Converter queue one right after another stay in the order you chose; the Remote Desktop viewer keeps showing No picture yet after control is approved if no picture has arrived; on the Users page in the browser, each action button names the person by username or email when they have no display name, so screen readers say who it is for. Windows only. The phone app (1.74, build 146) and Beebo Auto (1.22) are separate releases. Full list in the release history.

Testing scope: Automated checks: each group of merged changes was tested on the files it touched. In the first group 547 of 549 files passed; the 2 failures came from one change that was then taken out of this release, and both pass without it. In the second group 124 of 124 player, cast and watching-together files passed (one timing-sensitive test passed when run alone), and in the third 54 of 54. Each change had a clear code review (two for protected changes), and the signed installer passed its signature, size, converter, connection-helper and language checks. The full Windows desktop test suite was not run on this release, and no person has installed this version or tried its in-app update yet. Windows 0.1.128 (Windows only) has had automated tests and code reviews only; no person has tried its changes or its in-app update yet.

Windows 0.1.127

Browser player: "Resume from ...?" is offered from this browser's own newer spot even when the home PC has none; each household member's spots are kept separately, so nobody is offered another member's position; the intro skip waits while the question is open; Cinema Mode's pre-show still starts on a fresh film. Browser player: a seek from the lock screen while the screen is off is saved, and switching to screen-off audio never overwrites the saved spot with zero; Previous steps over a missing episode to the nearest one you have; the M (mute) and C (subtitles) keys also work with Shift held; on phones, a late full-screen rotation error no longer undoes a newer one. Music in the browser: album search ignores punctuation and accents (acdc live finds AC/DC (Live), rock and roll finds Rock & Roll); a volume change during a crossfade is remembered; a last song that cannot play follows Repeat all instead of leaving playback stuck; an artist's guest appearances follow the album order shown on the artist page; an album radio mix still opens with its album when every song on it was played recently. Audiobooks and podcasts: a late answer from the previous book can no longer add or delete a bookmark on the book you are on; an older Change folder result can no longer replace a newer folder choice; a damaged front cover no longer hides a usable back cover; a podcast feed with an unusual document header (an address in square brackets) can be followed, and nothing extra is downloaded. Live TV: when the free tuner cannot be reached and the other is busy, the message says the tuner is unreachable instead of all tuners busy; a new guide source no longer inherits the old source's long retry wait; Cancel, Delete and Remove on Recordings show why the home PC refused; pressing Record twice after a guide end-time correction no longer schedules a programme twice; long programme details scroll on short phone screens. Library and metadata: a show folder named like an extras folder is still listed when its season folders use other languages (Saison, Temporada, Staffel, Säsong, Specials); impossible dates in episode names (such as 2024.02.30) are no longer read as air dates; in Fix match, a slow successful save is always applied; the last country you pick on a title sheet or in Discover's where-to-watch picker is the one saved; a late metadata settings save no longer hides a download result; screen readers name the missing-poster card in the sequels view once; Photos reports the full number of located photos when the map shows only part of them. Subtitles: an OpenSubtitles download named .srt that is really WebVTT is saved as .vtt so it plays; older Windows subtitle files show curly quotes, dashes and the euro sign correctly. Watching together: a late pause no longer rewinds everybody to an older spot after a newer seek; in Live Rooms, a late block or report answer no longer closes a newer dialog; on the Movie Night TV page the arrow keys work inside the claim-code box; Movie Night guest search and the smart playlist preview show only the answer for your newest typing. Sharing with another household: a guest whose share access has expired can no longer load the shared library's posters. Settings and start-up: a slow earlier save of the nightly backup keep box no longer erases the number you are typing; while Appearance has unsaved layout edits, Layout Studio and Import layout are turned off so they cannot overwrite them; if you turned Beebo off in Task Manager's start-up list, Beebo no longer switches it back on when it starts, and a diagnostics file shows the real reason. Games and Jellyfin apps: Number Grid gives the same perfect score when cells were restored; Jellyfin apps list episodes in the right order even with very large episode numbers. Windows only. The phone app (1.73, build 145) and Beebo Auto (1.22) are separate releases. Full list in the release history.

Testing scope: Automated checks: the full Windows desktop test suite ran on the first group of merged changes (2,083 files) with no real failures; one test failed only from test-machine memory and passed with more memory, and 3 timing-sensitive tests passed on a re-run. All 581 files touched by the second group of changes then passed. Each change had a clear code review (two for protected changes), and the signed installer passed its signature, size, converter, connection-helper and language checks. The full suite was not re-run after the version-number change, and no person has installed this version or tried its in-app update yet. Windows 0.1.127 (Windows only) has had automated tests and code reviews only; no person has tried its changes or its in-app update yet.

Windows 0.1.126

Live TV recording: a new Remove from list button takes an entry off the recording list, entries beyond the 1,000-entry limit are no longer dropped when the app starts, and the limit still holds after a restart. Backup: a link or other non-file in the safety-copy folder is no longer treated as a safety copy, so restore and clean-up never follow a link outside that folder. Nothing new is deleted. Audiobooks: old library indexes re-read M4B and MP4 parts once so their covers are repaired (chapters kept); a late Library error no longer replaces a good Series view; in the browser, choosing another tab while a search is running no longer jumps back to the library list. Beebo Inbox and organizer: a double-episode file keeps both numbers in its new name (S01E01-E02); the not enough space notice clears when Windows reports the same file with different letter case; when the folder you pick is itself an Extras, Trailers or Samples folder, its videos are treated as extras. Renames only, nothing is deleted. Library and metadata: bracketed tags at the end of an episode title are removed cleanly, a show named only with a year is matched, an Extras, Samples or Trailers folder with S01E01-style bonus names is not mistaken for a show, and library search ignores every accent mark in more scripts. Converter: Convert whole show finds queued files whatever the letter case in the path. Watch Together in the browser: a room update waits until you finish typing a word with Japanese or Chinese input, and an invalid room code in the address no longer turns a remembered room into an invite. Windows only. The phone app (1.73, build 145) and Beebo Auto (1.22) are separate releases. Full list in the release history.

Testing scope: Automated checks: the full Windows desktop test suite ran on the merged changes (2,083 files); its one real failure came from a change that was then taken out of this release, and a re-run of the 289 affected files passed 289 of 289. One other test failed only from test-machine memory and passed with more memory, and one timing-sensitive test passed when run alone. Each change had a clear code review (two for protected changes), and the signed installer passed its signature, size, converter, connection-helper and language checks. The full suite was not re-run after the version-number change, and no person has installed this version or tried its in-app update yet. Windows 0.1.126 (Windows only) has had automated tests and code reviews only; no person has tried its changes or its in-app update yet.

Windows 0.1.125

Sharing with another household: you accept an invite with the share code alone and one Accept invite button, with no e-mail, password or guest box. German share-code wording uses formal Sie. Link a TV: a TV you remove loses its connection to the home PC within minutes, including profiles switched on that TV. Campsite: the owner can host a Campsite from this computer for guests on the same Wi-Fi (in development), with limits per guest device and translated screens. Users: removing a household member removes only the settings, sessions and party access of that person, keeps the settings of every other member, and refuses to remove the last owner. No videos, exports or backups are deleted. Jellyfin apps: when Live TV is switched on, Jellyfin apps can list channels and the guide and play a channel (read-only). Live TV recording: stopping, cancelling or resuming a recording keeps the parts already recorded, a lost tuner resumes, cancelled series airings are skipped, channel logos inside M3U lists show, and counts use the right plural. DLNA speakers: playback resumes after a speaker scan ages out, speakers show the track name and details, and old speakers drop off the list. Backup and restore: messages are accurate and translated, the restore preview says which items will be replaced, and the parental PIN and backup passphrase are checked without freezing the app. Watching in the browser: after a seek the subtitle for the new spot shows at once, going Back to Movies keeps your search, a podcast speed chosen while settings load is kept, phone speakers no longer flicker Out of sync, and pressing Movie Night again brings the open room forward. Smaller fixes: Up Next and Previous handle files that hold two episodes; sizes just under a unit show as the next unit and broken lengths show as zero; zh-Hant metadata uses Traditional Chinese; Remote Desktop says No picture yet when nothing arrives; asking for a title again reopens a closed request; Family Watch never shows empty extra tiles; the window keeps a 640-pixel minimum width; layout dialogs open with visible focus; the Get the app page shows dates in the page language. Separate server changes: share-code-only invites (about 7:45 AM Eastern) and the faster Link a TV removal check plus the party rejoin fix (about 8:45 AM Eastern) went live on the Beebo server on Nov 17, 2026; none of this is part of the Windows installer. Windows only. The phone app (1.73, build 145) and Beebo Auto (1.22) are separate releases. Full list in the release history.

Testing scope: Automated checks: the full Windows desktop test suite ran on the merged changes (2,082 files) and everything passed except 3 guard tests, whose two causes were fixed before this build; a re-run of the 482 affected files then passed except 2 that failed only from test-machine memory or timing and passed when run alone. Each change had a clear code review (two for protected changes), and the signed installer passed its signature, size, converter, connection-helper and language checks. The full suite was not re-run after the version-number change, and no person has installed this version or tried its in-app update yet. Windows 0.1.125 (Windows only) has had automated tests and code reviews only; no person has tried its changes or its in-app update yet.

Windows 0.1.124

Windows app engine: updated Electron to 44.9.1 with a security fix for encrypted internet connections. Sign-in: the Windows server now supports the new Keep me signed in flow and tracks kept sign-ins through normal restarts. Changing a password, ending a session or changing the owner also ends the affected kept access. An owner notice explains when a remote sign-out still needs to finish. Audiobooks in the browser: a slower answer from an earlier search can no longer replace the results for the words you have just typed. Story Studio: a story whose saved starting page is missing opens at its first available page, including after Restart. A failed page-path check now shows an error instead of disappearing silently. Library and subtitles: browser library cards have names for screen readers, episode names lose release tags without losing real hyphenated names, shows named 1923 or 1883 keep their real names, and title-review rows show the current TV match. Subtitle files have better language names and forced or hearing-impaired flags, and capital italic, bold and underline tags keep their styling. Music: volume and mute carry over to the next song and next visit in the desktop and browser players. Desktop media keys can move to the next track, empty lyrics files no longer hide embedded lyrics, short crossfaded songs are counted, and extreme ReplayGain values no longer produce an invalid volume multiplier. German Music wording uses formal Sie. Live TV guide: invalid seconds in programme times are rejected, and the next programme can be found beyond a twelve-hour gap in the cached guide. Separate server change: a limit on share-code guesses went live on the Beebo server on Nov 16, 2026, 10:59 PM Eastern (47 of 47 server tests passed before and after the change). It is not part of this Windows installer, and no real household has tried it yet. Windows only. The phone app remains a separate release; the new Android Keep me signed in flow is not in the public phone app (1.72 build 144 or 1.73 build 145). New Live TV search is outside this release. Music analysis handles damaged audio samples without propagating invalid numbers. Photos Map limits the rows shown at once when map tiles fail, while keeping located photos reachable by scrolling and keyboard. Audiobook titles and series names taken from folders keep endings such as .22 or .sf instead of treating them as file extensions. Audiobook chapter parsing handles very large chapter times safely. Extremely large video chapter times no longer crash HLS playlist creation. Long radio metadata titles keep complete Unicode characters, including emoji, at the title limit. Full list in the release history.

Testing scope: Automated checks: the full Windows desktop test suite ran on this exact source (20,849 passed, 0 failed, 84 not run, 35 to-do), two independent code reviews were clear, and the signed installer passed its signature, size, converter, connection-helper and language checks. No person has installed this version or tried its in-app update yet. Windows 0.1.124 (Windows only) has had automated tests and code reviews only; no person has tried its changes or its in-app update yet.

Android 1.75 (build 147)

This release fixes Watch Together wiping your place in a film: joining a room while another film was open could jump that film to 0:00 and save it. Joining now opens the room’s film, and the host keeps their spot. It also fixes audiobooks stuck on “Checking”, the Watch Together room labels overlapping with the phone turned sideways, and Home opening at the previous person’s scroll position after a different person signs in.

Not in this build: the Movie Night fix for phones signed in with a beebo.tv address, and the Watch Together sign-in fix for party admission, come next.
