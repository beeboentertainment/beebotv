A good first step

New to Beebo?

Follow the setup guide →

You bring the media. Beebo brings it together.

Beebo does not include movies or shows. Choose files you have permission to store and play, create your owner account, then connect your phone on the same Wi-Fi for the first setup.

Prefer a browser?

The browser player is Available. Open the Beebo address shown by your home computer and sign in. There is no separate player to download.

Read before relying on it

What changed. What still needs a try.

See testing & progress →

These notes describe the named releases. Earlier device checks do not prove a newer download.

Windows 0.1.120

This release adds clearer browser playback errors and recovery after a stuck seek, improves Watch Together and Movie Night, and repairs photo controls and update checks.

Testing limit: automated tests and code review only. No person has tried this build’s changes or its in-app update yet.

Android 1.68 (build 140)

This release keeps you on Home after you visit Browse, fits Watch Together in landscape, stops game screens (Sea Battle, Nature, standings) from overlapping, steadies Bingo when you scroll and makes marked squares clear, shows item counts correctly in the Scavenger hunt, words it more clearly when an episode cannot play, and adds Number Grid Expert stats.

Testing limit: automated tests, independent code reviews and file checks only. This release has not been tried on a real phone or TV, or opened on an emulator.

Beebo Auto 1.22 (build 23)

This release gives clearer reasons when a saved item cannot start, keeps already-loaded lists through a loss of signal, and separates work belonging to different sign-ins.

At publication: the evidence consisted of automated tests, code reviews and file checks.

Later recorded check: public Beebo Auto 1.22 (build 23), Nov 13, 2026, 8:49 PM Eastern. On a phone emulator, the settings stayed portrait at both text sizes and an empty picture-in-picture window opened and returned: 19 checks passed, 0 failed and 6 were not run.

No real-phone or car-head-unit test is recorded for this release. Active passenger media, car controls, maps ducking and driving were not tested. Beebo Auto had limited car checks on older builds on Oct 6 and Oct 27, 2026. The current build (1.22) has not been tested in a real car. Read the dated Auto record and its limits.
