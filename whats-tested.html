Windows 0.1.79

  • We fixed a few security problems we found in our own review. We keep the details private.

  • Music radio: pressing "Next song" very fast no longer fills the wrong station.
  • Sign-in now only uses Beebo's own address. You should not notice a change.

Windows may still show a SmartScreen warning, because the installer is not code-signed. Full notes: Windows 0.1.79.

Android 1.51-tester12 and Beebo Auto 1.16

  • Watch Together and Car Party: better privacy for guest names, and ending a car party is more reliable. Both are Beta.
  • The app explains when a Watch Together session was interrupted, and keeps your place if your sign-in refreshes.

  • The Campsite walkie-talkie guest page opens in the guest's language.
  • Beebo Auto: more messages, including the sleep timer, now follow the app language. A Car Party can only be run from the car that started it, and an old Start press is ignored.

Android 1.51-tester11

  • Changing the app language now works at once. You no longer have to close and reopen the app.
