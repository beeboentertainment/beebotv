In development · next-release changes

Updated Oct 22, 2026, 6:25 AM Eastern. Windows 0.1.99 and Android 1.51-tester34 (build 84), published Oct 21, 2026, 3:46 PM Eastern; Beebo Auto 1.20 (build 21), published Oct 21, 2026, 6:52 AM Eastern. Device runs after publishing are recorded in the release evidence; most of the changes below have still not been tried by a person.

New in Windows 0.1.99 and Android 1.51-tester34 (published Oct 21, 2026, 3:46 PM Eastern; not yet tried on real devices): audiobooks keep your place when the player closes and books that showed "[object Object]" are read again, hidden files such as Mac "._" copies are skipped in every library scan, sidebar Movies and TV Shows counts update when a folder is added or removed, Live TV handles a failed parental check the same way everywhere, the converter can use libx264 when ffmpeg has no OpenH264, Next and Previous work on downloaded episodes (also offline), the screen-off switch moved into Quality & audio, genre names follow the app language, and more screens are translated. Beebo Auto is unchanged at 1.20. Full release notes.

Earlier the same day (Oct 21, 2026, 6:52 AM Eastern), Windows 0.1.98, Android 1.51-tester33 and Beebo Auto 1.20 added (In development where marked; not yet tried on real devices): Picture frame and Family achievements (both off until turned on), 12-hour guest passes with early renewal, Live TV confirmation of home-network playlist addresses, Movie Night and walkie-talkie fixes, and Beebo Auto sign-in storage fixes. Full release notes.

The changes below are in those downloads unless a card says otherwise. The Oct 22 device runs added no passed check; they found the failures listed on this page. Recorded feature percentages and device limits remain separate.

Parental limits in Live TV and Movie Night · In development

In the Oct 21, 2026 release: a Live TV link stops when its profile is restricted, and a Movie Night room removes blocked titles when limits tighten. If a parental check itself fails, viewing continues; this is the owner’s choice.

What is left to reach 100%: Check restricted and normal profiles on a real PC after release, including changes during viewing.

Guest-safe Campsite video downloads · In development

In the Oct 21, 2026 release: guests get an H.264/AAC copy when the original audio will not play in a browser, and host approval no longer cuts a running download.

What is left to reach 100%: Play and save on real guest phones, hear the sound, seek, and approve another request during a running download.

Walkie-talkie from anywhere · In development

Off and in the Oct 21, 2026 release: Invite from anywhere always asks the host to approve each guest, Android has a host panel without a microphone, and each guest has message limits. Code is complete; it has not been tested on real phones. The server part is on; the Oct 21, 2026 release is the first app release that uses it.

What is left to reach 100%: Use a host and guests on unrelated networks; check approvals, message limits, disconnect and rejoin.

Android accessibility and layouts · In development

Another accessibility and layout pass is in Android 1.51-tester32 (Oct 21, 2026, 3:43 AM Eastern). Earlier phone tests cover the earlier screens and builds only.

What is left to reach 100%: Try TalkBack, 200% text, high contrast, landscape and large playback controls on real phones after release.

Beebo Auto hardening · In development

In the Oct 21, 2026 release: typing is blocked while projecting to the car, saved sign-in is kept in encrypted storage, and Car Party controls stay usable. Beebo Auto 1.20 has not been tested in a real car; an earlier build had one first check in one car on Oct 6, 2026.

What is left to reach 100%: Run the first real-car and head-unit tests, parked, including sound, controls, sign-in, maps and reconnect.

Windows first-run connection test · In development

A first-run connection test card is in Windows 0.1.97 (Get Started shows a background connection test). It is not a promise that every router or away-from-home route will connect.

What is left to reach 100%: Check a real PC and different routers, including failed and unavailable connections.

Podcast import · In development

Podcast import from an OPML list file or a public feed link is in the downloads (Windows 0.1.88 and later; fixes in 0.1.97). Existing podcast playback keeps its earlier status.

What is left to reach 100%: Try real files, links and names, duplicates, failed imports and playback after release.

Remote Desktop keyboard helper · In development

Off by default. The keyboard helper change is in Windows 0.1.97; Remote Desktop has never been run on a real PC.

What is left to reach 100%: Use a real PC and phone to check keyboard, mouse, viewing, approval, disconnect and revoke.

Online browser games · In development

The online browser-games page connection is merged, off and unreleased. The separate browser games already on this website keep their own status.

What is left to reach 100%: Play with real people on different networks; check reconnect and host controls.

BeeboVPN · In development

The owner connected one phone with the standard WireGuard app and it worked. A BeeboVPN Android app has not been started. BeeboVPN is not offered for public sale.

What is left to reach 100%: Verify supported devices and connection behaviour before calling the service Available.

Ideas, not promised: starting a film on your home TV from your phone while you are away (a design only; nothing is built), a fuller wishlist. TV volume from the phone while casting, media requests and kitchen announcements are now in the downloads (In development where marked). Casting to a TV near you while you are away from home is a different, existing Beta feature (Windows 0.1.85 and later).

Roku, Samsung, LG, Xbox and Apple TV apps still need real hardware. Beebo Auto 1.20 has not been tested in a real car (an earlier build had one first check in one car on Oct 6, 2026), walkie-talkie from anywhere still needs real phones, and Remote Desktop still needs a real PC.

What's new · the October 5, 2026 release

What changed, in plain words.

These notes are for the October 5, 2026 release. Newer release notes are on Updates.

Windows 0.1.79

  • We fixed a few security problems we found in our own review. We keep the details private.
  • "Stream once" (one film shared with guests at a campsite) now needs your computer's yes. A TV link, a viewer link or a library guest cannot start it.
  • The Admin web pages, the Movies and TV pages in a web browser, and the guest pages (phone speakers, Movie Night, the walkie-talkie) are now in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil) and Italian.
  • The Surprise, Upload and trip-share pages are translated too. A trip-share page opens in the viewer's language.
  • Settings search and six sidebar labels are now translated. German now uses the polite "Sie" everywhere.
  • The new translations were made with a machine's help. A native speaker has not read them yet, so some wording may be a little off.
  • Music radio: pressing "Next song" very fast no longer fills the wrong station.
  • Sign-in now only uses Beebo's own address. You should not notice a change.

Windows may still show a SmartScreen warning, because the installer is not code-signed. Full notes: Windows 0.1.79.

Android 1.51-tester12 and Beebo Auto 1.16

  • Watch Together and Car Party: better privacy for guest names, and ending a car party is more reliable. Both are Beta.
  • The app explains when a Watch Together session was interrupted, and keeps your place if your sign-in refreshes.
  • The phone's Back button closes the Watch Together panel first.
  • The Campsite walkie-talkie guest page opens in the guest's language.
  • Beebo Auto: more messages, including the sleep timer, now follow the app language. A Car Party can only be run from the car that started it, and an old Start press is ignored.

These changes were checked by automatic tests only. Nobody has tried tester12 on a real phone yet. Beebo Auto was checked once in a real car on Oct 6 (see the real-device table); these particular changes were not part of that check.

Android 1.51-tester11

  • Changing the app language now works at once. You no longer have to close and reopen the app.
  • When the picture stops because the video screen was closed, the player says so and offers "Try again". It no longer wrongly says the phone can't play the file.
  • Picture-in-picture keeps playing when you change the language.
  • Missing episodes no longer show the episode code twice.
  • Casting: the first cast of a film your computer is still preparing now waits up to 2 minutes instead of failing after 30 seconds. The phone says "Preparing on your computer" while it waits.
  • Campsite: the Wi-Fi QR code keeps Wi-Fi names in other alphabets. The guest page no longer mixes up two films with the same name.
  • Clearer help on the Campsite Wi-Fi card for hotspot, "wrong password" and "no internet" problems.

This is a test build you install yourself. It is not on Google Play. Full notes: Android 1.51-tester11.

Known problems in these versions

Problems we know about.

  • Found on October 21 and 22, fixes are merged in the code or still in testing (none is released or retested): on the phone, no way to choose embedded subtitles, sidecar subtitles not shown and no version picker for a film with two files (Galaxy S25); Next on a downloaded episode skipped a saved episode while online when one in between was missing (two phones; other runs on the same build disagree); text cut off at 150% text (the player top bar, the title-page Download button and some other buttons); the Campsite guest page says nothing after the host stops; photo pictures do not load for a child profile; opening a film in a browser through the away address fails; and on Windows 0.1.98 a folder’s own poster and NFO files are not used. Found on Oct 22 with no fix recorded yet: a child profile’s Movie Night page showed a raw error.
  • Found on October 12, fixed in the code, not yet in a published download: a shared-library guest could be dropped to a sign-in screen with no way back to their own home; a stopped share showed a wrong message and still looked active in the guest’s list; the web player froze for minutes after a phone switched from Wi-Fi to mobile data; the home PC could stop accepting viewers after many sessions because ports were not released; the app’s own picture-in-picture window did not close properly; some text was clipped at 150% text size; Library health showed a misleading counter and a stale “cannot be reached” line; skip-intro jumped over a 2:39 opening scene on one show; Auto quality chose 480p on a fast home connection; a share with downloads switched off still offered a download button.
  • Found on October 12, still open: a 40 to 70 second freeze after seeking far into a long converted film (a safeguard is written, but the cause is not fully confirmed); voices slightly out of sync after jumps on one TV episode (a likely cause was found and fixed in the code, but it is not confirmed on the real episode); the Read It To Me buttons were hidden behind the bottom bar (a fix is in progress); a new home name could not get its secure certificate until it was registered by hand; casting from a browser to a friend’s Chromecast was blocked because that TV is not yet registered for Beebo’s test receiver (casting to a TV through the phone app was proven earlier on the home TV).
  • Watch Together: if a viewer's app is closed and reopened, it does not rejoin the party and does not explain that it left. A notice that the party ended on that phone, and a fix for the Back key in the Watch Together panel, are fixed in the next test build.
  • Casting: stopping a cast after switching to the next episode leaves the phone's player stuck on "The picture stopped", and Try again does not help. Leave the player and open the episode again. A fix is being tested, along with one for the lock screen keeping the old episode title while casting and the missing notification picture.
  • Car Party: the October 3 problems (the QR saying the Wi-Fi password is wrong, and the app not loading after joining) are not confirmed fixed on real phones. Some Car Party privacy fixes are in the next test build.
  • Largest text size: at 150% text the player’s top bar is cut off on four phones and some buttons are clipped (Oct 22, tester34); the fix is merged in the code, not released or retested.
  • Windows installer: digitally signed (publisher: Nicholas Will) since Windows 0.1.86. Windows SmartScreen may still warn the first time because the publisher name is new. Since Windows 0.1.96 the updater also checks that an update installer is signed by the Beebo publisher before it installs it; older versions keep checking the file checksum until they update.
