What's new · October 5, 2026

What changed, in plain words.

Windows 0.1.79

  • We fixed a few security problems we found in our own review. We keep the details private.
  • "Stream once" (one film shared with guests at a campsite) now needs your computer's yes. A TV link, a viewer link or a library guest cannot start it.
  • The Admin web pages, the Movies and TV pages in a web browser, and the guest pages (phone speakers, Movie Night, the walkie-talkie) are now in Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil) and Italian.
  • The Surprise, Upload and trip-share pages are translated too. A trip-share page opens in the viewer's language.
  • Settings search and six sidebar labels are now translated. German now uses the polite "Sie" everywhere.
  • The new translations were made with a machine's help. A native speaker has not read them yet, so some wording may be a little off.
  • Music radio: pressing "Next song" very fast no longer fills the wrong station.
  • Sign-in now only uses Beebo's own address. You should not notice a change.

Windows may still show a SmartScreen warning, because the installer is not code-signed. Full notes: Windows 0.1.79.

Android 1.51-tester11

  • Changing the app language now works at once. You no longer have to close and reopen the app.
  • When the picture stops because the video screen was closed, the player says so and offers "Try again". It no longer wrongly says the phone can't play the file.
  • Picture-in-picture keeps playing when you change the language.
  • Missing episodes no longer show the episode code twice.
  • Casting: the first cast of a film your computer is still preparing now waits up to 2 minutes instead of failing after 30 seconds. The phone says "Preparing on your computer" while it waits.
  • Campsite: the Wi-Fi QR code keeps Wi-Fi names in other alphabets. The guest page no longer mixes up two films with the same name.
  • Clearer help on the Campsite Wi-Fi card for hotspot, "wrong password" and "no internet" problems.

This is a test build you install yourself. It is not on Google Play. Full notes: Android 1.51-tester11.

Status · October 5, 2026

Where each platform and feature stands.

Beta means you can try it now, but it is new, some parts may not work, and some parts are not tested on real equipment. The last column has a download or a way to try it, where there is one. In development means it is being built or is switched off, and it is not ready to rely on. Planned means it is not built yet.

Automatic tests only

Checked by our automatic tests, not yet by a person.

Automatic tests run on a computer. They catch many mistakes, but they cannot tell us how a screen looks or feels, or how a real network behaves.

  • Windows 0.1.79: all the new translations (checked for missing words and broken text in all six languages), the German "Sie" and Portuguese wording fixes, the "Next song" fix, the network-folder message, genre names with a $ sign, and the sign-in address change. The "stream once" approval was also tried on a real test server, but not with real guests or a TV link.
  • Android 1.51-tester11: the "picture stopped" message, picture-in-picture during a language change, Link a TV and pairing answers coming back after Android closes the app, the Campsite QR with a non-Latin Wi-Fi name, the hotspot help text, and the 2-minute cast wait (with a simulated clock).
  • Test runs on the code from release night (October 5): on a Linux test machine, 739 of 739 Windows-app test files, 951 of 951 server tests and 506 of 508 service tests passed (2 skipped because they need Windows or a missing tool). The Android app's 4,120 automatic tests passed the same night. These ran a few hours before the final release commit, not on it.
  • TV apps (Roku, Samsung, LG, Xbox): their automatic tests pass on a desktop computer. That proves the code, not a TV.
Not tested yet · October 5, 2026

What nobody has tested yet.

  • The exact 0.1.79 installer and the exact tester11 file on a real PC or phone. The published files were checked against their published fingerprints (SHA-256), but no hands-on test of them is recorded.
  • The new translations on a real screen, and by a native speaker.
  • The Android language change on Android 11, 12 or 13, and on phones from other makers. (It was tried on Android 10 and Android 16.)
  • The 2-minute first-cast wait and the "Preparing on your computer" message on a real Chromecast.
  • The Campsite QR with a non-Latin Wi-Fi name scanned by a real phone, and the guest-page changes with a real guest phone.
  • Link a TV, Android TV on a real TV, Beebo Auto in a real car, and every TV app (Fire TV, Roku, LG, Samsung, Xbox) on real hardware.
  • iPhone in Safari for Campsite and Watch Together, a real Mac, a real Linux desktop and any NAS.
  • Away from home on many different networks; so far it was tried on one mobile-data connection.
  • Windows: an update over an existing install on a second PC, and the Windows hand-test list.
Known problems in these versions

Problems we know about.

  • Watch Together: if a viewer's app is closed and reopened, it does not rejoin the party and does not explain that it left. A notice that the party ended on that phone, and a fix for the Back key in the Watch Together panel, are fixed in the next test build.
  • Casting: stopping a cast after switching to the next episode leaves the phone's player stuck on "The picture stopped", and Try again does not help. Leave the player and open the episode again. A fix is being tested, along with one for the lock screen keeping the old episode title while casting and the missing notification picture.
  • Car Party: the October 3 problems (the QR saying the Wi-Fi password is wrong, and the app not loading after joining) are not confirmed fixed on real phones. Some Car Party privacy fixes are in the next test build.
  • Largest text size: one problem in the Android bottom bar.
  • Windows installer: not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn the first time.
