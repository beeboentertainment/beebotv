02 / What human testing adds

Check the experience, not just the code.

Automated cases can check a setting, policy or request in a controlled setup. They cannot establish the picture on your TV, the sound from your receiver, the behavior of your phone’s hotspot or whether a screen reader makes a workflow understandable. Human sign-off belongs to a recorded feature, build, device and connection.

A pale-green check applies only to the listed passing checks. Dark green requires a matching real-test record. A feature may also be unavailable, disabled or awaiting activation; its evidence label does not remove those limits.

What “stays the entire time” means

  • Your test period starts when you are accepted.
  • It ends when we announce, on this page and by email, that testing is finished. We expect it to last at least 14 days. We will tell accepted testers the dates in their welcome email.
  • Staying means you remain in the test for the areas you signed up for and can be reached by email, until that announcement. If something comes up, email us before you leave and we will tell you honestly where that leaves you.
  • Android closed-test participants should follow the invitation and the continuous opt-in instructions in the Android testing goal.

What “real feedback” means

  • You actually use the product on your own equipment.
  • You send specific reports: what you did, what you expected, what happened, your device and version. Screenshots are optional.
  • At least one report for each product area you signed up for is a fair minimum.
  • A short “it works” report counts if it is specific (“played a 2-hour MKV on a Samsung QN90 over Wi-Fi with no stutter”).
  • We do not reward volume or praise. One honest report beats ten cheerful ones. We never ask for or reward store ratings or reviews.

The conditions, in plain words

  • Free to join. There is no purchase and no payment is ever asked, to join, to test or to receive the pass.
  • “Lifetime” means for as long as Beebo Entertainment offers that service. If we stop offering a service, the pass for it ends with it.
  • One pass per person. It is for your personal use. It cannot be transferred, given away or sold.
  • The pass has no cash value.
  • Beebo Entertainment decides whether your participation and feedback qualify, judged fairly on the criteria listed on this page.
  • The offer and its terms may be updated. Any change will be posted on this page with the date.
  • At the end of the test we will confirm by email, in writing, whether you qualified and the terms of your pass.

  1. Tell us your areas, equipment, installed versions, whether you have a Beebo server and your time zone.
  2. We review the devices and coverage needed. Applying does not guarantee a place or access to every platform.
  3. If accepted, your welcome email supplies the test dates, exact builds or invitations, access needed and the results to send.
  4. Complete the assigned checks and stay in contact through your test period.

Your information and participation.

Share only what helps

Your application and feedback include the contact and device details you choose to send. They are used to coordinate testing and confirm the existing tester offer. A store invitation may require sharing the relevant account address with that distribution service; follow your invitation.
