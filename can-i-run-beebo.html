Will Beebo work on my internet?

Beebo streams your own films from your own computer, straight to your phone — no middleman. That works on almost every home connection, but not quite all of them. This checks yours in about ten seconds. Nothing to install, nothing saved.

What this can and can't tell you. This page tests how your connection behaves from a browser, which is a very good predictor — but it can't see inside your router. Two things are only knowable once Beebo is installed: whether your router will open a door on its own request, and whether traffic actually arrives. Beebo asks your router to open a door automatically when it starts — and if your router says no, watching away from home still works anyway, because Beebo connects your phone straight to your PC without needing that door at all.

Nothing here is sent anywhere or stored. The check runs entirely in your browser and talks only to public address-lookup servers, the same ones video calls use.
