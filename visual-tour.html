A LITTLE HELP, RIGHT WHERE YOU NEED IT

See it.
Tap it. Settle in.

Follow the numbers to find your way around the Android app. Tap a screenshot to enlarge it. These are existing screenshots from an earlier build; they may show the former MovieAPP name.

FOLLOW THE NUMBERS

Find a movie and open its details

  1. Tap Search titles and enter part of the name.
  2. Choose a genre chip to narrow the list.
  3. Tap the small i button on a poster for its details.

FOLLOW THE NUMBERS

Find a TV show

  1. Tap TV in the bottom navigation.
  2. Use Search shows to find a series.

FOLLOW THE NUMBERS

Find the Cast button

  1. Look for the screen with waves icon in the top bar. Keep your phone and supported TV on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Tap it in your app, then select your TV when the device list appears. The list itself is not shown here.

FOLLOW THE NUMBERS

Save a title, then find your downloads

  1. Tap the down-arrow on a title to start its download.
  2. Open Downloads along the bottom. Wait until downloading finishes before leaving Wi-Fi.

Existing Android screenshot from an earlier build. Labels or layout may differ in your version. Sample library content is not included with Beebo.

FOLLOW THE NUMBERS

Carry on where you left off

  1. Open Continue in the bottom navigation.
  2. Tap the play triangle beside a title to resume.
  3. Use Continue or All history at the top to change the list.

Existing Android screenshot from an earlier build. Labels or layout may differ in your version. Sample library content is not included with Beebo.

FOLLOW THE NUMBERS

Let Beebo pick something to watch

  1. Choose Movies, TV Shows or Both.
  2. Set a category and year, or leave them on Any.
  3. Tap Start surfing.
