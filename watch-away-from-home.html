Watch away from home

Your movies live on the computer at home. Open one web address and your whole library is there, on mobile data, at a friend’s place or in a hotel.

Your address3 stepsFor the ownerQuestions

YOUR FAMILY’S ADDRESS

One address, forever.

Every Beebo gets its own free web address that never changes, like yourname.beebo.tv.

Show moreShow less

The owner sees the exact address on the app’s Get Started screen, under “Watch away from home”, with a QR code. Share it with your household once, and everyone uses it from then on.

STEP 1

Open your address.

On your phone, tablet or laptop, type your yourname.beebo.tv address into any web browser, or scan the QR code from the owner’s Get Started screen.

STEP 2

Sign in with your own name and password.

The ones the owner made for you on the home computer. Never the password of whoever pays for Beebo. Continue Watching stays yours, on every screen you use.

Show moreShow less

Don’t know the web address? Go to login.beebo.tv/find, type the email of whoever pays for Beebo, then your own name and password. Beebo finds the right home for you.

In the phone app it is the same: put that email in the Home box, then your own name and password.

Some houses prefer one shared pass for everybody. That still works: leave the name blank and type the pass. It is deliberately not the owner’s account password, it can’t touch the subscription or the settings, and the owner can change it whenever they like.

Don’t have a login? Ask the owner to add you. See Add & manage people.

Owner? Sign in with your Beebo account, and set a shared pass at yourname.beebo.tv/household if you want one.

STEP 3

Press play.

Your full library loads, just like at home. It streams over the internet, so it uses your data plan when you’re not on Wi-Fi.

Show moreShow less

Install it for one-tap access. Tap Install app or Add to Home Screen so Beebo gets a real icon. Full steps are in Get Beebo on your phone.

One install works everywhere. Once Beebo is on your phone, it connects through your home Wi-Fi when you’re home and switches to your .beebo.tv address when you’re out, automatically.

FOR THE OWNER

It’s usually already on.

Your .beebo.tv address is free and automatic. It comes online a few seconds after Beebo starts, and you’ll see it with its QR code on the Get Started screen.

Show moreShow less

Direct first. Beebo always tries a direct connection, straight from your computer to your phone, encrypted end to end.

Not sure it’ll connect? A few home networks don’t allow the direct route. Run the connection test on your home computer to find out.

If direct isn’t possible, Beebo can try automatic UPnP, or you can use your own Cloudflare relay or open ports on your router — all $0. Beebo Relay is a simple paid fallback if none of those work for you. A relay passes your video along but can’t see it.

Questions & fixes

The local 192.168… address stopped working when I left home

That local address only works on the home Wi-Fi. Away from home, use your yourname.beebo.tv address instead — it’s built for exactly this.

My beebo.tv address won’t load

First, the home computer must be on and running Beebo — it’s your server. Give it a few seconds after Beebo starts. If it still won’t connect, the owner can turn on the temporary remote access backup in Get Started, or restart Beebo on the home PC. To find out whether your home internet lets it through at all, run Will Beebo work on your internet? on the home computer.

Does watching away from home use my data?

Yes — off Wi-Fi it streams over your mobile data, like any streaming app. To avoid data use entirely, download movies to your phone while on Wi-Fi first (available in the Android app), then watch them offline.

Is it safe to open my library to the internet?

Yes. Everyone, including you, must sign in with an approved account to see anything — and your video files never leave your computer; they’re streamed on demand. The owner controls exactly who has a login in the Users tab.

Do I need to forward ports on my router?

Usually not. That’s the point of the .beebo.tv address — on most home connections it connects without any router setup. Some stricter connections need one port opened; this check tells you which kind you have, using your computer and your phone.

Can several of us watch away from home at once?

Yes — different people can stream different things at the same time, each signed into their own account.
