Watch away from home

Your movies live on the computer at home — but you don’t have to. On mobile data, at a friend’s house, in a hotel, on the other side of the world: open one web address and your whole library is right there. No router settings, no tech wizardry.

Your family’s permanent address

🔗 It looks like this

Every Beebo gets its own free web address that never changes:

yourname.beebo.tv

The owner sees the exact address on the app’s 🚀 Get Started screen (under “Watch away from home”), along with a QR code. Share it with your household once, and everyone uses it forever.

Watch away — step by step

  1. Open your address in a browser

    On your phone, tablet, or laptop, type your yourname.beebo.tv address into any web browser — or scan the QR code from the owner’s Get Started screen with your phone’s camera.

  2. Sign in twice — once for the house, once for you

    First the household pass: one shared pass the owner sets for your address. It is deliberately not their account password — it can’t touch the subscription or the settings, and the owner can change it whenever they like. That’s all it does: proves this address belongs to your family.

    Your library then opens and asks for your own username and login code, so Continue Watching and everything else stays yours. Don’t have a login? Ask the owner to add you — see Add & manage people.

    Owner? You can sign in with your Beebo account instead, and set the household pass at yourname.beebo.tv/household.

  3. Press play

    Your full library loads, just like at home — posters, Continue Watching, everything. It streams over the internet, so it uses your data plan when you’re not on Wi-Fi.

  4. Install it for one-tap access

    Tap Install app / Add to Home Screen so Beebo gets a real icon and you skip typing the address next time. Full steps in Get Beebo on your phone.

💡 One install, works everywhere. Once Beebo is on your phone, it connects through your home Wi-Fi when you’re home and switches to your .beebo.tv address when you’re out — automatically. You don’t change a thing.

For the owner: turning it on

✅ It’s usually already on

Your permanent .beebo.tv address is free and automatic — it comes online a few seconds after Beebo starts, and you’ll see it (with its QR code) on the Get Started screen. Nothing to configure.

🔁 Backup: temporary remote access

If the permanent address ever won’t connect on a tricky network, Get Started also offers Turn on remote access — a temporary web address you can type into the app. Handy as a fallback, though it changes each time you restart Beebo.

⚙️ Advanced: your own domain

Prefer an address like watch.yourfamily.com? Get Started has an optional, one-time setup (about 15 minutes, needs a domain name) using a free Cloudflare tunnel. Totally optional — the free .beebo.tv address works great on its own.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

The local 192.168… address stopped working when I left home

That local address only works on the home Wi-Fi. Away from home, use your yourname.beebo.tv address instead — it’s built for exactly this.

My beebo.tv address won’t load

First, the home computer must be on and running Beebo — it’s your server. Give it a few seconds after Beebo starts. If it still won’t connect, the owner can turn on the temporary remote access backup in Get Started, or restart Beebo on the home PC.

Does watching away from home use my data?

Yes — off Wi-Fi it streams over your mobile data, like any streaming app. To avoid data use entirely, download movies to your phone while on Wi-Fi first (available in the Android app), then watch them offline.

Is it safe to open my library to the internet?

Yes. Everyone, including you, must sign in with an approved account to see anything — and your video files never leave your computer; they’re streamed on demand. The owner controls exactly who has a login in the Users tab.

Do I need to forward ports on my router?

No. That’s the point of the .beebo.tv address — it connects without any router setup.

Can several of us watch away from home at once?

Yes — different people can stream different things at the same time, each signed into their own account.
