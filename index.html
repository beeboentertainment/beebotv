YOUR HOME. YOUR COLLECTION. YOUR CHOICE.

A useful little addition
to everyday family life.

Beebo Entertainment is personal home media server software for Windows 10 and 11. It organizes the media files you provide and brings them to your household’s phones, tablets, browsers and supported TVs.

More from what you already own

Bring movies, shows and home videos together in one searchable library. Continue watching across your routine and prepare downloads for time without internet. Beebo supplies the software; you supply the content.

How your library works ↗

Household access you manage

Your source collection stays on your own computer. Create and manage family accounts, choose who can connect, and remove access when needed. Select appropriate content for your household and keep your devices and server updated.

Your account controls ↗

Thoughtful about privacy and use

The app’s published policy describes no in-app advertising or analytics. Account sign-in and remote connections use a coordination service. In the car, video is for passengers; Beebo Auto carries audio through the car speakers.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Visit the help centre ↗
Does Beebo come with movies and shows?

No. Beebo organizes and streams the files you already own. It is personal media server software, with no included streaming catalogue.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Your Windows PC needs to be on when you stream its library. For time away from the internet, prepare downloads or use Campsite Mode with supported local sharing.

Can I use Beebo on an iPhone?

Yes, through Safari. You can add it to your Home Screen. The current native phone app is for Android; a native iPhone app is planned.

Can I watch away from home?

Yes, with your home server online and remote access set up. See the away-from-home guide for the requirements.

Who’s the little rabbit?

That’s Beebo, the black-and-white French Lop who inspired the name. A little personality behind a very personal media library.
