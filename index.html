What Beebo actually is

Beebo Entertainment is private home media server software for Windows 10 and 11. You install it on the computer where your own video files already live. It reads those folders, tidies them into a library with posters and descriptions, and streams them to your family's own phones, tablets and TVs. That is the whole product.

It is the same kind of software as Plex, Jellyfin and Emby. Beebo does not host video, does not index or scrape video from the internet, does not connect to torrent or file-sharing networks, and ships with no catalog of movies or channels. Point it at an empty folder and it shows you nothing, because it has nothing of its own to show.

Is Beebo a streaming site or a piracy app?

No. Beebo only ever plays files already sitting on your own computer — typically home videos, and discs you own and have copied for personal use. It adds no content of its own and reaches out to no video source on the internet.

Is Beebo related to “BeeTV” or similar APKs?

No. Beebo Entertainment is entirely independent and shares no code, developers, servers or history with BeeTV or any other third-party streaming APK. The similarity is the name alone — Beebo is named after our black-and-white French Lop rabbit.

Who can see my library?

Only the people you create accounts for. There is no public directory, no sharing marketplace, and no way for a stranger to browse or search your library. Away from home, video travels peer-to-peer from your PC straight to your device.

Is it safe for children?

A child can only ever see what a parent put on the family computer. No algorithmic feed, no comments, no recommendations from strangers, nowhere to wander off to. No ads and no third-party tracking, ever.
