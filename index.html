WHAT IS BEEBO?

Your movies. Your PC. Every screen.

Beebo turns the movie and show files you already own into a friendly library, and streams it from your Windows PC to your phone, TV and browser.

Show moreShow less

Beebo is personal home media server software for Windows 10 and 11. It doesn’t come with movies or shows. You supply the content; Beebo supplies the software.

Your files stay on your own computer. That PC needs to be on while people stream from it.

Less searching. More watching.

Point Beebo at your folders and it builds a library with posters, cast and descriptions. Continue Watching remembers where each person left off.

Phone, tablet, browser or TV.

AWAY FROM HOME

Your library, anywhere.

Every Beebo gets a free address like yourname.beebo.tv. Open it on mobile data or any Wi-Fi, sign in, and your whole library is there.

Show moreShow less

Direct first. Beebo connects your phone straight to your home PC, encrypted between your phone and your PC. For most homes there’s nothing to set up.

If direct isn’t possible, Beebo can try automatic UPnP, or you can open ports on your router or bring your own relay — all $0, at any resolution. Beebo Relay (Beebo’s own relay) is a simple paid fallback if none of those work for you. A relay passes the video along but can’t see it.

Your home PC must be on and running Beebo.

OFFLINE & CAMPING

No signal? Still movie night.

Download videos to your Android phone before you go. With Campsite Mode, your phone becomes the hub: friends join your hotspot, scan a code and watch in their browser.

Show moreShow less

Campsite Mode needs no internet, and guests need no app and no account.

There are guest games to play together, plus a star chart, a packing list and a scavenger hunt.

Available now — test build (Beta): turn every phone into a speaker. Beebo Campsite Mode can play the same music in sync on many phones at once, and can split it into a left and right stereo pair across two phones. It is in the Android test build 1.39 and has not been tested on real phones yet.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No ads. No data selling.

Your library and your videos stay on your own equipment. The Beebo apps have no ads and no third-party analytics or trackers.

Show moreShow less

MORE THAN MOVIES

Music, photos and playlists.

The same computer that holds your films can look after your songs and your photos, keep playlists filled for you, and let you pick the quality and the subtitles while you watch.

WHY BEEBO

Ten reasons a household might pick Beebo.

Plex, Jellyfin and Emby are established and ahead of us on devices and add-ons. Here is what Beebo offers, with an honest label on each one.

Turn every phone into a speaker

Available now — test build (Beta): Beebo Campsite Mode can play the same music in sync on many phones at once, and can split it into a left and right stereo pair across two phones. Guests join the host phone’s Wi-Fi and open a link in their browser, with no app and no account. It is in the Android test build 1.39, tested on a computer and not yet on real phones. Available now — Beta (Windows 0.1.58, simulated only): phones as surround speakers for movies, while a TV or PC shows the picture. It was tested in simulation and by measurement on one computer, not on real phones. How phone speakers work · Step-by-step guide

Free at home

Available now. The app is free to use at home. Away from home is free too with a direct connection, automatic UPnP or your own relay. Only optional Beebo services, such as Beebo Relay, are paid.

Your library stays on your PC

Available now. Your library and video don’t go to Beebo. Beebo’s servers handle sign-in and introductions. Privacy in plain words.

Made for camp-outs and road trips

Available now: Campsite Mode on Android. Available now — test build (Beta): synced group music on many phones, and Trip Journal private links served from your own PC (Android 1.39 with Windows 0.1.58). Beta — next Android release: License Plate Hunt, Trip Clock, Quiet Hours, Roadside Quiz packs and a Songbook engine.

Works with more apps

Available now — Beta (Windows 0.1.58). An optional, off-by-default mode lets some Jellyfin-compatible apps connect to Beebo. It was checked against the official Jellyfin API description but has not been tried with real Jellyfin apps. App-by-app table.

More than movies

Available now — Beta (Windows 0.1.58): Live TV and DVR with your own HDHomeRun tuner (never tested on a real tuner), audiobooks, podcasts and internet radio, watch together, automatic intro skipping, themes, six languages, Cinema Mode pre-show trailers, Movie Night party games, and a tool to move your watch history from Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Kodi or Letterboxd.

Built for the big screen

Available now: the Android app on Android TV and Google TV, and casting to Chromecast and Google TV. In development: apps for Fire TV, Roku, Samsung, LG, Xbox and Apple TV. Beebo does not claim passthrough of HDR10+, Dolby Vision, Atmos or DTS:X to a receiver, because it is unverified on real equipment. Home theater and big screen.

Uses your graphics card

Available now — Beta (Windows 0.1.58): a rebuilt video-conversion engine that tries your PC’s NVIDIA, Intel or AMD encoder, falls back automatically, limits how many run at once and has an old-PC mode. Only Intel Quick Sync and software conversion have been exercised on real hardware. Linux and Docker downloads are Beta too.

Local-first, offline-minded

Watching at home never needs the internet. Away from home, updates and looking up brand-new titles do. The owner check with the router unplugged is still to be done. Your library and watch history live on your own PC.

Honest about where we are

The server is Windows first. Linux and Docker are Beta, Fire TV, Roku, Samsung, LG, Xbox and Apple apps are in development, and an open-source direction is planned: the source is published for transparency, but no open-source licence has been granted. Ads are off by default: they would only ever switch on if you opt in, and are never required.

Beta means new and still being tested on real equipment. Available now — Beta is in the current download (Windows 0.1.58, Android 1.39); Beta — next Android release is not. For the living room, see Home theater and big screen. Compare in detail: all four side by side, Beebo vs Plex, Beebo vs Jellyfin, Beebo vs Emby.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Visit the help centre ↗
Does Beebo come with movies and shows?

No. Beebo organizes and streams the files you already own. It is personal media server software, with no included streaming catalogue.

Can Campsite Mode play the same music on several phones?

Yes, in the Android test build 1.39 (Beta). Beebo Campsite Mode can play the same music in sync on many phones at once, and can split it into a left and right stereo pair across two phones. It has not been tested on real phones yet, and it does not turn phones into movie surround speakers, which is in development. See Turn every phone into a speaker.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Your Windows PC needs to be on when you stream its library. For time away from the internet, prepare downloads or use Campsite Mode with supported local sharing.

Can I use Beebo on an iPhone?

Yes, through Safari. You can add it to your Home Screen. The current native phone app is for Android; a native iPhone app is in development and not published.

Can I watch away from home?

Yes, with your home server online and remote access set up. See the away-from-home guide for the requirements.

That’s Beebo, the black-and-white French Lop who inspired the name. A little personality behind a very personal media library.
