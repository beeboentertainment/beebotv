★ Watch Together, wherever you are

Every screen in the car plays the same moment, together.

Beebo Entertainment's Watch Together keeps every phone, tablet and laptop in the car in sync — the same movie, the same instant — while the sound comes through the car speakers over Beebo Auto.

In the Beebo app, each viewer gets a customizable audio-sync buffer to nudge their own picture into lip-sync with the speakers — so nothing looks dubbed, even over Bluetooth or headphones.

  • Update from inside the app. Once you’re on v2.1, Beebo tells you when a new version is ready and installs it with one tap — no need to come back here.
