Get started

Set up your own Beebo in four steps

Beebo runs from a computer in your home. Install the server on that PC, point it at your movie folders, then watch on every phone in the house. Google Play is on the way — for now you install directly.

①

Install the server (Windows PC)

On the computer that holds your movies, download and run the installer — it sets up your personal Beebo server.

⬇ Download for Windows

Windows 10/11 · ~225 MB. Windows may warn it’s from an unknown publisher — choose “More info → Run anyway”.

②

Create your account & pick folders

Beebo opens to a Get Started screen: make your username & password, then choose the folders where your movies and shows live. That’s the whole setup. Full guide →

③

Get the phone app

Install Beebo on each phone in the house.

⬇ Download for Android

Android APK · ~17 MB · arm64. Car companion: Beebo Auto.

④

Connect & watch

Open the app and type the server address shown on your Beebo Get Started screen (on your home Wi‑Fi it looks like 192.168.1.50:47811), then sign in. Your whole library is there.
