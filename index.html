WHAT IS BEEBO?

Your movies. Your PC. Every screen.

Beebo turns the movie and show files you already own into a friendly library, and streams it from your Windows PC to your phone, TV and browser.

Show moreShow less

Beebo is personal home media server software for Windows 10 and 11. It doesn’t come with movies or shows. You supply the content; Beebo supplies the software.

Your files stay on your own computer. That PC needs to be on while people stream from it.

YOUR LIBRARY

Less searching. More watching.

Point Beebo at your folders and it builds a library with posters, cast and descriptions. Continue Watching remembers where each person left off.

Show moreShow less
  • Posters and details come from TMDB with your own free key.
  • Can’t decide? Surf picks something for you.
  • Trailers: watch a title’s trailer from the Android app.
  • Request a title: ask for something that isn’t in the library yet, from the Android app.
  • Beebo Inbox: drop new videos into one folder and Beebo sorts them into Movies and TV Shows. Anything it isn’t sure about waits for you to check.

EVERY SCREEN

Phone, tablet, browser or TV.

Use the Android app, or open Beebo in any web browser. Cast to a Chromecast or Google TV and your phone becomes the remote.

Show moreShow less

iPhone and iPad: watch in Safari and add Beebo to your Home Screen. A native iPhone app is planned.

Casting works when your phone and the TV are on the same Wi-Fi.

AWAY FROM HOME

Your library, anywhere.

Every Beebo gets a free address like yourname.beebo.tv. Open it on mobile data or any Wi-Fi, sign in, and your whole library is there.

Show moreShow less

Direct first. Beebo connects your phone straight to your home PC, encrypted end to end. For most homes there’s nothing to set up.

If direct isn’t possible, you can use Beebo Relay (included with your subscription, no extra usage charge), your own Cloudflare relay, or open ports on your router. A relay passes the video along but can’t see it.

Your home PC must be on and running Beebo.

OFFLINE & CAMPING

No signal? Still movie night.

Download videos to your Android phone before you go. With Campsite Mode, your phone becomes the hub: friends join your hotspot, scan a code and watch in their browser.

Show moreShow less

Campsite Mode needs no internet, and guests need no app and no account.

There are guest games to play together, plus a star chart, a packing list and a scavenger hunt.

ROAD TRIPS

Happy passengers.

Watch Together keeps passenger screens in sync, with road-trip games for when the movie ends. Beebo Auto plays your library’s sound through the car speakers.

Show moreShow less

Watch Together works between Beebo apps signed in to the same Beebo account. Everyone opens the same video, then syncs.

Beebo Auto is audio only. Car video is for passengers or when parked.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No ads. No data selling.

Your library and your videos stay on your own equipment. The Beebo apps have no ads and no third-party analytics or trackers.

Show moreShow less

Sign-in and connection set-up use a small Beebo service that sees your account email, never your library.

With a direct connection your video never passes through Beebo’s servers. If a relay is used, your video stays encrypted end to end.

MORE THAN MOVIES

Music, photos and playlists.

The same computer that holds your films can look after your songs and your photos, keep playlists filled for you, and let you pick the quality and the subtitles while you watch.

Show moreShow less
  • Music: your albums, with covers and lyrics from your own files, a queue, shuffle and repeat.
  • Photos: your photo folders on every screen, and your phone’s camera copied home on Wi-Fi.
  • Playlists: pick titles by hand, or set rules once and let Beebo fill the list.
  • Quality & subtitles: change the picture size, the soundtrack and the subtitles as you watch.
  • Your household: age limits, bedtimes and a PIN, or share your library with one friend in another home.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Visit the help centre ↗
Does Beebo come with movies and shows?

No. Beebo organizes and streams the files you already own. It is personal media server software, with no included streaming catalogue.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

Your Windows PC needs to be on when you stream its library. For time away from the internet, prepare downloads or use Campsite Mode with supported local sharing.

Can I use Beebo on an iPhone?

Yes, through Safari. You can add it to your Home Screen. The current native phone app is for Android; a native iPhone app is planned.

Can I watch away from home?

Yes, with your home server online and remote access set up. See the away-from-home guide for the requirements.
