Can I watch my videos away from home on mobile data?

Yes. Open your own yourname.beebo.tv address to stream your videos from your home PC to your phone on mobile data, without downloading them first. Beebo tries a peer-to-peer connection first, with Beebo Relay as the fallback. Away viewing and Beebo Relay are Beta. Keep the PC on with Beebo running; both ends need internet, and network restrictions and home upload speed affect playback.

Do I need Tailscale, a VPN or port forwarding?

No. Beebo has built-in remote streaming through your own yourname.beebo.tv address. You do not need Tailscale, an extra VPN app or manual port forwarding. Beebo Relay is the Beta fallback when a direct connection is not possible. Some networks can still prevent a connection; try your setup before relying on it away from home.

Do my videos go to Beebo's servers?

No. Your video library stays on your PC. Video streams peer to peer when possible; Beebo Relay may forward the encrypted stream as a fallback. video is end-to-end encrypted and the relay cannot see it; connection records, including IP addresses, are kept 7 days for abuse handling; nothing is sold.

Dated test, October 4, 2026: a Galaxy S9 played a film through Beebo Relay while connected to another phone’s mobile-data hotspot. This checks that one setup on that date; it does not establish current-build acceptance or success on every network. Read the test record and limits.
