A SIMPLE FIRST DAY

From download to movie night
in seven clear steps.

Our planned default: one month of Tier 1 Relay, free. Start without router setup, then move automatically to a smaller free relay allowance. During your first month, explore your own free connection options for the quality your equipment and internet can support. Launch plan & availability

  1. 01

    COMPUTER

    Download Beebo.

    Get the Windows desktop app on the computer that holds your movies and TV shows.

    Get the desktop app ↓
  2. 02

    COMPUTER

    Install and open it.

    Run the installer, open Beebo, and follow the welcome screens.

  3. 03

    YOUR ACCOUNT

    Create your sign-in.

    Choose your email, username and password. Keep the password somewhere safe—you will use this sign-in on your phone.

  4. 04

    YOUR LIBRARY

    Choose your folders.

    Point Beebo to the folders that contain your movies and TV shows. Beebo builds your personal library from the files you choose.

    Add posters & info (optional) ↗
  5. 05

    PLANNED LAUNCH INTRODUCTION

    Let Beebo connect you.

    The launch plan includes one month of Tier 1 Relay by default for eligible new accounts. Try your own connection options during the month. No paid renewal is planned unless you choose it.

    Introduction & free fallback ↗
  6. 06

    PHONE OR TABLET

    Download the phone app.

    Install the Beebo app on your phone or tablet, then sign in with the account you just created.

    Get the phone app ↓
  7. 07

    WATCH

    Choose a title and press play.

    Open your library in the phone app, pick a movie or episode, and start watching. That is it.

AFTER THE INTRODUCTORY MONTH

Keep watching on the route that suits you.

Our plan is to move you automatically to a basic free Beebo Relay allowance after the month, unless you choose a paid plan.
