★ Watch Together, wherever you are

Every screen in the car plays the same moment, together.

Beebo’s Watch Together keeps every phone, tablet and laptop in the car in sync — the same movie, the same instant — while the sound comes through the car speakers over Beebo Auto.

In the Beebo app, each viewer gets a customizable audio-sync buffer to nudge their own picture into lip-sync with the speakers — so nothing looks dubbed, even over Bluetooth or headphones.

🚗

Safety first: the driver’s screen never shows video. Real video on the car’s own display is handled by Beebo Mirror, a separate partner project. Beebo’s own Watch Together keeps every passenger screen in sync, with sound through Beebo Auto.
