The short setup guide →

  1. Make a home for it.

    Install Beebo on a Windows 10 or 11 PC. This is the computer that holds your library.

  2. Add your collection.

    Choose the folders containing your own media and let Beebo organise them.

  3. Find your seat.

    Connect your Android app or browser. Keep the home PC on while streaming from it.
