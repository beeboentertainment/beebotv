Delete your Beebo account

This permanently removes your Beebo Entertainment account and everything tied to it. This cannot be undone.

  • Your account and sign-in are erased
  • Any active subscription is cancelled — you won't be billed again
  • Your remote-access address and its records are removed
  • Your trial, licence, referral and usage records are deleted

The movies and files on your own computer are never touched — Beebo never stores those. You can also do this from inside the Beebo app under Settings.
