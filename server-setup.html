Set up your Beebo server

Beebo turns one computer in your home into your family’s streaming server. Here’s the whole first-time setup — about ten minutes, no tech knowledge needed.

Before you start

What you need

A Windows 10/11 computer that stays on when you want to watch, the folders where your movies and shows already live, and each phone that will watch. For the first setup, keep the computer and phones on the same Wi‑Fi.

1 · Install the server on your PC

Download & run

On the computer that holds your movies, download the installer and run it:

⬇ Download for Windows

Windows may show a blue “Windows protected your PC” box because the app isn’t signed yet — click More info, then Run anyway. When it finishes, Beebo Entertainment opens on its own.

2 · Create your account

Pick a username & password

The first time Beebo opens, it shows a Get Started screen. Step 1 is “Create your owner account” — choose a username and password. This is what you’ll sign in with on your phone, so keep it handy.

3 · Choose your video folders

Point Beebo at your movies

Step 2 on that screen is “Choose your video folders.” Click Choose Movies folder and pick the folder your films are in; do the same for TV shows if you have them. Beebo scans them and builds your library automatically — it never moves or changes your files.

4 · Get the phone app

Install Beebo on each phone

On every phone in the household that'll watch, download and install the app. Guests never need it — you show them a QR code and Beebo opens in their browser:

⬇ Download for Android

Beebo app v1.7 (build 8) · test build Fri, Sep 11, 2026 at 1:28am Eastern

Your browser may ask permission to install apps — allow it, then tap the downloaded file to install.

5 · Connect the phone to your server

Type the address, then sign in

Step 3 of the computer’s Get Started screen shows a server address — a few numbers like 192.168.1.50:47811. Open the Beebo app on the phone, type that address where it asks, then sign in with the username and password you made. Your whole library appears. (Make sure the phone is on the same Wi‑Fi as the computer.)

Watching away from home (optional)

Later, from anywhere

Everything above works on your home Wi‑Fi with no extra setup. To reach your library from cellular or a friend’s house, your free yourname.beebo.tv address switches itself on a few seconds after Beebo starts — you’ll find it, with a QR code, on the Get Started screen. To choose a different name, open Settings → Your Beebo address. The Get Started screen also shows it as a QR code you can scan.

Open it in any browser — then “install” it

Away from home, just open yourname.beebo.tv in a browser on any phone, tablet or laptop and sign in — your whole library is there. In Chrome tap Install app, or on an iPhone in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and Beebo gets its own app icon that opens straight to your library. Works on iPhone and iPad too.
