Turn on BeeboBook stories

BeeboBook is Beebo’s bonus bedtime feature: kids can write their own branching storybooks, dreamed up by a private AI that runs free, right on your home computer. Nothing is ever sent to the internet. The read-along books work out of the box — this guide switches on the “write your own story” button.

Already works, no setup: the built-in read-along storybooks are ready the moment you install Beebo. You only need the steps below if you want kids to create brand-new stories of their own.

Why the extra step? Writing new stories uses a small AI “brain” called a language model. Beebo keeps it 100% on your own PC — free, no API key, no subscription, and nothing leaves your computer. To do that, you add a free helper app called Ollama. It’s a one-time setup of a few minutes.

Switch it on — step by step

Do this once, on the computer that runs Beebo.

  1. Install the free Ollama app

    Download it from ollama.com/download and run the Windows installer (about a minute). It runs quietly in the background — you’ll see its little icon near the clock.

  2. Add one small story-writing model

    Open Command Prompt (press Start, type cmd, press Enter) and run:

    ollama pull llama3.2

    This downloads the story “brain” — about 2 GB, one time. (A llama3, qwen, mistral, or phi model works too, if you prefer.)

  3. That’s it — open BeeboBook

    Beebo finds Ollama on its own. The “write your own story” button turns on the next time you open BeeboBook. Hand it to the kids and let them build a tale starring themselves.

🌙 Family-safe and private by design. The story AI runs entirely on your computer, so stories can be made even with no internet, and nothing your children write ever goes online.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

The “write your own story” button still isn’t showing

Make sure Ollama is actually running — look for its icon near the clock (bottom-right of the Windows taskbar). If it’s not there, open Ollama from the Start menu, then reopen BeeboBook. Also confirm the ollama pull llama3.2 download finished without errors.

Is this free? Do I need an internet key or subscription?

Completely free. There’s no API key and no subscription — unlike the posters feature, the story writer needs nothing from the internet at all. You only download Ollama and one model once.

The ollama pull command says it can’t find “ollama”

Close and reopen Command Prompt after installing Ollama (it needs a fresh window to notice the new app). If it still doesn’t work, restart the computer once, then try the command again.

How much space and power does it need?

The llama3.2 model is about 2 GB on disk. It runs comfortably on an ordinary modern PC; a story takes a few moments to write. If it feels slow, that’s just the computer thinking — no internet is involved.

Can I use a different model?

Yes — Beebo works with other small models too. Just ollama pull a llama3, qwen, mistral, or phi model instead, and Beebo will find it.

Where do I read more about Ollama?

Visit ollama.com. Beebo also explains all of this in Settings → Story writer (BeeboBook AI).
