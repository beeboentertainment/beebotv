Snakes and Ladders

Roll the die, climb the ladders, mind the snakes. First one home wins. Play against Hopping Beebo or pass the screen around with friends.

Gold ladders go up. Coral snakes slide you down. A six rolls again, up to three rolls in a turn.

Session score

    Scores are kept only while this page stays open. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

    How to play
    1. Everyone starts off the board. Take turns rolling the die and moving that many squares.
    2. Land on the foot of a ladder and you climb to its top. Land on a snake's head and you slide down to its tail.
    3. Roll a six and you go again, but never more than three rolls in one turn.
    4. You do not need an exact roll to finish. Reach or pass square 100 and you win.

    With Hopping Beebo in the game it rolls for itself. The die is the only thing that decides a move, so there is no difficulty setting. Press the Roll button with Enter or Space.
