Tic-Tac-Toe

Get three in a row. Play Hopping Beebo, or pass the screen to a friend.

Scores last until you close this page.

How to play
  • Players take turns placing X or O on an empty square. X goes first.
  • Get three of your marks in a row - across, down or diagonally - to win.
  • If all nine squares fill up with no line, it is a draw.
  • On Hard, Hopping Beebo plays perfectly, so the best you can do is draw.

Keyboard: Tab to the board, use the arrow keys to move between squares and press Enter or Space to place your mark.
