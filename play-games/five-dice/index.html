How to play
  1. Roll five dice. After the first roll, select any dice to keep, then roll again. You may roll up to three times or score after any roll.
  2. Choose one open category each turn. A zero is allowed if the roll does not fit. The upper six boxes earn a 35-point bonus when they total at least 63.
  3. After the Five-of-a-kind box is filled, another five of a kind must go in its matching upper box if that is open. Otherwise it may go in any open box; Full house and straights get their fixed values. Another five of a kind earns 100 bonus points only if the original box holds 50.
  4. After all thirteen boxes are filled, the highest total wins. A solo game shows your final score.

With friends, pass the screen after scoring. Controls support touch, mouse, keyboard and TV remote focus. The game stores nothing and sends nothing to a server.
