Reversi

Trap your opponent's discs between yours and flip them. Play Hopping Beebo, or pass the screen to a friend.

Scores last until you close this page.

How to play
  • Dark goes first. Place a disc so that it traps one or more of your opponent's discs in a straight line (across, down or diagonally) between the new disc and another of yours. Those discs flip to your colour.
  • You can only place a disc where it flips at least one. Legal squares show a small dot.
  • If you have no legal move, your turn is skipped automatically.
  • When neither player can move, the player with more discs wins. Corners are very strong.

Keyboard: Tab to the board, use the arrow keys to move between squares and press Enter or Space to place a disc on a square marked with a dot.
