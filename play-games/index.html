Your games stay on your device

Games here run entirely in your browser, on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and there are no accounts. If a game remembers your score, it only does so for this browser session.

What the full app adds

In the Beebo Android app and the Beebo desktop app, Campsite Mode lets guests join on their own phones over local Wi-Fi, from a browser, with no app needed. It comes with 40+ games.

  • Android app: Test download, not on Google Play
  • Campsite Mode: Beta
