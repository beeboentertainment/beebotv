Dominoes

The double-six draw game. Match either open end, draw when you cannot play, and try to be first out.

How to play
  • A double-six set has 28 tiles. Two players receive seven each; three or four receive five each. The rest form the boneyard.
  • The player with the highest double starts; if nobody has one, the heaviest tile starts. They can lead with any tile in their hand.
  • Match a number at either open end. If a tile fits both ends, choose where it goes. If nothing fits, draw one tile at a time until you can play or the boneyard is empty. Passes then happen automatically.
  • First to use every tile wins and scores the pips left in other hands. If everyone is stuck, the fewest pips wins; equal lowest hands draw.
  • With friends on one screen, each new turn hides the hand until that player accepts the screen. Do not peek while passing the device.

All controls are keyboard focusable and labelled for screen readers. This game stores nothing and sends nothing to a server.
