Mancala

Sow stones, earn extra turns and captures, and finish with the most in your store.

Pass the screen

Hopping Beebo: 0
You: 0

Choose a numbered pit on your side. The stones move counter-clockwise; the far store is skipped.

How to play
  1. Each player has six pits with four stones apiece. Choose a non-empty pit on your own side.
  2. Drop one stone into each following pit, including your store but skipping the other player's store.
  3. If your last stone lands in your store, go again. If it lands in your empty pit and the opposite pit has stones, move those stones and your last stone into your store.
  4. When either side is empty, the other side sweeps its remaining stones into its store. The larger store wins; equal stores draw.

With a friend, pass the screen at each new turn. Controls support touch, mouse, keyboard and TV remote focus. The game stores nothing and sends nothing to a server.
