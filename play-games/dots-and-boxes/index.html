Dots and Boxes

Draw a line between two dots. Close the fourth side of a box and it is yours, and you go again. Most boxes wins.

Session score

    Scores are kept only while this page stays open. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

    How to play
    1. Take turns drawing one line between two neighbouring dots. Press or tap the gap between two dots.
    2. When your line closes the fourth side of a box, the box is yours, shown with your number. You must then draw another line.
    3. When every box is taken, the player with the most boxes wins.

    Tip: try not to draw the third side of a box, because that lets the next player take it. Hopping Beebo on Easy often misses things; on Medium it takes every free box and avoids giving boxes away; on Hard it also counts chains and sometimes declines the last two boxes of a chain to keep control. With the keyboard, use the arrow keys to move between lines and Enter or Space to draw.
