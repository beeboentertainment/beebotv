Checkers

Jump your opponent's pieces and reach the far side for a king. Play Hopping Beebo, or pass the screen to a friend.

Scores last until you close this page.

How to play
  • Gold starts at the bottom and moves first. Pieces move one square diagonally forward onto an empty dark square.
  • Jump over an opponent's piece to capture it. Jumping is compulsory: if you can jump, you must. You keep jumping with the same piece while more jumps are available.
  • Reach the far row to be crowned a king. Kings move and jump one square in all four diagonal directions. Crowning ends your turn.
  • You win when your opponent has no legal move. Forty moves in a row with no capture or crowning is a draw.

Keyboard: Tab to the board, use the arrow keys to move between squares, press Enter or Space to select a piece and again on a highlighted square to move. Escape cancels a selection.
