How to play
  • A standard deck is used. Two players get seven cards each; three to six get five. The first top card is never an eight.
  • Play a card matching the top card's rank or the active suit. An eight is wild; choose a new suit when you play it. There are no skip, reverse or draw-two powers.
  • If nothing matches, draw exactly one card. If it plays, play it now. Otherwise the turn moves on automatically. When the stock is empty, the discard pile except its top card is shuffled back in.
  • If nobody can play and the stock is empty, the fewest cards left wins; equal lowest hands draw.
  • With friends on one screen, every new turn hides the hand until the next player accepts the screen. Do not peek while passing the device.

This game stores nothing and sends nothing to a server. Controls support touch, mouse, keyboard and TV remote focus.
