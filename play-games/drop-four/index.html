Drop Four

Drop discs and line up four. Play Hopping Beebo, or pass the screen to a friend.

Scores last until you close this page.

How to play
  • Gold goes first. Players take turns dropping a disc into one of the seven columns; it falls to the lowest free space.
  • Be the first to line up four of your discs - across, down or diagonally.
  • If the board fills up with no four in a row, it is a draw.
  • Watch for three in a row: block it or your opponent wins.

Keyboard: Tab to the board, use the left and right arrow keys to pick a column and press Enter or Space to drop a disc. You can also press the number keys 1 to 7.
