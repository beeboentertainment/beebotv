Go Fish

Ask another player for a rank you hold. Collect all four suits of a rank to put a book on the table. The most books wins.

At the table

Pass the screen

Your hand

Only you should see these cards. Pick another player and a rank that is in your hand.

How to play
  • A standard 52-card deck is used. Two or three players receive seven cards each; four to six receive five.
  • Ask a player with cards for a rank already in your own hand. If they have any, you take all of that rank and ask again. If not, you fish one card. Drawing the rank you asked for lets you go again; otherwise the turn passes.
  • Four of a rank make a book and leave your hand. Empty hands draw one replacement if the stock still has cards. When all thirteen books are made, most books wins; equal top scores draw.
  • With friends on one screen, every new turn hides the hand until the next player accepts the screen. Do not peek while passing the device.

This game stores nothing and sends nothing to a server. Controls support touch, mouse, keyboard and TV remote focus.
