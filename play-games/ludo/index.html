Ludo

Roll a six to leave the yard, race four tokens around the board and bring every one home.

Your tokens

The circular track shows the shared 52 spaces. Safe starting spaces have a gold outline. Use the token buttons below to move; they show exact progress.

How to play
  1. Roll a six to bring a token from your yard to your safe starting space. A six lets you roll again; a third six in the same turn forfeits that turn.
  2. Choose a movable token after each roll. Land on an opponent's token outside a safe starting space to send it back to their yard. Your own tokens can share a space; they do not block.
  3. Reach home with an exact roll. The first player with all four tokens home wins. If the game reaches 240 completed turns, the player with the most total token progress wins; equal progress draws.

With friends, pass the screen between turns. Controls support touch, mouse, keyboard and TV remote focus. The game stores nothing and sends nothing to a server.
