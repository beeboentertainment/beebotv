Memory Match

Turn two cards at a time and find the matching pairs. Play on your own, against Hopping Beebo, or pass the screen around.

Session score

    Scores are kept only while this page stays open. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

    How to play
    1. All the cards start face down. On your turn, turn over two cards, one at a time.
    2. If they match, you keep the pair and go again. If not, they are turned back and the next player has a go.
    3. When all the pairs are found, the player with the most pairs wins. In solo mode the aim is to finish in as few moves as you can.

    Each card has a different shape and colour, and the shape is also named for screen readers. Hopping Beebo only remembers cards it has seen turned over, and only a few of them: Easy forgets quickly, Hard remembers a lot. Use the arrow keys to move over the cards and Enter or Space to turn one.

    Part of Beebo Entertainment's Campsite games. Beebo is free to use; the full app adds guests on their own phones.
