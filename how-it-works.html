How it works

Your PC is the
home of it all.

Beebo runs on the computer you own. Your player connects to that computer, so the collection stays under your control.

Your files have a home.

The Windows app is both a media server and a desktop player. A server is simply the computer that holds your library and sends it to the screen you’re watching.

Choose folders containing your own media, then connect from an Android phone or a browser. Start on the same Wi-Fi to make setup simpler.

For live streaming, leave the home PC on with Beebo running. Files deliberately downloaded to another device can be used separately.

Away from home

A connection back
to your collection.

No manual port forwarding. The route depends on the networks your devices are using.

01 / FIND EACH OTHER

A little coordination.

Beebo’s servers handle small text such as sign-in and connection signalling to help your player find your home PC.

02 / CONNECT DIRECTLY

Device to device.

Video streams peer-to-peer from your home PC whenever a direct connection can be made. Your home upload speed matters.

03 / A FALLBACK ROUTE

Relay, when needed.

Beebo Relay can carry the encrypted stream if a direct connection isn’t possible. It is Beta and not available to buy.

Some networks still prevent a connection. Try your setup before relying on it while away. See the recorded tests and limits.

Privacy, in plain words

video is end-to-end encrypted and the relay cannot see it; connection records (including IP addresses) are kept 7 days for abuse handling; nothing is sold.

A different kind of offline

A trip without
the connection.

Away streaming needs internet at both ends. Offline use is different: prepare files and activities on your phone before you leave.

Campsite Mode includes solo games and stories that can run without Wi-Fi. Shared activities need nearby devices on the same local Wi-Fi or hotspot, even if that network has no internet.

Offline storage, downloads and real trips have their own testing limits. Check what you need on your device before setting off.
