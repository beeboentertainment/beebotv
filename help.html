Help & frequently asked questions

A little helping hand.

Start here. A few simple steps and the answers people need most.

From your PC
to your player.

Open downloads →

  1. Install on your PC.

    Get the Windows installer from the download page. Open Beebo and follow the app’s setup to create your owner account.

  2. Choose your folders.

    Add the folders containing your own movies, shows or music. Give Beebo time to organise your collection.

  3. Connect a player.

    Install the Android APK or open your Beebo in a browser. Connect on the same Wi-Fi first, using the details in your own app.

Keep the home PC awake. It needs to be on with Beebo running while other devices stream from it.

The short answers.

Need the detail behind a feature? Check the evidence and open issues.

Does the home PC have to stay on?

Yes, while you stream files from it. Leave Beebo running and make sure Windows is not sleeping. Media deliberately downloaded to another device is separate from live streaming.

Does Beebo come with movies or shows?

No. Beebo organises and plays the media you bring. It does not include a movie or television catalogue.

Is Beebo free?

The server and apps are free. Optional paid extras are not for sale yet. You do not need to choose a paid plan to download the apps.

Why can’t I find it on Google Play?

The Android app is a sideload download from this website. Google Play is In development. See the Android download and its current testing limits.

My phone cannot find the home PC. Where do I start?

Make sure Beebo is running on the PC and both devices are connected to the same local network. A guest Wi-Fi network can keep devices apart. Use the connection details shown by your own Beebo app. Keep those details private.

Can I watch away from home?

Beebo can connect your player back to the home PC without manual port forwarding. The PC must stay on, and both ends need internet. Network restrictions and home upload speed affect the result. Read how the connection works.

What should I prepare before an offline trip?

Open and check the activities and files you need before leaving. Solo Campsite activities can work without Wi-Fi; a shared session needs a local Wi-Fi connection or hotspot. Offline download and trip testing is still incomplete.

Can I install it on a TV, Mac, Linux PC or NAS?

Windows, Android phone and browser are Available. Android TV and Link a TV are Beta. Linux and Docker/NAS are Beta with no download offered here. Other native platforms are In development. See the complete platform list.

What does “Available” mean?

It means the product is offered now. It does not mean every device, feature or real-world situation has been checked. The testing page keeps those distinctions visible.

How do I report a problem?

Note the app version, device model, what you tried, what you expected and what happened. If you share a screenshot, remove account details, network addresses and connection codes. Bring that description to the Beebo community on Discord.

How do I remove my account?

The delete-account page explains which kind of account you have, what is removed and what is kept. Read it before making any change.

Something not quite right?

Your version, device and a clear description help.
