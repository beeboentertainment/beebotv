October 14 builds: Windows 0.1.88 (October 14, 2026) and Android 1.51-tester23 (build 73, October 14, 2026).

Both were published then and are no longer the current downloads (see above). The Windows installer is digitally signed (publisher: Nicholas Will). Windows SmartScreen may still show a blue warning until the publisher builds reputation (choose More info, then Run anyway). The Android app is a test download from this site, not on Google Play. In-development tester builds for Fire TV, Roku and LG TVs are also on the site, with install guides for Fire TV, Roku and LG; none has been tested on a real TV yet.

Real-device checks from October 5 (on tester11, an older build): a Galaxy S9+ passed 10 of 11 checks (1 not run) and a Galaxy S24 Ultra passed 11 of 11. Casting to a Chromecast TV from both phones started first try in about 8 seconds (21 of 22 cast and phone checks passed).
